# Answer key: paywall-meditation (designers must not read this)
Strong answers:
- Read the rising refunds as a sign of surprise charges or unclear terms, not as "conversion is too high". Fix transparency first: trial-end date, reminder before charge, price billed, how to cancel.
- Optimize revenue per install net of refunds (or 12-month LTV), not trial start rate. Guardrails: refunds, chargebacks, 1-star reviews, D30 retention.
- Show value before the paywall (a first session or a personalized plan that is actually used), and consider monthly + annual with honest per-month framing.
- Offer a trial timeline (Today, Day 5 reminder, Day 7 charge). At most one skippable save offer at cancel.
- Sizing that is realistic for 2M MAU, and a pre-registered decision rule.
Penalize: fake urgency, hidden close buttons, pre-selected most expensive plan without clarity, "free" framing that hides the charge, optimizing trial starts alone, cancellation friction.
