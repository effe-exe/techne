# Mode: redesign — full redesigns and new products (start big, then compress)

Use for: new products, new major features, complete redesigns, rebrands of a product UI.

## Phase 1 — Big picture (diverge)
1. **Frame it:** problem, users, JTBD, business model, success metrics (North Star + inputs), constraints, and what the existing data/research says. For a redesign, ask *why rebuild instead of iterate*. Rewrites throw away hidden knowledge (Spolsky), so list what the current product does well and must keep.
2. **Learn from the best:** pull 8–15 references from the taste library and beyond (→ `modes/inspire.md`, `references/inspiration/taste-model.md`), plus the relevant master lenses and conversion patterns.
3. **Generate 3 distinct concepts** from different angles (e.g. simplest possible / power-user / emotional-brand-led). For each: core idea in one line, key screens, the bet it makes, the risk.
4. **Judge** them against the goal, the evidence, feasibility and ethics. Pick one, and graft the best ideas from the others.

## Phase 2 — Compress (converge)
5. **Flows** (→ `modes/flow.md`) and **IA**.
6. **Visual direction** (→ `modes/visual.md`) → tokens → components (→ `modes/system.md`).
7. **Key screens at high fidelity** in Figma or a code prototype (→ `modes/figma.md` / `modes/prototype.md`), with all states.
8. **De-risk the launch:** usability test plan, staged rollout with holdback, metrics and guardrails, rollback criteria (→ `modes/experiment.md`).

## Output
Brief → 3 concepts → chosen direction with rationale → flows → visual system → key screens → validation & rollout plan. Show decisions and the reasons behind them, not just artifacts.
