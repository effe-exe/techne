## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- If the hero background moves, give it a visible pause button: Retool's autoplaying film sits behind the prompt box with a persistent pause control bottom-left.

## Why it works
- Light-weight display type: saansFont 72px at weight 300 (-1.6px tracking) in warm off-white rgb(233,235,223) on rgb(21,21,21) — thin type on dark reads premium rather than shouty.
- Hero is an interactive prompt box pre-filled with a concrete example ('Build a simple KPI dashboard that pulls revenue and churn metrics from @Snowflake…') plus 'Starter prompts', 'Import code' and 'Build with AI agents' options — the input shows what the product can do.
- Visible pause control on the background video and a separate 'Watch the film' pill; reduced-motion CSS present; transitions use custom curves cubic-bezier(0.72,0,0.12,1).
- Secondary typeface pxGroteskFont at 12px for buttons/labels gives a technical texture without a full monospace.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any landing page with background video or animated hero; AI/builder tools demonstrating capability through an example input.
- Doesn't: The prompt-box hero is now a commodity pattern across AI products — differentiation must come from the example content, not the box.
