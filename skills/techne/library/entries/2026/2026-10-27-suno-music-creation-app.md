## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Offer two depths in one input: a plain 'Chat to make music' prompt for novices, an 'Advanced' toggle for pros and a dice button for the stuck — one field serves three user intents without branching the page.

## Why it works
- Centred 72px Neue Montreal headline at 500 (-0.96px, line-height 1.0) — 'Make any song you can imagine' — over a grainy amber-magenta gradient that reads like stage light rather than tech glow.
- The input bar is the CTA: '+' attach, 'Advanced' sliders toggle, a dice (randomise) button and a gradient 'Create' pill; the subhead names both paths ('simple prompt or … pro editing tools').
- Tilted, blurred track cards with play buttons ('Porch Light On', 'Dancing With My Eyes Closed') flank the hero — real outputs with creator names, playable; press logos (Forbes, Rolling Stone, Variety, WIRED) close the fold.
- Dark rgb(16,16,18) ground with warm off-white rgb(247,244,239) text, translucent oklab panels, pill buttons; 0.15s standard ease, reduced-motion CSS present; Editorial New serif reserved for accents.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creative generators, any tool with both casual and expert users, search/configurators with an 'advanced' mode.
- Doesn't: tasks where randomness is unwelcome (finance, health); the atmospheric gradient needs care for text contrast.
