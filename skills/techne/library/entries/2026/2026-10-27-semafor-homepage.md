## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Signal a global outlook in the masthead itself (six city clocks around the wordmark) and open with a numbered 'World at a Glance' briefing that links key phrases inline.

## Why it works
- Masthead flanks SEMAFOR with analogue clock dials for D.C., BXL, Lagos, Riyadh, Beijing and SG, plus the local time/date at left.
- Left rail 'The World at a Glance' is a numbered list (1–5) with the linked phrase underlined and bold inside each sentence and an 'Updated 10:40pm UTC' timestamp with a red dot.
- Paper-tinted ground rgb(248,245,215) with dotted hairline rules; FeatureFlatHeadline (124 uses) 34px/370 centred lead headline over a 20px grey dek.
- 'View / …' opinion cards are visually separated as tinted tiles rgb(236,233,210).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: briefings, dashboards and newsletters where scannable summaries lead to depth.
- Doesn't: a ~250px empty ad slot preceded the masthead at capture.
