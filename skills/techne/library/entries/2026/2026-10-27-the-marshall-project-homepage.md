## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Open with a three-up triptych of equal stories — serif headlines over darkened photos with red mono series labels — so the homepage shows breadth, not a single lead.

## Why it works
- Three equal 480px panels, each with a series label in red mono caps (THE FRAME, CLOSING ARGUMENT, LIFE INSIDE) and a large white serif headline over a darkened photo.
- Header: red menu block, barcode-like logo mark, monospace tagline 'Nonprofit journalism about criminal justice', red DONATE.
- Below, list items use red mono category + grey mono date ('NEWS AND AWARDS October 26') above a serif headline and dek.
- Pressura (mono/grotesk hybrid) for meta on warm off-white rgb(246,246,244).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: orgs with recurring series or columns.
- Doesn't: red mono labels on mid-tone photos were low-contrast at capture.
