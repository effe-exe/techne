## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Pair each typeface with a photograph that embodies its personality, so the specimen becomes a mood, and put 'Try for Free' right on it.

## Why it works
- Each font is a 20px-radius card with a full-bleed photo and the family name at 120–169px in white: 'Neue Gstaad' over Swiss alpine meadows with 'Calm. Efficient. Swiss.' and a red (#FF2F00) 'New' pill.
- Two pill buttons per card, solid 'Explore font' and outline 'Try for Free': the free trial is equal in weight to browsing, which suits the foundry's free-to-try licensing.
- Plain utility chrome: Neue Montreal 14px nav (All fonts, Font starter pack, Font in use, Academy, Support), search, bag and menu icons; 196 of the text nodes are one family.
- Respects prefers-reduced-motion. Easing cubic-bezier(0.22,1,0.36,1) at 0.5s; radii 20px cards and 999px pills.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any catalogue of products with personality (fonts, fragrances, templates, music packs) where mood sells better than specs.
- Doesn't: Utilitarian catalogues where comparison matters more than mood.
