## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Package an AI capability as an object people already know how to use — here a podcast-style audio player with scrubber, timestamps and thumbs up/down — so the novel thing needs no explanation and feedback is built into the artefact.

## Why it works
- Audience-specific headline in Google Sans 76/500 (-0.85px): 'Turn your assignments into Audio Overviews', the feature name in Google blue rgb(49,134,255) — names the job (students) and the output in one line.
- The hero visual is a tilted, oversized player pill (pause button, 1:44 / 3:01, blue progress) with thumbs up/down, floating over sine-wave lines and blurred source-document chips — inputs become audio.
- Single dark 'Try Gemini Notebook' pill (rgb(31,31,31), 94px radius) under the headline; generous 24-55px rounded panels on a pale rgb(243,247,251) card.
- Reduced-motion CSS present; transitions 0.25-0.5s ease-in-out. Cookie bar is notice-only ('OK, got it' + Learn more) — no optional-decline control on the fold.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: AI features that transform content into a new format (summaries, briefings, flashcards, videos); anywhere a familiar container lowers the learning curve.
- Doesn't: outputs that need verification more than consumption — a podcast format can hide citations; pair with source links.
