## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Run all meta (kickers, bylines, section labels) in a house monospace with highlighter-block labels, and save a condensed display face for headlines only.

## Why it works
- WIRED Mono (102 uses) at 11–13px uppercase for nav, kickers ('NEED TO KNOW', 'FOLD FIX'), bylines ('BY DUNCAN FYFE').
- Section labels 'TODAY'S PICKS' / 'TRENDING STORIES' sit in violet highlight blocks on a violet rule (rgb(147,32,184)).
- Headlines in a tall condensed display face (~58px lead) with a centred serif dek; nav links in the same violet as labels.
- Audio-available headphone badges on thumbnails; 4px radii (34 uses).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: tech and culture media, developer blogs, changelogs.
- Doesn't: an empty ~260px ad slot sat between nav and content at capture.
