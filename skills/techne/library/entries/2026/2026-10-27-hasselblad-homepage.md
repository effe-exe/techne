## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Wide letter-spacing on a few words of caps (≈0.4em) plus one gradient light source is enough to make a black page feel premium.

## Why it works
- Pure black page; centred 'HASSELBLAD MASTERS 2026' in gold-gradient caps with very wide tracking over an eclipse-like glow.
- Nav is tiny tracked caps (12px, 2px tracking: 'X SYSTEM', 'V SYSTEM', 'PHOCUS') with an outline 'STORE' box.
- Consent bar: 'COOKIE PREFERENCES', 'REJECT ALL', 'ACCEPT ALL' as equal outline buttons. DNA: outfit + Tw Cen; one red accent rgb(203,45,45).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Event/award announcements, luxury launches, dark-mode brand pages.
- Doesn't: Body text; extreme tracking hurts reading beyond a few words.

## Style DNA (optional, compact)
type: Tw Cen caps 12 (2px tracking), wide-tracked display, outfit 14 · color: #000, gold gradient, red accent · shape: square outline buttons · motion: minimal
