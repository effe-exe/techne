## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn the masthead into a statement (a ~1000px-wide serif PROPUBLICA beside 'Investigative Journalism in the Public Interest') and list the live investigations as a 'Reporting on' rail.

## Why it works
- Masthead wordmark fills ~70% of the width in a condensed high-contrast serif; tagline set two lines at the right in Feature Headline.
- 'REPORTING ON' label (tracked caps) followed by active beats (Citizens Detained, Sports Betting, Raw Milk) — the nav is the current investigations.
- Lead: Feature Headline 32/700 at line-height 1.2 left, photo right; dek in grey Brut Grotesque.
- Newsletter footer bar copy: 'No corporate owners, no paywall, no bullsh*t — just deeply researched reporting… more than 600,000 subscribers rely on' — value, proof and number in two lines; close button present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: mission-driven orgs, research groups, any brand with ongoing 'workstreams' to surface.
- Doesn't: brands without a strong name to scale.
