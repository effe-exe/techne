## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make a pro tool approachable by hiding depth behind a near-empty canvas and gestures, and sell it with one honest price instead of a subscription maze.

## Why it works
- Hero headline 'Art is for everyone.' (system-ui 600, 70px, -0.35px) with eyebrow 'CREATIVE TOOLS MADE FOR YOU' over hands-on video of an iPad + Pencil — the claim is inclusivity, matching its ADA Inclusivity award.
- Product cards for Procreate and Procreate Pocket each have exactly two actions, 'Learn more' (grey pill) and 'Buy now' (blue pill) — the purchase is direct and one-time (no subscription tier shown).
- Dark system rgb(11,11,11) with 72px and 24px radius card tiles; DNA found prefers-reduced-motion CSS and 1s fades on video tiles.
- Evidence: long-lived category leader on iPad; ADA Inclusivity jury recognition [moderate as craft signal].

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creative/pro tools aimed at hobbyists as well as experts; pricing pages where simplicity is the trust signal.
- Doesn't: collaborative cloud tools where ongoing server costs genuinely require subscriptions — state that honestly instead.
