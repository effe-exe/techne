## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- State requirements and defaults where decisions happen: 'macOS 13.1 or later recommended' under the download button, and cookies 'Only essential … turned on by default' with 'Essential only' and 'Allow all' as equal buttons.

## Why it works
- Apple-like composition: app icon with violet glow, a 'Screen Studio 4.0 released' chip, then system font (BlinkMacSystemFont) 72px/600 'Beautiful screen recordings, in minutes.' on pure black.
- Subhead highlights the four hard parts in white within grey text — 'zooms, motion, sound, and framing' — a selective-emphasis technique that scans in one glance.
- Single violet CTA (rgb(157,139,255)-family accent) 'Download for Mac' with the macOS requirement line; consent bar defaults to essential cookies and offers 'Essential only' at equal weight.
- Motion: 0.2-0.3s with cubic-bezier(0.23,1,0.32,1); reduced-motion CSS present; very long page (25k px) driven by demo videos.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Downloadable software, especially single-platform; any consent banner.
- Doesn't: The Apple-keynote aesthetic is widely imitated — it signals 'Mac indie' and may not suit cross-platform or enterprise brands.
