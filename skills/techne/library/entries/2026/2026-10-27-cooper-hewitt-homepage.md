## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Expose the whole site map as the header: a ruled grid of every top-level section plus a second colour-coded row of actions, so nothing hides behind a hamburger.

## Why it works
- Black header is a 5-column grid: each section label (VISIT, EXHIBITIONS, LEARNING, NATIONAL DESIGN AWARDS…) sits under a white hairline rule; a third row of actions is set in orange rgb(255,87,0).
- Everything uses the museum's own condensed CooperHewittDisplay in uppercase (40px/500 hero labels), so the nav itself reads as the identity.
- Hero text is set in white-boxed knockout labels over photography ('WELCOME TO COOPER HEWITT' / 'PLAN YOUR VISIT') — legible on any image without gradients.
- Short page (~2,000px) with 0.15–0.2s ease-in-out transitions and reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: institutions or products with 8–12 equal top-level destinations.
- Doesn't: deep hierarchies where a full grid would overwhelm; the hero is still a carousel.
