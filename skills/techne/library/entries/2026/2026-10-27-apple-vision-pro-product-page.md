## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put the two next steps for a high-consideration product side by side at equal reach: a low-commitment trial ('Book a demo') next to 'Buy'.

## Why it works
- First viewport is one portrait photograph on #F5F5F7, the Apple logo + 'Vision Pro' wordmark set ~80px bottom-left and a single factual subline ('New powerful M5 chip and comfortable Dual Knit Band.', 28/600).
- Two pill CTAs bottom-right at the same height: outline 'Book a demo' and filled near-black 'Buy' — the trial path is given equal visual reach for a $3k-class product.
- DNA: SF Pro Text/Display only; 20px radius on 48 elements, 36px cards, 980px pills; section padding 146–180px; transitions mostly 0.1–0.32s with cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.6,1).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any expensive or unfamiliar product where trying first lowers risk (hardware, cars, B2B demos).
- Doesn't: Impulse purchases, where a second CTA only dilutes the primary action.
