## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Ship AI results as editable, non-destructive artifacts (an adjustable mask, a reversible layer) rather than baked-in pixels, so automation never takes away control.

## Why it works
- Copy frames every intelligent feature as a reversible starting point: 'Remove backgrounds with just a click… with a mask you can adjust anytime'; 'Auto Enhance… then refine further'; 'With nondestructive editing… easily revert without affecting image quality'.
- Page follows Apple's product-page system: SF Pro Display 600 at 80px (-1.2px tracking), SF Pro Text 21/1.19 body, black with rgb(29,29,31) tiles, 28px radius cards, link blue rgb(41,151,255).
- Pricing is spelled out: part of Apple Creator Studio subscription with free trial, and 'Pixelmator Pro for Mac is also available as a one-time purchase on the App Store'.
- DNA: cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.6,1) 0.32s/0.24s transitions; prefers-reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product adding AI automation to user content (writing, code, design, spreadsheets): output as a layer, diff or suggestion the user can tune or revert.
- Doesn't: contexts where intermediate editable artifacts confuse novices (one-tap consumer filters) — hide the layer but keep undo.
