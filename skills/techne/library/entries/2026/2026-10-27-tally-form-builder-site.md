## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Remove the commitment: 'Create a free form' leads straight into the editor with 'No signup required' under the button, and the hero demo is the editor itself mid-typing.

## Why it works
- Headline 'The simplest way to create forms' (Inter 64px/700, -2px tracking) with a hand-drawn magenta squiggle under 'simplest' — one human mark on a strict grotesk.
- Single blue CTA rgb(0,112,215) 'Create a free form →' with 'No signup required' caption; nav keeps a second 'Create form' button top-right.
- Hero demo is the real document-style editor (breadcrumbs 'Marketing / Customer f', Draft chip, Customize / Preview / Publish) with a caret typing the title.
- Black hand-drawn doodles (speech bubbles 'Yes!', stars, faces) and magenta strokes frame the hero; warm-grey text rgb(55,53,47) borrowed from Notion's palette; 0.1s transitions.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any tool where trying is cheaper than reading: editors, generators, calculators.
- Doesn't: Products that need account context or data import to do anything useful.
