## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Borrow the visual vernacular of your domain and subvert it: banknote engraving (a currency portrait holding a phone) signals 'money' instantly and 'modern' through the twist.

## Why it works
- Hero illustration is a large line-engraved banknote-style portrait rendered in duotone cyan/violet, wearing an earbud and holding a smartphone — currency engraving as brand language, with a wink.
- Headline "Turn data into revolutionary financial products" in Plaid Sans 76px weight 500, -3.4px (-4.5%) tracking, with a green→cyan→white gradient fill across the words; subline 24px white.
- Floating, translucent pill nav (100px radius, inset from viewport edges); paired CTAs "Talk with our team" (pale mint pill) and "Start building" (black pill with iridescent outline) split the sales vs developer audiences.
- Fine contour-line texture across the blue field; a "Fall Release" spiral-notebook card announces product news. Motion 0.25–0.3s ease-in-out; prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: infrastructure brands that are invisible to end users and need a memorable face; any domain with a rich visual vernacular (maps, blueprints, stamps, receipts).
- Doesn't: the gradient-filled headline and busy texture fight readability if copied onto weaker contrast; keep text on solid areas.
