## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Radical text-first landing: one monospace size, inline links instead of feature cards, and a plain bordered logo grid — speed and confidence through absence of decoration.

## Why it works
- Every text node is ui-monospace at 16px (sizeDistribution has a single entry: 16); hierarchy comes only from weight 700 and a 2px orange rule left of the headline.
- Hero is two paragraphs of prose with blue rgb(11,110,197) inline links ('fastest databases', 'NVMe drives', 'PlanetScale Managed') — the copy is the feature list.
- Customer logos sit in a hairline-bordered 5-column table (Square, Cash App, Cursor, Etsy, Slack, Notion…).
- Nav items separated by '|' pipes; CTAs are square: outlined 'View sandbox' and solid orange rgb(243,88,21) 'Get in touch'; yellow rgb(251,202,0) announcement bar. Only one 0.15s transition.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: technical products with a confident, expert audience; documentation-heavy brands; low-bandwidth contexts.
- Doesn't: consumer products or anything needing visual demonstration; long mono prose tires readers.
