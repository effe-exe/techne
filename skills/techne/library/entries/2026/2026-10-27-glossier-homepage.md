## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the wordmark be the hero's floor: a viewport-wide logotype cropped at the bottom of an editorial still life brands the page without a slogan, while a small white card does the selling.

## Why it works
- Hero: flat-lay editorial photo (ballet flats, knitwear, the boxed fragrance duo on a black-and-white checkerboard) with a viewport-wide white "Glossier." wordmark cropped by the bottom edge. Brand recognition without a headline.
- The commercial message lives in a small square-cornered white card top-left: "NEW!" eyebrow, "Glossier You Duo" (20px Apercu), one line of copy, a "Gift now" button. Clear hierarchy, low pressure.
- Nav in small caps Apercu with one handwritten item ("Gift Guide") as the only flourish; the announcement bar has pause/next controls for its carousel (a nice accessibility touch).
- Zero border-radius across the page (DNA found no radii), Apercu + Apercu Mono only, pink rgb(251,209,218) and electric blue rgb(6,0,255) as accent surfaces. Motion 0.4s ease-in-out; no reduced-motion rules detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: brand-led DTC stores with a strong wordmark and photography; seasonal campaigns.
- Doesn't: stores whose visitors arrive to find a specific SKU fast — the hero is mood first, catalogue second.
