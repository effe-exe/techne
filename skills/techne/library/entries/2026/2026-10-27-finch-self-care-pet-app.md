## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Redirect the caretaking instinct: you look after a pet by looking after yourself, so progress feels like nurture rather than a score.

## Why it works
- Hero is a cartoon meadow under sky blue with clouds, a pink bird pet and an orange cat; 'Your new self-care best friend.' in Rubik 52/700 white with a dark teal outline stroke — storybook legibility over a busy illustration.
- Value prop in one line: 'Daily self-care is finally fun — take care of your pet by taking care of yourself!' then App Store / Google Play badges and a big '5.0 ★★★★★ 500k+ ratings' (site claim).
- Consent dialog offers Customize / Reject All / Accept All with Reject All as a peer button — rare among consumer apps in this set.
- Greens rgb(10,164,108)/rgb(24,150,61) with a 4px solid darker-green bottom shadow on the primary button (tactile, Duolingo-like); 12px radii.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: wellbeing, habit and journaling apps; products where users neglect themselves but would care for something else.
- Doesn't: adult professional tools; pet guilt (a sad pet when you skip) can become manipulative — keep the tone forgiving.
