## Why it works
- Hero: 'How to build real peace' in Aeon Serif Symbol 72/600 (line-height 1.0) with a 3-line sans dek, centred over a darkened documentary photo.
- Chrome is monoFont 12–14px with 1.2px tracking and slash separators (MENU / search, DONATE / NEWSLETTER / SIGN IN).
