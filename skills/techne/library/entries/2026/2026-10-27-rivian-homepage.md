## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give 'Reject All' the same shape, colour and size as 'Accept All' — consent is a brand moment, not a trap.

## Why it works
- Translucent grey nav bar floats 12px in from the edges with a tracked-caps RIVIAN wordmark and an amber 'Demo drive' pill as the single primary action.
- Hero is a campaign carousel (Ghostbusters collaboration, 'Who ya gonna call?' set in the custom 'Adventure' face at 72/600, -3px) with circular arrow buttons and a pill dot pager; below it, model names run at 360px.
- Cookie dialog: 'Accept All' and 'Reject All' are identical black pills, 'Manage Preferences' as a link. DNA: 36–40px radii, 99/9999px pills; signature easing cubic-bezier(0.83,0,0.17,1) on 60 transitions.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Brands with an owned typeface and a campaign-driven homepage; any site that must ask for consent.
- Doesn't: Carousels hide all but the first slide for most visitors; avoid if the second slide matters.
