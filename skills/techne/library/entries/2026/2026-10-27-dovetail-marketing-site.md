## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give an abstract B2B concept a funny physical stand-in: a googly-eyed pixelated photocopier sits inside the headline 'Feed the facts machine'.

## Why it works
- Giant headline in Inter 160px/600 at line-height 0.88 with a dithered photocopier-with-eyes image set inline between words.
- Background is a dim field of ASCII-like characters and digits (data texture) on rgb(10,10,10); the pixel-font PP Mondwest and JetBrains Mono add a lo-fi data voice.
- The positioning sentence sits small at top-left in mixed serif/sans ('The Customer Context Layer that turns every sales call, support ticket, and user interview into context for agents…') with blue 'Book a demo' and outlined 'Start free trial'.
- Review-score stars (G2, Capterra, TrustRadius) top-right; mono uppercase announcement bar 'ASK. NEVER GUESS.'; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: B2B brands that want memorability in a sea of gradients; research/data products.
- Doesn't: The real explanation is small and above the joke — clarity suffers for first-time visitors; humour must match the brand's tone.
