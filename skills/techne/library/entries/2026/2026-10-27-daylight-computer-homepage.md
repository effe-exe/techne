## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put availability right above the buy button ('✓ IN STOCK · SHIPS IN 3–5 BUSINESS DAYS') — the most persuasive copy on a hardware page is a delivery date.

## Why it works
- The page sits inside a rounded inset frame (16px radius) on cream rgb(250,245,242); hero is an overhead photo of the DC-1 on grass, its paper-like screen showing a botanical engraving.
- Headline in ABC Arizona Flare 60/300 with tight -4.2px tracking ('The computer, de-invented') plus one sentence ('designed for deep focus and wellbeing').
- Bottom-right cluster: newsletter card 'GET UPDATES · NO SPAM', stock/shipping line, and a large orange rgb(255,157,0) 'ORDER NOW'; a video thumbnail with an orange play triangle sits bottom-left.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Hardware and physical-goods launches; any product where shipping time is a known worry.
- Doesn't: Back-ordered products — only show a ship date you can keep.
