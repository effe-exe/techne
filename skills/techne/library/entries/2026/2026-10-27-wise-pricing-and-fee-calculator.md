## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- If you don't have plans, say so in the headline area ('You only pay for what you use — no subscriptions or plans') and list a price per feature, each one a link to its own calculator.

## Why it works
- Positioning is the pricing: "WISE IS BUILT TO SAVE YOU MONEY" (Wise Sans 900, 89px, lh 0.85) followed by a direct claim about competitors — "Other providers hide fees in the exchange rate… Not Wise."
- Second section "PRECISE AND PREDICTABLE" introduces a white 18.8px-radius card listing each feature (Send money, …) with a one-line promise and a round arrow button — a price index instead of a tier grid.
- Same two-colour system as the homepage (lime rgb(159,232,112) field, forest rgb(22,51,0) ink), so the pricing page feels like the product, not a legal appendix; secondary audience handled by a single text link ("Switch to Wise Business").
- Short page (6,646px, 109 links), 0.35s motion, prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: pay-as-you-go products with several independently priced services (payments, telecom, cloud, logistics).
- Doesn't: comparative claims about competitors need substantiation and legal review in many jurisdictions.
