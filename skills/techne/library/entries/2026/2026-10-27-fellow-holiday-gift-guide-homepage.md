## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Offer two parallel ways into the same catalogue — 'Shop by moments' and 'Shop by price' — so emotional and budget-driven shoppers each get their own first step.

## Why it works
- Hero is a 2×2 grid of editorial still lifes (espresso machine, breakfast tray, a person with a red drink and playing cards, a cocktail shaker on a silver tray) beside a white panel: "Holiday Gift Guides" in Fellow Solar 48px weight 500 and the explicit framing "Two guides, one organized by holiday moments and the other by price".
- The two links "Shop by moments" and "Shop by price" are equal underlined text links — no visual preference between the browsing models.
- Dense but tidy category nav (Espresso · Coffee Makers · Grinders · Kettles · … · Academy) with the seasonal "Holiday Shop" item tinted; Söhne for UI, near-black rgb(30,30,31) on white; "Shop by category" carousel with dot pager and an outlined "Shop All" pill.
- Caveat: DNA found no prefers-reduced-motion rules; announcement bar is a manual carousel with arrows.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: gifting seasons, marketplaces, any catalogue where intent splits between occasion/use and budget (furniture, travel, wine, software plans).
- Doesn't: tiny catalogues where a single list is clearer.
