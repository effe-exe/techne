## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the primary query a single segmented pill (Where / When / Who) with one coloured action, and show the honest total price by default.

## Why it works
- Search is one white pill with three labelled segments ('Where / Search destinations', 'When / Add dates', 'Who / Add guests') separated by hairlines and a single Rausch-red circular search button — the whole product is one query.
- Category tabs (All / Homes / Hotels / Experiences / Services) use small skeuomorphic 3D icons with an underline on the active tab; the 2025–26 multi-vertical expansion is absorbed without a new nav model.
- Single family 'Airbnb Cereal VF' throughout (28/700 heads, 22/500 at -0.44px, 14 UI); near-black rgb(34,34,34) text with grey rgb(108,108,108) secondaries; 32px/100px pill radii, 8px cards.
- Total price display: Airbnb reports nearly 300,000 listings removed or lowered fees after the 2023 tool, and made all-in pricing the default worldwide [moderate — company-reported].

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any marketplace or booking flow with a few structured query dimensions; any product with junk-fee temptation.
- Doesn't: open-ended search (text-first) or products with more than ~3 required filters — the pill becomes a form.
