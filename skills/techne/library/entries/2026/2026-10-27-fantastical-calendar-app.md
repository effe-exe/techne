## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Parse natural-language input and show the parsed result live before the user commits — the preview teaches the grammar.

## Why it works
- Product site leads with the app icon, a 'Fantasti[cal]' wordmark with the 'cal' boxed in red, and a single benefit line 'Give Your Calendar Superpowers' (Effra 40px/500).
- Hero is surrounded by large glossy, blurred rounded-square shapes in the app's event colours (blue, pink, green, yellow, orange) — the palette of calendar categories doubles as brand.
- CTA pair: solid blue pill 'Start for Free' rgb(64,143,255) and pale pill 'Download Fantastical for Mac' (24px radius); copy names the pain ('Say goodbye to double-bookings and missed updates').
- Natural-language event parsing is the app's signature feature as documented by Flexibits; ADA recognition [moderate as craft signal]. Reduced-motion CSS present on the site.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any input that accepts free text with structure (dates, amounts, assignees, filters); scheduling and task tools.
- Doesn't: Inputs where parsing errors are costly and silent (payments, medical) — always show the parse and allow correction; site visuals feel more 2015-glossy than current.
