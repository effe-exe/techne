## Why it works
- Cookie dialog: 'DECLINE ALL', 'SAVE SETTINGS', 'ACCEPT ALL' are three identical outline buttons; Functional and Statistical toggles start off.
- Behind it: dark video hero with 'Form. Function. Feeling.' (BeoSupreme 24), one line of product copy, an outline 40px pill 'Beosound 2' and a pause control bottom-right.
- DNA: custom BeoSupreme + BeoLogue; off-white text rgb(252,250,238); a deep blue surface rgb(6,13,170); easing cubic-bezier(0.165,0.84,0.44,1) on 45 transitions.
