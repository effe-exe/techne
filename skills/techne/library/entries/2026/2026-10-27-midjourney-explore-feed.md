## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the community's best output be the front page: a full-bleed 'Top day' grid of results with a persistent prompt bar above it sells the capability without a single marketing sentence.

## Why it works
- No hero copy at all: a 4-column edge-to-edge grid of community images under a 'Top day' label, Images/Videos toggle at right — showing range of styles (3D, illustration, print, photo) is the pitch.
- Prompt bar ('Log in to start creating…') sits at the top of the canvas; the left rail groups verbs (Explore, Create, Edit, Organize) and an 'Aesthetics' section (Personalize, Moodboards) — the IA teaches the creative loop.
- Chrome is almost invisible: DM Sans 14-18px only, white ground, slate greys rgb(46,48,56)/rgb(115,121,140), one vermilion accent rgb(242,51,13) for the active nav pill and Sign up; 9999px pills.
- Weakness: no prefers-reduced-motion CSS detected; dense imagery has no visible captions/credits at grid level.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creative tools, marketplaces and UGC products where output quality is the selling point.
- Doesn't: products whose outputs are private or unremarkable to strangers; regulated domains where showing user content needs consent and moderation.
