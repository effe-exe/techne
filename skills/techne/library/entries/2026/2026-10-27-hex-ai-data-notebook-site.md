## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Write the positioning as a sentence with inline product chips — 'Rethinking [BI] [Conversational analytics] and [Context management] for the agentic era' — so the subhead doubles as navigation to the three products.

## Why it works
- Headline pairs PP Formula SemiExtended 74/700 (-1.85px) 'The Analytics platform' with a large italic serif second line 'for humans and agents' — the human/agent duality is expressed typographically.
- Subhead is set at 24px with three rounded chips carrying small coloured icons (chart, chat, graph) inline in the sentence.
- CTAs and the nav 'Get started' wear corner selection handles (small circles at each corner) — a canvas/editor motif signalling 'this is a tool you build in'; graph-paper grid background, mono ticker of resources across the top.
- Ink rgb(20,20,28)/plum rgb(49,38,59) on pale grid rgb(236,237,242), 1-4px radii; WebGL with reduced-motion CSS; customer logos under a mono 'TRUSTED BY LEADING DATA COMPANIES' label with bracket ticks.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: multi-product platforms, data and dev tools wanting craft personality; any subhead that must introduce several modules.
- Doesn't: products with a single job (chips become decoration); selection-handle motifs only fit creation tools.
