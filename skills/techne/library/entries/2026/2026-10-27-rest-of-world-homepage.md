## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Encode the mission in the wordmark (diacritics drawn from many scripts over 'rest of world') and carry it through one saturated blue used for every label.

## Why it works
- Wordmark sets Latin letters with accents from multiple languages in electric blue rgb(36,46,247), flanked by ruled topic tabs (TECH GIANTS · EV REVOLUTION / CHINA OUTSIDE CHINA · INNOVATION) in Input Mono caps.
- Kickers ('INNOVATION') and 'LATEST STORIES' are Input Mono 12px uppercase, 0.96px tracking, in the same blue; headlines are Moderat 28/700, deks in Georgia.
- Every list item carries read time ('3 MIN READ') and audio icons where listening is available — cost of attention stated up front.
- Membership ask is a single blue card in the right column, not an overlay.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: mission-driven brands and newsrooms; any feed that benefits from read-time and audio affordances.
- Doesn't: brands without a clear point of view to encode.
