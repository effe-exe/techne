## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put the working quote in the hero: a live 'You send / Recipient gets' calculator with the locked rate and arrival time answers the only question before the visitor asks it.

## Why it works
- The hero's centrepiece is a functional transfer calculator in a white 28px-radius card: "You send exactly 1,000.00 USD → Recipient gets 891.72 EUR", with a rate chip "1 USD = 0.8966 EUR" (lock icon) and an inline fee-discount note for transfers over 25,000 USD. The pitch is the price.
- Amounts are set huge (~52px Wise Sans heavy) and right-aligned, making the numbers the hierarchy, while labels sit small (~14–16px Inter) above.
- Headline in Wise Sans weight 900 at 89px, line-height 0.85, all caps, in forest rgb(22,51,0) on a full lime field rgb(159,232,112) — the brand is one colour pair, used at full saturation.
- Subline states a falsifiable promise with a footnote ("Most arrive in under 20 seconds.¹"). Pill CTAs (9999px), 0.35s cubic-bezier(0.8,0.05,0.2,0.95); DNA found prefers-reduced-motion rules.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: anything with a quote — FX, shipping, insurance, loans, SaaS usage — where a calculator can replace a pricing table.
- Doesn't: products whose price can't be computed without onboarding; a fake estimator that changes at checkout is worse than none.
