## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- One playful headline, one tactile primary button, a quieter 'I already have an account' — and the full catalogue as a chip row along the bottom.

## Why it works
- Left: an exploding cluster of characters around the green owl; right: 'The most fun way to learn languages, chess, and more!' (duolingo-sans 32/700 in grey).
- Green rgb(88,204,2) 'GET STARTED' has a darker bottom edge that makes it look pressable; below it an outline 'I ALREADY HAVE AN ACCOUNT' in blue caps — new vs returning users in two clicks.
- Bottom rail of course chips with flags/icons (English, Chess, Math, Spanish…), carousel arrows; 'SITE LANGUAGE: ENGLISH' top-right. DNA: 12px radius; very few transitions.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Consumer apps whose homepage is a gateway to sign-up; multi-course or multi-category products.
- Doesn't: Products needing explanation; this works because the brand is already known.
