## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn the brand name into a tiny live widget ('6 days until Monday'): personality through a working detail rather than decoration.

## Why it works
- The hero sentence is a rotating word: 'We make digital (and magical)…' in small grotesk, then 'Branding' in Clarendon Light at 80px, line-height 0.8, centred on white.
- Top-right is a calendar icon with '6 days until Monday', a live countdown that turns the studio name into a working joke.
- The work list is tagged by type (Experiences, Branding, Products, Platform), with recognisable clients (Google Gemini, Strava Year In Sport, Bang & Olufsen, Netflix identity), so the filters double as proof.
- NB International Pro 14–22 for UI against the Clarendon display; GSAP-driven; WebGL present; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Brands whose name or idea can become a live datum (a countdown, clock, weather, counter); any agency homepage.
- Doesn't: Serious or regulated contexts where whimsy undercuts trust.
