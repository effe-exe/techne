## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn the processing wait into the reward: CapWords' capture → cut-out → confirm → reveal sequence is animated so the API latency becomes the 'peeling a sticker off the world' moment.

## Why it works
- Apple's citation: 'Learn the name of a thing by taking a picture of it'; objects become interactive stickers, and 'each flash card transition is accompanied by a real-world sound… (and helps the word stick)'.
- Per Apple's design article: background removal via VisionKit; a confirmation step ('is this the object?') 'gives the system time to get results from the API'; a micro-animation covers the backend stages so 'most users don't notice the processing'.
- Origin story: the founder's daughter sticking stickers everywhere — 'peel things off from the real world and collect them'; design grounded in 'real-world physics: sound, touch, and sight cues'.
- Reviews (via search summary) note limits: no structured grammar lessons; free tier caps scans.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any AI or network feature with latency (generation, recognition, upload): design a staged, meaningful transition and a confirm step that buys time honestly.
- Doesn't: high-frequency professional workflows where a cute reveal on every action becomes friction.
