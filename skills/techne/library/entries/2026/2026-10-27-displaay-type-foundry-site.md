## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Colour-code the three buying intents (Trials, Tester, Buy) as separate pill buttons so the user picks a commitment level, not a single 'Shop' door.

## Why it works
- Two tiers of outlined pills in Saans: primary nav at 28px (Typefaces, Custom, Help, About) beside a filled black 'Displaay®' logo pill, and a 15px sub-row (New, Custom, Offline MERCH, Our Features, In Use, Retails); radius 10px on 26 elements.
- The mission statement is set in the foundry's serif (Serrif) at ~21px on a grey #C9CAC9 band; next to it three action pills: black 'Trials' with download icon, pale blue #A2E3FF 'Tester', pale green 'Buy'. Commitment level is colour-coded.
- 'New Releases' at 44px/600, tracking -1.1px, over a large photograph of physical specimen cards in acrylic boxes: real-world printed type as proof.
- 80% of the page is media; transitions are 0.15s with Material's cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1); no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any product with tiered commitment (free trial, try online, buy) where users self-select.
- Doesn't: Single-action funnels; three equal pills would dilute a single clear CTA.
