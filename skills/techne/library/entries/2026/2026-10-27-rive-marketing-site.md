## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Treat each showcase like a spec sheet: '01 / 06 NOW PLAYING', what it is, 'RUNS ON', 'USE CASES', then 'PLAY IT LIVE' and 'GET SOURCE' — proof you can inspect.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed cinematic scene (a hazy 3D city with flying craft) built in Rive itself serves as hero, with a 6-segment progress bar for the rotating showcase.
- Display type Orbitron 48px/900 uppercase ('BUILD GAMES, APPS, AND INTERACTIVE UI') with PragmataPro mono for labels (183 nodes) — a game-UI voice matching the audience.
- 'Start with your coding agent. No account needed to try it.' above a copyable prompt box with a gradient border and COPY button — the first step is a paste, not a sign-up.
- Tiny radii (1.5px dominant), monochrome UI; WebGL present with reduced-motion CSS.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Creative/dev tools whose output is the proof; any showcase gallery that should be verifiable.
- Doesn't: Non-technical audiences; the game-HUD aesthetic is very specific to Rive's market.
