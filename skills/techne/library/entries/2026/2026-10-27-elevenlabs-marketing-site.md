## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make an invisible output tangible: each voice is a large gradient orb with a play button you can hear from the hero, and product lines sit in a segmented tab so a multi-product company still has one clear first interaction.

## Why it works
- Hero pairs a left-aligned 48px Waldenburg headline at weight 300 (-0.96px, line-height 1.08) with a right-column positioning paragraph — the two-column split keeps the headline short ('Bringing technology to life').
- Below, a segmented control (ElevenCreative / ElevenAgents / ElevenAPI, each with a coloured dot) switches a carousel of voice 'orbs' — grainy gradient spheres labelled Characters / Narration / Conversational with a centred play button: sound is sampled with one click, no sign-up.
- Monochrome chrome so the orbs carry all colour: rgb(253,252,252) ground, black pill CTAs (Sign up / Contact sales outline), warm grey rgb(119,113,105) secondary text, rgb(245,243,241) panel; radii 9999px pills and 16px panels.
- Uses WebGL but ships prefers-reduced-motion CSS; transitions 0.15-0.3s standard ease (194 uses).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: products whose output is audio, motion or anything non-visual; multi-product platforms needing one hero for several audiences.
- Doesn't: B2B tools where a carousel hides key info; avoid if samples can't play reliably (autoplay restrictions, accessibility — needs captions/transcripts).
