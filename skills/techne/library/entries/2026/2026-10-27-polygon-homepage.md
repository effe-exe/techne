## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give commodity photo cards a brand silhouette — a chamfered (cut-corner) magenta frame — so the grid is recognisable even with arbitrary images.

## Why it works
- Lead and secondary cards are framed with a red/magenta border whose top-left corner is cut at 45°, echoing the polygon logo mark.
- Latest rail: magenta caps relative time ('3 HOURS AGO'), Montserrat 900 headline, byline and comment-count icon, thumbnail right.
- Montserrat 32–42/900 for headlines (133 uses) and Roboto for body (162), white ground, 10px card radii (108 uses).
- Top topic chips (Zelda • GTA 6 • Guides • Pokémon) in magenta separated by dots surface what readers come for.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: feeds with user/stock imagery needing a brand layer (marketplaces, communities).
- Doesn't: calm or premium contexts — the frame is loud.
