## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give a dense news river one loud, ownable brand gesture (a giant vertical wordmark and a single electric accent) and keep everything else typographic and quiet.

## Why it works
- A ~400px-tall vertical 'Verge' wordmark runs down the left gutter beside the lead image — brand expressed through layout, not decoration.
- One accent, rgb(82,0,255) electric violet, used for author names, the LATEST pill toggle and SUBSCRIBE; everything else is near-black rgb(19,19,19) on white.
- PolySans (139 uses) for headlines at 24/700, fkRomanStandard serif for deks, polySansMono 11px uppercase with 1.1px tracking for meta — three clear voices.
- Right-rail 'Latest / Following' segmented toggle turns the river into a personalisable stream without leaving the page.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: content brands that need recognisability inside a commodity layout.
- Doesn't: at capture a ~250px empty ad slot sat above the masthead and a PMC terms banner covered the lower fold — the brand gesture fights for space with monetisation.
