## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Sell work in progress openly: label it 'Type in progress', show the unfinished specimens big, and make 'Browse' the only call to action.

## Why it works
- The fold is a full-bleed wall of in-progress display specimens (a blobby 'SYRSING', a wedge-serif line, a heavy '352 8th!') on warm grey #EDEDEA, with a single white card 'TYPE IN PROGRESS' and a blue 'BROWSE FONTS' pill.
- The entire UI is one monospace (VCTR Mono Web, 110 text nodes) at 15.7–19px with #333 text and #878787 secondaries; Fonts / Foundries / Activity nav makes the marketplace's multi-foundry nature explicit.
- Specimen motion has explicit up/pause/down controls at the right edge (visible in the screenshot), so the animated wall can be stopped.
- The cookie bar states essential-only cookies with a single 'Got It' — no consent theatre because nothing optional is set.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Early-access or beta marketplaces, creator platforms, anything sold before it is finished where honesty about status builds trust.
- Doesn't: Products where 'in progress' would undermine safety or reliability expectations (finance, health).
