## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Name your invisible algorithmic features and explain each with a concrete, human outcome ('so the loud ones don't blow your ears out') instead of technical specs.

## Why it works
- Whole site is one ~700px centered column: large orange broadcast-tower icon, 'Overcast®', one line 'Free, simple, award-winning podcast player for iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch.', an App Store badge.
- Two differentiators get trademark-named headings, Smart Speed® and Voice Boost®, each with two short paragraphs of plain benefit copy ('Conversations still sound so natural that you'll forget it's on — until you see how much extra time you've saved').
- Only -apple-system type (32/24/16, weight 500) and one colour, orange rgb(252,126,15), used for links and the icon; no motion, no tracking-heavy JS observed.
- Business model visible in nav (Podcaster Info, Ads) — free with ads, no paywall surprise.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: products whose value is a background algorithm (noise suppression, compression, ranking, smart defaults): brand it and show the saved time/effort.
- Doesn't: when the feature is commodity — trademarking a standard feature reads as puffery.
