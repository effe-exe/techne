## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A fully committed tone of voice can reach every microcopy surface (nav labels, CTAs, legal) — extreme consistency is what makes a commodity product a brand.

## Why it works
- Voice everywhere: nav items 'COUNTRY CLUB' and 'TIMEWASTER 5000', section headline 'MURDER YOUR THIRST' (Acumin Pro Condensed ~60), CTA 'HELL YES'.
- Caution: an email-capture modal opens on load ('WANNA CHEAT ON YOUR OTHER BEVERAGE COMPANY?', 2 free can coolers) over a collab carousel; its legal line ('agree to be brainwashed…') is ~9px grey on black.
- DNA: Acumin Pro + acumin-pro-condensed; 5px radius on 21 elements; 0.5s transitions.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Commodity products where brand is the only differentiator; campaign microsites.
- Doesn't: On-load modals and tiny legal text are not worth copying.
