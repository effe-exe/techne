## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give a developer API a luxury voice: serif display over pure black, one tactile 3D object, and mono for everything technical.

## Why it works
- H1 'Email for developers' set in Domaine 96/400 serif at -1% tracking on pure black rgb(0,0,0), with a subtle top-to-bottom grey gradient on the glyphs.
- A single textured 3D Rubik-style cube (WebGL detected) is the only image; a faint diagonal light streak crosses the fold.
- Type system mixes Domaine (display), ABC Favorit 56 (section heads, -2.8px), Inter 14 (UI) and Commit Mono (150 nodes, code/data).
- Section rhythm: 96px top padding repeated 10 times; CTAs are dark grey ghost pills; reduced-motion honored.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: devtools and infrastructure wanting a premium, editorial feel; small-surface APIs.
- Doesn't: products that need broad, non-technical accessibility — light grey on black at small sizes needs contrast checking.
