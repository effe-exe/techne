## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Present a catalogue as the physical object it sells: the homepage is a 3D stack of book spines, each a faithful, clickable rendering of the real cover material.

## Why it works
- WebGL stack of book spines on aubergine-black rgb(32,24,25); each spine reproduces the edition's texture (marbled paper, foil, cracked gold) with author and title in Ivar Headline.
- Left edge carries a tick-mark index (one dash per title) plus video and podcast icons — a scrubber for the stack.
- Minimal chrome: logo + italic 'Ideas for progress' tagline top-left and a '?' help button bottom-left.
- Ivar Headline/Ivar Text only (57 uses), 16–25px; CTAs are flat colour chips (pale cyan rgb(189,243,245), gold-on-purple).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: small catalogues of physical goods (books, records, objects) where materiality sells.
- Doesn't: large catalogues; no reduced-motion CSS was found for the WebGL scene.
