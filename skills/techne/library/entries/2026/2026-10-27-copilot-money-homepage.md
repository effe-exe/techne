## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn the product's own data primitive (spending category chips) into the hero's visual — floating, depth-blurred tags make 'organized' tangible before any screenshot.

## Why it works
- Headline "Your money, beautifully organized." in Jokker Semibold at 148px, line-height 0.9, -2.96px (-2%) tracking, with a silvery white-to-grey gradient fill on near-black navy rgb(0,8,20).
- Around it float rotated category pills (Groceries, Date Night, Baby, Wedding, Home, Dancing) each with an emoji and a saturated colour (green rgb(0,204,75), red rgb(255,68,51), blue rgb(28,108,255), gold, pink, orange), blurred to different depths — a depth-of-field effect that puts the type in focus.
- One blue "Get started" CTA with the app icon inside; nav reduced to Features · Pricing · Reviews (34 links on the page).
- Body type in Matter (variable) at 18px; cards 20–24px radius. Caveat: DNA found no prefers-reduced-motion rules.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: consumer apps whose core object is visual (tags, cards, stickers); premium-feeling personal tools.
- Doesn't: the 148px gradient headline and blur are expensive and fragile at small widths; check mobile legibility.
