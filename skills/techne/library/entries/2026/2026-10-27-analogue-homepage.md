## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Split the hero diagonally to present two sibling products at once — each half owns a colour world, a name and one link.

## Why it works
- One diagonal cut divides the screen: left deep blue-to-black with Mega Sg+ and Genesis cartridges, right white/grey with Super Nt+ and SNES cartridges; the consoles meet in the middle.
- Each side has only a 47px name (circularXx 500, -2.8px tracking) and an underlined 'Explore Now'; a quiet centred 'Ships Dec 2026' sets expectations.
- Navigation is reduced to three two-line text columns at 13px; radii 17.6px; transitions 0.3s cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Two-product launches, A/B editions, light/dark variants, 'for you vs for teams' splits.
- Doesn't: More than two items; the diagonal stops reading as a choice.
