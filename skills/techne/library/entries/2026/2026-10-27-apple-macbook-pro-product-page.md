## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Answer 'how much?' inside the hero: a compact price capsule (outright and monthly) with Buy beside it, instead of hiding price below the fold.

## Why it works
- Black page, two laptops lit from the side forming a 'V'; eyebrow 'MacBook Pro' (28/600) above an 80px headline 'Fast runs in the family.' filled with a grey-to-teal gradient, then 'Now with M5, M5 Pro, and M5 Max.'.
- A dark rounded capsule states 'From $1999 or $166.58/mo. for 12 mo.' plus a lease line, each footnoted, with a blue 'Buy' pill inside — price, financing and action in one object.
- DNA: 28px radius on 81 elements, 999px pills; 16 sections at 144px top padding; max-width 1260px; transitions 0.1/0.24/0.32/0.344s.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Considered purchases with a stable list price; subscription or financing offers that can be stated in one line.
- Doesn't: Quote-based or highly configurable pricing where a single 'from' number would mislead.
