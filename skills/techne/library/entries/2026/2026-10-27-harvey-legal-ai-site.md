## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- For enterprise AI, sell the buyer's proof of adoption: a 'Command Center' with DAU/MAU, per-feature usage and a Peers comparison toggle, plus a nudge ('15 recommended actions to increase your Harvey usage') — the admin's success metric is the hero.

## Why it works
- HarveySerif 72px (-0.9px, line-height 1.05) 'Build a Frontier Legal Organization' in two lines, right column with a one-sentence trust claim and a black square 'Request a Demo' — law-firm gravitas through type, not imagery.
- The product frame (dark sidebar: New Thread, Agents, Vaults, Command Center; Apps: Contract Intelligence) shows '[Firm]'s Command Center' with 49.5% DAU/MAU ↑5%, a 6-months/2026 filter, product-usage bars by feature vs Peers, and 'Needs attention' legend.
- Backdrop is a textured grey-green painted surface; palette near-black rgb(15,14,13) and warm white rgb(250,250,249), muted grey rgb(112,109,102); 3-8px radii; WebGL with reduced-motion CSS.
- Weakness: funding announcement bar ('We Raised $550M…') above the nav competes with the story.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: B2B AI sold to admins/leaders who must justify spend; any seat-based SaaS where adoption is the renewal risk.
- Doesn't: consumer or self-serve products; peer benchmarks need real, consented aggregate data.
