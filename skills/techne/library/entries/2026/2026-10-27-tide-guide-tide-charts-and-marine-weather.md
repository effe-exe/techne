## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the palette carry information: Tide Guide's colours follow the sky through the day, so the chart's background tells you time-of-day before you read an axis.

## Why it works
- Apple's citation: 'rich full-screen charts present a boatload of weather data — hour-by-hour forecasts, water temperature, swell height — in a crisp, clear manner that's easily understood by even the most landlocked'.
- 'The app's palette is even designed to match the color of the sky throughout the day' — ambient colour as a data channel.
- Widgets are positioned as the fast path ('help swimmers and boat captains save a step right from the Home Screen'); release notes mention scheduled automatic Live Activities and monthly tide tables via a station buoy icon (App Store listing, via search summary).
- Long-run craft signal: finalist 2023, winner 2026 [moderate]. Listing declares Larger Text, Dark Interface and Sufficient Contrast support.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: time-series and forecast UIs (energy, transit, health, finance) — big single chart per screen, ambient colour for context, widget for the one number.
- Doesn't: when colour already encodes a variable; adding time-of-day tint would conflict.
