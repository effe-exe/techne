## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Open the purchase flow with a choice architecture, not a form: one big 'Shop iPhone' title, a 'Take your pick' card row of models, and a visible path to a human specialist.

## Why it works
- Page title "Shop iPhone" in SF Pro Display 80px weight 600, -1.2px tracking on #f5f5f7 grey; section heading uses a two-tone pattern ("All models." in near-black rgb(29,29,31) + "Take your pick." in grey rgb(110,110,115)).
- Models are white 18px-radius cards in a horizontal carousel with a round next arrow; each card carries a small orange "NEW" eyebrow, the model name (~28px), and the product image — scannable, with no specs at this level.
- Secondary paths are explicit and calm: a tab row (All Models · Shopping Guides · Ways to Save · Accessories · Setup and Support · …) and top-right links "Connect with a Specialist ↗" / "Find an Apple Store ↗", plus a floating specialist avatar bottom-right.
- Financing promo sits in a dismissible carousel ribbon under the global nav rather than in the cards. Motion 0.3s cubic-bezier(0,0,0.5,1). DNA found no reduced-motion rules on this page.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: configurable or high-consideration products (devices, cars, furniture, plans); any shop with a small catalogue of hero products.
- Doesn't: large catalogues need filters and search, not a carousel.
