## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Pair a warm editorial serif headline with a calm neutral palette to make money management feel domestic and unstressed, then back it with named third-party awards right under the fold.

## Why it works
- Headline "The modern way to manage money" in Copernicus serif at 48px, weight 350, -3.2px tracking (very tight, ~-6.7%), inside a frosted card over a domestic kitchen video showing the real dashboard on a laptop.
- Palette is warm paper rgb(246,245,243) with ink rgb(34,32,29) and a single orange accent rgb(243,91,22) for "Sign up" and the hero CTA "Unlock better money habits"; ABC Oracle sans for UI.
- Trust row immediately below: 4.9 stars/100,000+ reviews, WSJ "Best Overall Budgeting App", Forbes "Best Budgeting App of 2026", CNBC — specific, attributed claims. A small lock row ("Bank-level security") sits inside the hero card.
- Slow ambient motion (1.5–2s, cubic-bezier(0.075,0.82,0.165,1)) for reveals; prefers-reduced-motion rules present. Cookie notice discloses session replay but offers only a close X.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: household/personal products where the emotion to manage is anxiety; any product with genuine third-party awards.
- Doesn't: the promo strip ("30% OFF … code WELCOME") competes with the calm tone; keep discounts out of the first impression if calm is the point.
