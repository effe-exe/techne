## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Cautionary: an AI whose headline feature is being 'completely undetectable' to the other people in the meeting designs deception into the product — disclose AI participation to everyone affected; compare Granola's 'without a meeting bot' honesty, which is about friction, not concealment.

## Why it works
- EB Garamond 80/500 headline '#1 Undetectable AI for Meetings' over an alpine sunrise illustration; subhead 'takes perfect meeting notes and gives real-time answers, all while completely undetectable'; nav item literally named 'Undetectability'.
- Product overlay floats over a video call: 'What should I say?' button, a scripted answer about discounted cash flow, and Assist / Follow-up questions / Recap chips plus a 'Hide' control — the UI is built to feed the user lines covertly.
- Craft is real (glass panels, keycap hints '⌘ ↵ for Assist', single blue 'Get for Mac' CTA, reduced-motion CSS) — which is exactly why it is a useful caution: polish does not make a deceptive premise acceptable.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Use as a caution for: meeting, interview, exam, sales-call and dating assistants — design visible disclosure and consent for all participants.
- Don't use as a positive model; for honest meeting AI see 2026-10-27-granola-marketing-site.
