## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Design kids' software as open-ended toys with no scores, timers or rewards loops — and say 'non-addictive' out loud as a promise to parents.

## Why it works
- Above the fold the value proposition is an ethical commitment: 'Non-addictive, educational games for ages 2-8.' under 'Montessori play in a whole new way!' in the custom rounded 'dashes' face, coral on white.
- Hand-drawn, flat-colour toy-town illustration in an iPad frame (river, bridge, houses, picnic), with a kid character peeking around it — warm, tactile, no UI chrome.
- Single CTA 'Try it free for 7 days' (sky-blue rgb(41,162,222) 100px pill, yellow text) plus three store badges; proof row: TIME100 2025, Apple Design Award Winner, App Store Award Winner, Apple quote 'Thoughtful design and mesmerizing interactions'.
- DNA: reduced-motion CSS present; palette of sky blue, coral rgb(239,105,80), sunshine yellow rgb(253,235,106), teal rgb(28,181,165).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product for children or vulnerable users; consumer apps that want to compete on wellbeing rather than engagement.
- Doesn't: skill-drill products where measurable progression is the goal (though even there, avoid compulsion loops).
