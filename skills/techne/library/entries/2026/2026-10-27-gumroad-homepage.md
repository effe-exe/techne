## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Set a tiny, achievable goal as the headline ('Go from 0 to $1') — reframing success to the first dollar lowers the barrier more than any feature list.

## Why it works
- Headline "Go from 0 to $1" in ABC Favorit at 96px weight 400 (sizes reach 192px further down), subline "Anyone can earn their first dollar online… see what sticks, and get paid." — ambition scaled down to the first milestone.
- Neo-brutalist kit: 1px black outlines, square black "Start selling" button next to an outlined "Search marketplace…" field (seller and buyer entry points side by side), off-white paper rgb(244,244,240), and pink rgb(255,144,232) 3D "G" coins with hard black strokes scattered around the frame.
- Header ends in a full-height black "Start selling" block, divided from "Log in" by a vertical rule; a GitHub "10K ★" badge beside the logo and "Contribute or fork on GitHub" under the CTAs signal openness.
- Flat colour (yellow rgb(255,201,0), lemon rgb(241,243,51), red rgb(220,52,30)), no shadows; 0.15s transitions; prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creator tools, marketplaces, any product whose buyers fear starting; brands that can carry playfulness.
- Doesn't: regulated finance or enterprise procurement contexts where the brutalist jokiness undercuts trust.
