## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Draw the system as the hero: the product at the centre with every rail it connects (ACH, RTP, FedNow, wire, stablecoins) as labelled monospace chips — the diagram is the feature list.

## Why it works
- Hero diagram: a black logo tile with orthogonal connector lines to pale-mint chips labelled in mt-mono caps (FEDNOW, RTP, STABLECOINS, WIRE, CHECKS, ACH, PUSH-TO-CARD). Scope is communicated before any paragraph.
- Headline "Money Movement for Builders" in mt-neue-display 71px weight 450 (light-ish, normal tracking), centred; grey subline names concrete capabilities (onboarding, accounts, ledgering, compliance).
- Square-cornered, architectural buttons: filled black "Talk to us →" and outlined "Start building →" (2–4px radii) on warm off-white rgb(248,247,245) with a faint grid fading in from the top-right.
- Four-family type system (mt-sans, mt-mono, mt-neue-display, mt-neue-text); motion mostly 0.15s. Caveat: cookie notice uses implied consent ("By continuing to use our site, you consent") with only a Close button; DNA found no reduced-motion rules.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: APIs, integrations, platforms whose value is connectivity; technical buyers who prefer a schematic to a metaphor.
- Doesn't: consumer audiences, for whom rail names are jargon.
