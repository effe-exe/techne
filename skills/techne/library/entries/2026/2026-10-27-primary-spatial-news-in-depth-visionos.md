## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- In spatial or immersive media, the UI should get out of the way of the story: minimal chrome, no sensational hooks, provenance stated up front.

## Why it works
- Apple's citation: founded by a former Associated Press journalist; 'shines the spatial spotlight on breaking news… without once resorting to sensationalism or clickbait. And with a properly minimal UI, Primary wisely gets out of the way of the news'.
- App Store listing (via search summary): spatial video shot on iPhone by reporters in the field; 'every scene is witnessed, verified, and documented on the ground, with no AI involved'; free, no subscription or IAP; no data collected.
- Business-model honesty (no IAP, no tracking) aligns with the anti-engagement editorial stance.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: news, documentary and education products, especially immersive ones; any feed tempted by engagement-optimised hooks.
- Doesn't: entertainment formats where spectacle is the point.
