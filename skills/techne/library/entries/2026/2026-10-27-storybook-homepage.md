## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put adoption numbers in the CTA row (80.58m installs/month, 2,282 contributors, 'Version 10') so social proof sits exactly where the decision is made.

## Why it works
- CTA row: white pill 'Get Started', outlined 'npm create storybook@latest' with copy icon, then two stats '80.58m Installs per month' and '2282 Contributors'; a large 'Version 10' marker at right.
- Below, tabs Development / Interaction testing / Visual testing / Documentation switch a full-width mock of the real Storybook UI (sidebar, canvas, Controls panel with 'Space Helmet X24').
- Headline 'Build, test & document components' in Nunito Sans 56/700 over a pink→red→navy gradient; GitHub 91,144 and Ctrl K search in the header.
- Pill CTAs (9999px), ui-monospace for code; 0.15–0.3s transitions; reduced-motion honored.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: open-source and devtools with strong usage numbers; tool UIs that explain themselves when shown.
- Doesn't: early products — small numbers in the CTA row hurt more than they help.
