## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- When you sell two different things, give each its own card with one CTA and one eligibility chip, under a single headline and a row of three checkable proofs.

## Why it works
- Centred headline 'Sleep better all with Headspace' (Headspace Apercu ~52/700), then three check-icon proofs: '1,000+ meditations and Ebb, your AI companion', 'Affordable therapy that accepts insurance', 'Trusted mental health app with 105M+ downloads'.
- Two warm-cream cards (rgb(249,244,242), 32px radius) split the offer: 'Mental health app…' → dark pill 'Try for $0'; 'Online therapy that accepts insurance' → 'Check your coverage'; each with a 'HSA/FSA Eligible' check chip.
- Orange smiling-blob character and phone mock ('Managing Anxiety') keep the tone soft; yellow rgb(255,206,0) promo bar; blue 'Try for free' pill in nav.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Two-sided or two-product offers (app + service, consumer + business) and health/finance where eligibility matters.
- Doesn't: Three or more products; the side-by-side split stops being scannable.
