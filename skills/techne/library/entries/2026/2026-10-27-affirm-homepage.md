## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Show the instalment exactly as it will appear — '0% APR option · $124.73 every month for 6 months' — and offer a no-commitment check ('Check my purchasing power') before any sign-up.

## Why it works
- Headline "Flexible plans for every purchase" in Axiforma for Affirm at 80px weight 300, -3.2px (-4%) tracking, white on deep indigo-black rgb(19,19,31); subline names both plan types ("interest-free or more time to pay").
- The hero phone shows a concrete plan row: a lavender "0% APR option" chip and "$124.73 every month for 6 months" — the monthly amount and the term in one line.
- Two distinct commitments in the nav: "Sign up" (white pill) and "Check my purchasing power" (outlined pill), plus a top-level For shoppers / For businesses switch.
- Cookie bar offers Manage / Reject all / Accept all as three identical outlined pills. Motion 0.2–0.3s with an expo-out curve cubic-bezier(0.19,1,0.22,1); prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any financing, subscription or instalment offer — always pair the monthly figure with the term (and the APR).
- Doesn't: pay-over-time can encourage overspending; the honest version shows total cost and APR at the decision point, which should be verified in the live checkout, not just the homepage.
