## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Name the product like a menu item and let a close-up photo carry it: eyebrow joke, 80px dish name, one lime 'ORDER NOW'.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed close-up of a wrap in hand; eyebrow 'SURPRISE! THERE'S A PICKLE', then 'Molly's Big Dill Caesar Wrap' in SweetSans ~80 with tight tracking, and a lime rgb(230,255,85) pill 'ORDER NOW'.
- Cream rgb(244,243,231) chrome, caps nav split around a centred wordmark, dark-green 'ORDER' pill; an 'Enable Accessibility' button is the first item on the page.
- Cookie card: 'Accept all', 'Reject all', 'Manage' as three identical dark-green pills. DNA: SweetSans/SweetSansText + Grenette serif; 20px radius on 17 elements; 0.3s ease-in-out.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Food, retail and any product with nameable, photographable SKUs.
- Doesn't: Abstract or B2B products without a hero object.
