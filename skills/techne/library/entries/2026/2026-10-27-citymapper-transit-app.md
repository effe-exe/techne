## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put the full trip query (Start, End, swap, Now / Arrive / Leave) on the landing page, and personalise it to the user's city at a glance.

## Why it works
- 'Making Cities Usable' (proxima-soft 44/700, 'Usable' in green rgb(55,171,46)) above a line-drawn skyline of buses, trains and bikes with the green mascot.
- Main block is a working planner on slate blue rgb(65,113,147): Start/End fields with a swap button, Now / Arrive / Leave time modes, a big green 'GO' pill; right side shows 'London' with a top-hatted mascot and 'Switch City' — locality as identity.
- Rounded 20px panels, proxima-soft throughout; secondary panel 'Download Our App — One of the most loved apps on mobile'.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: routing, logistics, any query with origin/destination/time.
- Doesn't: mascot whimsy in high-stress contexts must not cost speed; evidence here is reputation, not data.
