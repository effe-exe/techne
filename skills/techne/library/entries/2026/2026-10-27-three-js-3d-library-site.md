## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the homepage nothing but a wall of what people built, with a plain mono sidebar for everything else and a 'submit project' button: the community is the pitch.

## Why it works
- Zero marketing copy: a 300px white sidebar in Roboto Mono (blue #049EF4 section heads Learn / Tools / Community / Code / Resources / Merch at 18px, grey #444 links at 15px) and a full-bleed 6-column grid of square project thumbnails (games, product configurators, data art).
- A version tag 'r186' sits in a bordered chip next to the wordmark; a floating 25px-radius 'submit project' pill sits bottom-right — the feedback loop into the gallery is always visible.
- The grid carries 282 links; media covers the page several times over (mediaCoverage 5.47), and thumbnails show real variety (Japanese-language game art, billiards, terrariums) as proof of range.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Platforms, engines, template marketplaces: anywhere user output is diverse and abundant.
- Doesn't: Early products with few showcase projects; a sparse wall signals emptiness.
