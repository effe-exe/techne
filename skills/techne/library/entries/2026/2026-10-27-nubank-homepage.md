## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Start sign-up with the one identifier the market already uses (Brazil's CPF) in a single field, right in the hero — the first step is the form, not a button to a form.

## Why it works
- Hero signup card (frosted glass, ~16px radius) asks for one thing: "Digite seu CPF" then a full-width purple rgb(141,13,227) "Continuar" pill (999px). Local ID-first onboarding removes name/email/password friction.
- Display type is nuSansDisplay at 112px weight 600, line-height 0.94, -1.4px tracking, white over warm lifestyle video; sizes reach 156px further down. Huge, tight, friendly.
- Brand purple carries both CTAs and full-colour sections (purple rgb(141,13,227) / deep violet rgb(93,5,153)), balanced with a warm cream rgb(243,239,230). DNA found prefers-reduced-motion rules.
- Diversity pick: Latin America's largest digital bank; the page is Portuguese-first and built around local rails and IDs.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: markets with a national ID or phone-number identity (CPF, Aadhaar, phone-first in SE Asia/Africa) — use the identifier people already know by heart.
- Doesn't: markets where asking for a national ID up front feels invasive (most of the EU/US); there an email or phone is the equivalent.
