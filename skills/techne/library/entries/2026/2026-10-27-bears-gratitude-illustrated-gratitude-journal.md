## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use characters as the doorway into reflective content: the bears appear in 'delightfully unexpected places' so returning daily feels like visiting someone, not filling a form.

## Why it works
- Apple's citation: 'a warm and welcoming way to establish — and, just as crucially, maintain — a daily gratitude practice… the bears and other characters are super-huggable and turn up in delightfully unexpected places. But they also serve as a welcoming way into the content.'
- Made by an Australian husband-and-wife team; illustrations drawn by Nayomi Hettiarachchi during lockdown (per Apple Developer story summary).
- Listed as free in Health & Fitness with ~4.9 average (third-party tracker snapshot) [weak].

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: journaling, wellbeing and habit apps; empty states and daily prompts in any product where blank pages intimidate.
- Doesn't: professional or clinical contexts where cuteness undermines seriousness.
