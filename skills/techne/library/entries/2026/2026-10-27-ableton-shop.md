## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Split the shop's first screen into flat colour blocks, one per product, each with a name, one sentence and a 'Buy now ›' link — a storefront as a poster, not a catalogue.

## Why it works
- First screen below the sub-nav is a 50/50 split: peach rgb(255,195,165) block for "Live" and light grey for "Move", each with the product name in futura-pt 60px bold, a one-line description ("A portable tool for instinctive music making.") and an underlined "Buy now ›" text link.
- Sub-nav (Overview · Live · Push · Move · Educational offers · Max for Live · Merchandise) puts discounts for students on equal footing with products; right side carries "🔒 Secure shopping, learn more" — reassurance as a link to detail.
- Financing is stated plainly in a black band ("Rent-to-own Live Suite over 24 affordable monthly payments. Learn more").
- No border-radius, no shadows (DNA: none), one typeface (futura-pt) at 60/30/20/16/14; accent colours are flat (orange-red rgb(211,67,18) for the active "Shop" item, pure blue rgb(0,0,255) for links/"Try Live 12 for free").
- Consent dialog explains US data transfer and offers Accept (filled blue) vs Close (outlined, = deny) and More info; denial is explicit though not equal weight.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: small catalogues of flagship products; brands with a flat, confident colour system.
- Doesn't: large catalogues; flat colour blocks need real product imagery to avoid looking like placeholders.
