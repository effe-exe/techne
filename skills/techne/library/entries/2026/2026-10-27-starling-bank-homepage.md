## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let users attach their own photo and name to every savings pot ('For Chester £75.00') — a personal image turns an account balance into a goal people protect.

## Why it works
- Hero shows the real app's "Spaces" screen in a hand: "Total in Spending £720.00", then pots with user photos and names — a dog ("For Chester £75.00"), a beach ("Santorini Spending £345.00" with a 57% progress bar), a house — goals made emotional and specific.
- Headline "Good with money starts h…" in Avantt 48px weight 550, -1.44px (-3%) tracking; buttons and nav in CoFo Sans Semi-Mono, which gives the UI a ledger-like, numeric feel; deep aubergine ink rgb(50,30,55) on warm off-white rgb(248,245,240).
- Teal "Apply now" / "Learn more" blocks with 4px radii; content cards 16–24px radius.
- Cookie dialog: "Accept" and "Decline" are identical cyan buttons with "Manage cookies" as a link. Motion 0.2–0.3s ease-in-out; prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: savings, budgeting, goal-tracking features in any app (fitness, learning, travel funds).
- Doesn't: photo personalisation needs safe defaults and privacy (images stay private; never shown in shared views by default).
