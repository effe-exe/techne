## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Replace streak guilt with a personal 'activity path' band: judge today against your own recent range and celebrate rest, so the app motivates without punishing missed days.

## Why it works
- Headline frames the ethic: 'Focused on consistency and personal progress, not generic goals.' (Inter 700, 60px, -1.8px tracking) over a warm photo of runners in a field.
- In-app screen on the phone: orange blob mascot (Yorhart) running, greeting 'Hi Katarina, Just What You Needed', supportive copy ('…leaving you in a state of post-exercise vitality'), and a green band chart with a weekly line staying inside a personal range rather than a ring to close.
- Proof row: WINNER App of the Year, WINNER Apple Design Awards, FINALIST Apple Design Awards, each with laurels.
- iOS-native palette: rgb(242,242,247) grouped-background, 30px/24px cards, 100px pills, soft 0 10px 30px 0.1 shadow.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: habit, fitness, learning and finance apps that rely on streaks; any metric where 'more every day' is unhealthy.
- Doesn't: competitive training contexts where athletes want hard targets; compassion copy must not hide genuinely concerning data.
