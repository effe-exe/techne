## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Have a point of view and say it in the headline: 'Gmail, Outlook, and Apple got complacent…' — a named enemy plus 'No obligation, no CC required' under the CTA.

## Why it works
- Headline set in Really Sans Large 83px at weight 900 with a violet-to-pink gradient fill ('We finally fixed your email + calendar!'), followed by a 40px/850 black second line naming competitors.
- Three five-star quote snippets above the headline ('Finally a privacy-respecting inbox') front-load social proof (quotes are short and unattributed at the fold).
- One gradient pill CTA 'TRY HEY FREE FOR 30-DAYS' with italic reassurance 'No obligation, no CC required.' directly beneath; the product shot shows the hand-circled 'Imbox' concept.
- Rounded everything (pill nav bar, 43px card radii), warm off-white rgb(249,247,245) frame; 0.15s transitions; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Challenger products in entrenched categories; brands with a founder voice.
- Doesn't: Neutral/enterprise brands; gradient-filled heavy type reduces legibility at small sizes.
