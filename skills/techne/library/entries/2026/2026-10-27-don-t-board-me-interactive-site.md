## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A playful micro-interaction can replace the intro screen ('Bounce a ball to get to the site'), but it must be one tap and must state why it exists.

## Why it works
- The first screen is a game gate: 'BOUNCE A BALL TO GET TO THE SITE' in Bayon uppercase on flat pink, in red #E33529, with a yellow tennis ball and footer microcopy: 'You've landed on a dog walking site. Follow our rules to keep your dog happy.'
- Giant compressed display: h2 Bayon 293px at line-height 0.78 and -5.86px tracking, nav at 70px. Neue Montreal 14px carries the body.
- Earthy palette behind the pink: red #E33529, steel blue #2B6786, brown #693413, cream #F6F6ED; heavy use of circles (50% radius ×52).
- Behind the gate it is a simple pet-sitting site (pricing, services, a 4-step 'how it works'). Step 03 openly says Premium membership is required to message. No reduced-motion CSS.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Small playful brands, campaigns and event microsites where the delight is the message.
- Doesn't: Any visitor with a task: the gate adds a step before content, and it is a barrier for keyboard and assistive-tech users unless it has a skip.
