## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Treat a release archive as a physical shelf: every edition is a cover art 'record', the newest wears a 'NOW PLAYING' sticker, and a dated timeline below doubles as the index.

## Why it works
- Two lit 3D shelves hold nine edition covers, each with its own art direction (Everywhere, Renaissance, Horizons, Boring, Unified, Foundations, Imagine My Business, Built to Last, Connect to Consumer). The latest has a holographic 'NOW PLAYING' sticker.
- A timeline row under the shelves lists every edition as 'year / season / name' (2026 Spring Everywhere … 2022 Summer Connect to Consumer), so the playful shelf and the scannable index show the same data twice.
- Plain utility chrome around the spectacle: 'Shopify Editions' plus Search left, 'Shopify.com' and a black 'Start for free' pill right, the line 'Everything new across Shopify. Every six months.', and a mute button for sound.
- Inter Variable 11–14px only, grey #CDCDCD ground with soft vignettes, 4px radii, 0.15–0.5s material-style easing; WebGL.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Changelogs, release archives, annual reports, podcast or issue archives: any series with a cadence.
- Doesn't: Very long archives (shelves stop scaling past about 20 covers); the shelf needs cover art for every item.
