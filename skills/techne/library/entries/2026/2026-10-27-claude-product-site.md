## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put the real first step (sign-in) in the hero instead of a marketing CTA, and give an AI product a warm, humanist identity — serif display, paper background, documentary photography — rather than sci-fi gradients.

## Why it works
- Hero is a working sign-in card (Google / email, with the privacy acknowledgement inline) under a 56px anthropicSerif headline at weight 330 — the page is the front door to the product, not a brochure; 'Download desktop app' is a quiet secondary outline button.
- Warm off-white rgb(250,249,245) ground, near-black rgb(20,20,19) text and a single coral asterisk mark; no gradients, no glow — the AI category's default visual language is deliberately refused.
- Right half is documentary photography (a person at a paper-strewn desk) framed in a rounded 16-24px card, signalling 'thinking partner for real work' rather than robot imagery.
- Motion is restrained: 0.2s transitions dominate (92 uses) with expo-out cubic-bezier(0.19,1,0.22,1); prefers-reduced-motion CSS present; media covers only 9% of the page.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product where the primary conversion is 'start using it now' (consumer AI, note apps, tools with free tiers); brands that want trust and calm in an anxious category.
- Doesn't: enterprise products that need a demo/qualification step first; products whose value needs a product screenshot to be understood (the hero shows none).
