## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Trust badges and 'all data is safeguarded' copy cannot substitute for trustworthy behaviour — regulators judge the system (product safety, addictive design), not the reassurance strip.

## Why it works
- Visiting temu.com landed directly on 'Sign in / Register' — no browsing before identity. The page stacks reassurance: green padlock 'All data will be encrypted' in the header, 'All data is safeguarded' under the title, and a pale-green strip of four guarantees (Price adjustment within 30 days, Free shipping 'Excludes items shipped from sellers', Free returns up to 90 days, Delivery guarantee).
- Orange rgb(251,119,1) 26px-radius 'Continue' for email vs four outline social logins; footer pushes 'Affiliate & Influencer Program: Join to Earn' and a seller-recruitment card.
- Evidence: the European Commission opened DSA proceedings in Oct 2024 covering addictive design features among others, and on 28 Jul 2025 preliminarily found Temu breached its duty to assess illegal-product risks (mystery shopping found non-compliant baby toys and electronics) [strong — regulator, preliminary].

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Lesson applies: marketplaces and commerce apps — earn trust with verifiable product safety, honest shipping terms (note the fine-print exclusion inside 'Free shipping') and non-gamified browsing.
- Don't copy: forced sign-in before browsing; reassurance copy as a conversion device.
