## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use the wordmark as a window: a shaped mask cut through giant type reveals the 3D world behind it, so brand and content share one plane.

## Why it works
- The hero is the wordmark 'KPR' in ABC Whyte Plus at 159px, line-height 0.84, -12.7px tracking (about -8%), black on white. A slanted, chamfered slot cuts through the middle letter and shows a purple/cyan 3D render behind it.
- Secondary text is IBM Plex Mono at 9–14px and the only visible action is 'SIGN IN', so the brand world is all mood and no explanation on first view.
- Very long scroll story (page height 16,416px) with WebGL. Small radii (10px, with asymmetric 10px 0 0 corners on tabs). No reduced-motion CSS.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Entertainment, gaming and IP brands with a world to reveal; the masked-type window transfers to any launch hero.
- Doesn't: Anything that needs to say what the product is in one screen; web3/NFT context, so verify the product claims separately.
