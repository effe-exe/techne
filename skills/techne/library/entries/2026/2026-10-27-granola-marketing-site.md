## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Position against the category's annoyance in one line ('Without a meeting bot.') and make an AI product feel human with a bookish serif and paper-like textures instead of gradients.

## Why it works
- Headline in the serif 'quadrant' at 86px / 400 / line-height 0.93 (-1.7px tracking), stacked over four lines; UI text in 'melange' at 14px — a literary display face paired with a quiet grotesk.
- Two-line subhead does positioning work: 'Notes, actions and memory. Without a meeting bot.' — names the competitor pattern users dislike.
- Primary CTA is a dark olive pill rgb(91,111,0) 'Download for free ↓' with 'Available for macOS, Windows, iOS, Android' beneath; the palette (olive, chartreuse rgb(178,194,72), paper rgb(247,247,242)) avoids the default AI purple.
- Hero product window (a real note with 'Generate notes' button) is layered over halftone/print textures and a video-call tile — collage communicates 'notepad during a call' at a glance. Reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: AI and productivity tools in crowded categories; brands wanting warmth and credibility over futurism.
- Doesn't: Products whose audience reads serif display as old-fashioned or where density of info matters more than mood.
