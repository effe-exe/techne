## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Open on one specimen set large on a blank page with a single underlined 'Discover' link; put the tools (Typetester, theme toggle, search) in a compact pill bar.

## Why it works
- The fold is just 'PEIGNOT JUMBO' in black at display size on white, centred, with '→ Discover Peignot Jumbo' underlined below: one font, one action.
- Top-right pill bar on #F2F2F2 at 13.3px/500 (Catalogue active in red #ED0000, Custom Fonts, Typetester, light/dark toggle, search, Cart, overflow): the foundry's own Enduro face is used for 180 text nodes.
- Motion uses springy custom curves (cubic-bezier(0.16,1.08,0.38,0.98) on 41 nodes, 0.5s) — slight overshoot that feels tactile without being bouncy.
- Cookie dialog offers 'Accept all' and 'No, adjust' as equal-size pills (white vs black); refusal is not hidden, though there is no one-click reject.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Product launches where a single hero item should dominate; tool-heavy sites that need a compact utility bar.
- Doesn't: Catalogues where users arrive to compare many items at once.
