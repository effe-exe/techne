## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Borrow the grammar of engineering documents: 'FIG. 1' captions, leader lines from UI to object, mono uppercase labels — the page reads like a spec sheet, which is the brand promise.

## Why it works
- Hero product render (the rack) is captioned 'FIG. 1  OXIDE CLOUD COMPUTER' in a boxed mono label; a thin leader line connects a terminal panel to it.
- The terminal panel has CLI / API / CONSOLE tabs and shows 'oxide auth login · Enter code · LU8A-9AMP' — three ways to drive the product, one is chosen.
- Type pairing: GT America Mono (120 nodes) for labels, nav and buttons at 11–12px uppercase +0.64px; SuisseIntl 65/400 at -2.5% for the headline 'On-prem that feels like the public cloud'.
- Palette in oklch: near-black oklch(0.162 0.01 260), one green accent for logo and rack LEDs; 1–2px radii; cookie banner gives 'REJECT OPTIONAL' and 'ACCEPT ALL' equal styling.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: hardware, infrastructure, scientific and engineering products; any brand selling rigor.
- Doesn't: consumer or emotional products; mono-at-11px labels need contrast care.
