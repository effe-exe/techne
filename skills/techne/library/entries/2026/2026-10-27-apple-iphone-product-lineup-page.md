## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Open a multi-product family page with a row of thumbnail + name chips (with 'New' flags) so every model is one click away before any storytelling starts.

## Why it works
- 'iPhone' at 80/600, -1.2px tracking, then a horizontal chapter nav of 10 product thumbnails with 14–17px labels; new models carry a small orange 'New' under the name.
- Next band is a horizontal carousel of #F5F5F7 cards (28px radius) — 'iPhone Duo / Hello, hello.' with outline 'Buy' and filled blue 'Learn more' pills; the next card peeks at the right edge to signal scroll.
- DNA: SF Pro Text/Display; 50% radius on 31 icons, 28px cards, 980px pills; 144px section padding; 0.3s / 0.32s transitions with cubic-bezier(0,0,0.5,1).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any catalogue with 4–12 sibling products (plans, models, editions) where wayfinding beats persuasion.
- Doesn't: Single-product brands; with more than ~12 items the chip row becomes a scroller nobody finishes.
