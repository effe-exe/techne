## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Design the AI product page for its hardest audience first: a visible 'Switch color mode' and Language control before the nav, large high-contrast CTAs, and copy that frames AI as one option alongside human volunteers.

## Why it works
- Utility bar at the very top: 'Jump to Business Site', 'Switch color mode', 'Language' — accessibility settings are first-class, not buried in a footer.
- Hero card with 1px blue border: Inter 64/500 'Let's see the world together' in navy rgb(20,42,90) and a plain sentence: connects blind or low-vision users 'with volunteers and companies… through live video and AI' — AI is positioned beside, not instead of, people.
- Large amber rgb(237,159,16) 'Download App' pills with dark text (both in nav and hero); real photography of a blind user with a white cane.
- Cookie banner gives 'Reject All' the same filled button as 'Accept All'. Caveat: no prefers-reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product serving disabled, elderly or low-literacy users; AI products where human fallback builds trust.
- Doesn't: the craft is plain — study for inclusion and clarity, not visual originality.
