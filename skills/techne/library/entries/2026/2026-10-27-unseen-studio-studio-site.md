## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Offer 'Enter' and 'Enter without audio' as equal choices at the threshold: get consent for sound up front instead of autoplaying it or burying a mute.

## Why it works
- The entry screen is a quiet blush (#EFDED9) field with an animated letter of the U-N-S-E-E-N logotype and a 3-line studio description in Neue Montreal at about 14px, centred.
- The DOM offers two entry buttons, 'Enter' and 'Enter without audio', before the experience. Sound is opt-in at the threshold, with 'Toggle Sound' kept in the chrome afterwards.
- Inside, the instructions are explicit: 'Click & Hold', 'Drag to explore our world'. A numbered menu (01 Index, 02 Projects, 03 Contact, 04 World) keeps a flat route to the work.
- Motion: expo-out cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1) at 0.5–1s; WebGL; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any audio-led or immersive experience (music, games, brand worlds, museum pieces): the dual-entry pattern transfers directly.
- Doesn't: Utility sites, where a threshold screen is pure friction.
