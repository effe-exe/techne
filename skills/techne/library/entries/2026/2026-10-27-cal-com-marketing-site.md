## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put the product's core component, working, in the hero: Cal.com shows an actual booking widget (host, event, duration toggle 15m/30m/45m/1h, location, timezone, month grid) next to the headline.

## Why it works
- Headline 'The better way to schedule your meetings' in Cal Sans 64px/600, left column; right column is the booking UI with available days in grey tiles and the selected day in dark slate.
- Sign-up is two stacked full-width buttons — 'Sign up with Google' (dark) and 'Sign up with email >' (light) — plus 'No credit card required'.
- A release chip 'Cal.com launches v6.9.5' above the headline signals momentum; review badges (Trustpilot, Product Hunt, G2) sit under the widget.
- Neutral greys rgb(244,244,244) page / white card, 1px framed layout with '+' corner marks; no transitions detected and no reduced-motion CSS — a static, fast-feeling hero.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any product with a self-contained core widget (booking, pricing calculator, editor, search).
- Doesn't: Products whose value only appears after setup or with real data.
