## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- If two audiences need different sites, make the homepage an honest fork: one photo, two headlines, two buttons, nothing else.

## Why it works
- Single interior photograph (Aeron, Eames lounge) with two centred columns: 'Shop for home' (sub: 'The best in modern design, for your living space and home office.' → 'Online Store') and 'Plan for workplaces' (sub: 'Explore product models, material libraries, and more resources.' → 'Herman Miller Contract').
- Page is essentially one screen (1029px tall) — the fork is the whole homepage; wordmark bottom-centre.
- Caution: cookie bar has 'Accept All Cookies' (orange-red rgb(226,45,0)) and a 'Cookies Settings' link but no first-layer reject. DNA: Soehne 47.8/32, white 5px buttons on dark rgb(37,37,37).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: B2B + B2C brands, creator vs buyer marketplaces, any site serving two jobs.
- Doesn't: When most traffic is one audience, a fork adds a click for the majority.
