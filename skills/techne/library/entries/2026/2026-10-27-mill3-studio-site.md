## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Frame each case-study thumbnail in a mat colour sampled from the project itself, so the grid is varied but never clashes.

## Why it works
- Project cards sit in thick mats sampled from each client's brand: Moosehead's site in a deep green mat (rgb 20,42,31), Cob popcorn's pink site in a burgundy mat (rgb 59,0,23), on a black page.
- The wordmark 'MILL3' runs at viewport width in white Basel Grotesk, cropped by the bottom of the first screen, as a typographic floor.
- French-first nav at 14px uppercase ('PROJETS', 'À PROPOS', 'CONTACT') with a bracketed '[ EN ]' language switch. Basel Grotesk for 87 of the text nodes, sizes 14 / 24 / 48 / 64.
- In-out sine and cubic-out easings at 0.25–1.05s; WebGL; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any multi-brand grid (portfolios, marketplaces, app directories, case-study indexes).
- Doesn't: Grids of near-identical items, where there is no brand colour to sample.
