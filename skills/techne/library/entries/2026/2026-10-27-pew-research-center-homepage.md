## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put 'Our Methods' in the primary navigation and label every item by evidence type (FACT SHEET, REPORT, FEATURE) — credibility is information architecture.

## Why it works
- Primary nav: RESEARCH TOPICS / PUBLICATIONS / OUR METHODS / SHORT READS / TOOLS & DATASETS / EXPERTS / ABOUT US in franklin-gothic caps with 1.2px tracking.
- Each card has 'TYPE | DATE' meta (FACT SHEET | OCT 26, 2026) above an abril-text serif headline (28px/700 lead).
- Utility strip 'NUMBERS, FACTS AND TRENDS SHAPING YOUR WORLD' plus a 'CONTACTED BY US?' link that answers survey recipients' trust question.
- Consent panel stacks 'Accept All Cookies' and 'Accept only necessary cookies' as equal buttons.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: research, analytics, health and finance information where provenance matters.
- Doesn't: stock-photo-heavy cards make it feel generic.
