## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use a light, tightly tracked editorial serif on warm cream to make a gadget feel like jewellery, and surface the payment fact that matters to this buyer (HSA/FSA eligibility) in the top bar.

## Why it works
- Headline "Wear your best life" in Editorial New at 80px weight 300, -4px (-5%) tracking, lh 1.1, warm grey-brown ink rgb(74,71,65) on cream rgb(247,241,232); AkkuratLL for body (14–20px) and UI. Serif = jewellery, sans = instrument.
- Single photographic gesture: a hand wearing the ring, cropped so the product sits at the optical centre-right; one blue pill "Shop Now" CTA.
- The top bar states a buyer-relevant payment fact ("Use your HSA/FSA funds on Oura") instead of a discount code.
- Consent banner offers "Accept All Cookies" and "Accept Necessary" as identical filled blue pills plus an outlined "Manage" — the privacy-preserving choice is one click and equal weight. Motion 0.15–0.5s standard easing; prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: wearables, beauty, home goods — any tech product that must feel personal; health-adjacent products where payment eligibility is a real decision factor.
- Doesn't: the low-contrast warm-grey-on-cream text needs checking at small sizes.
