## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A one-size type system (every label at 11px) forces hierarchy to come from position, image scale and motion: an extreme constraint used as identity.

## Why it works
- The capture landed mid-preloader: a monochrome collage of project thumbnails nesting into a small square at the centre of a black screen, with a bare counter ('67') at the right edge.
- Every one of the 258 text nodes is the custom 'Obys' face at 11px/1.2. Hierarchy comes entirely from layout, imagery and motion.
- The HTML index lists 17 numbered projects, each tagged with sector and discipline ('Fashion · Web Design/Dev · 02'), so the work is scannable as data once loaded.
- Long expo-out motion: cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1) at 0.8–1.6s. WebGL present, no reduced-motion CSS.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Portfolios and fashion or culture sites whose imagery carries the hierarchy; numbered sector/discipline tags transfer to any case-study index.
- Doesn't: Reading-heavy or accessibility-critical products (11px everywhere fails comfortable reading), and anything where the first second matters (the preloader comes first).
