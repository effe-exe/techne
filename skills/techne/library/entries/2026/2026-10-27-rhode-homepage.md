## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A three-word positioning line ('Edited, efficacious, intentional.') plus one outline CTA over a black-and-white portrait — restraint is the brand.

## Why it works
- Inset rounded hero card with a grayscale close-up portrait; caps nav around a centred lowercase 'rhode' wordmark.
- Positioning line at ~36px in 'Swiss' plus a single outline pill 'RHODE ROUTINE'; promo is a soft pill bar ('FREE US SHIPPING ON ORDERS OVER $45').
- DNA: Swiss + Rektorat Heavy; warm grey rgb(103,100,94) text, rgb(241,240,237) surfaces; 90px pills; an accessibility widget tab on the right edge.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Small-catalogue DTC brands with a clear point of view.
- Doesn't: Large catalogues; the hero gives no product information.
