## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Answer 'where can I use it?' in the CTA's caption with personality ('Available on phone, tablet, watch, desktop, and the web (yep, all of them)').

## Why it works
- Single red CTA rgb(228,66,50) 'Start for free' with a subtle 3D lip/shine (darker bottom edge) — a tactile, pressable button rather than a flat rectangle.
- Headline 'The task system you'll actually stick with' (Graphik 55px/600) addresses abandonment, the real objection for to-do apps.
- Warm off-white background oklch(0.9945 0.0017 67.8) and warm near-black text rgb(37,34,30) instead of pure greys; peach gradient demo panel shows three capture modes 'Write / Attach / Speak'.
- Cookie notice offers 'Manage Preferences' as its action (no pre-checked accept-all); 0.15s transitions with cubic-bezier(0.2,0.8,0.2,1); reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Cross-platform consumer/prosumer tools; anyone whose main objection is 'I tried this before and quit'.
- Doesn't: Enterprise sites where playful parenthetical copy feels off-brand.
