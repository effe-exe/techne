## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- An enterprise system can open with a single typographic statement in its own brand face, then stay rigorously utilitarian in the docs.

## Why it works
- Hero is a full-bleed IBM blue rgb(0,45,156) panel with 'Let's make together' in IBM Plex Serif at 187px weight 200 — the only decoration on the page.
- Left nav is minimal and functional: Find, Getting started, Building blocks, Building experiences, Support, Feedback.
- IBM Plex Sans 14–20 for UI, interactive blue rgb(15,98,254), grey rgb(244,244,244) background; 8px is the single radius used; Carbon's productive easing cubic-bezier(0.2,0,0.38,0.9) at 70–160ms.
- Above the fold says almost nothing about what Carbon is — the statement wins over orientation.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: enterprise/data-dense products; teams needing a strict grid and token set.
- Doesn't: homepage orientation — first-time visitors must scroll to learn what the system offers.
