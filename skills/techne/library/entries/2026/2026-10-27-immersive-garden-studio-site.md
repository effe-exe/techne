## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Luxury register through restraint: one serif at essentially one size on a grey paper ground, with a numbered preloader that frames waiting as ceremony.

## Why it works
- The preloader is a single row: shield monogram and spaced small-caps 'IMMERSIVE GARDEN', then 'Innovative digital experiences studio' in PSTimes serif, then a grey counter ('55'), on #E8E8E8 with near-black #030303 text.
- The type system is essentially one serif at 36px (18 of 19 text nodes), 44px for the CTA.
- The HTML case list gives each project one sentence ('Explore our collaboration with Louis Vuitton on VIA…'), so clients read as stories rather than logos.
- Built on Nuxt with Lenis smooth scroll. Transitions run up to 1.9s ease-in-out. WebGL present, no reduced-motion CSS.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Luxury, fashion, wine and culture brands; any site where slowness is part of the brand promise.
- Doesn't: Utility or task flows; the long preloader and smooth-scroll hijack cost time and control.
