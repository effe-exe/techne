## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let 3D objects intrude into the headline's word spaces: gaps in the type become stage space, tying the hero copy and the WebGL scene into one composition.

## Why it works
- 'WHERE YOUR STORY MEETS CREATIVE TECHNOLOGY' in NeueMachina at 60px uppercase is set with deliberately irregular word gaps; glass/chrome 3D slabs float through the gaps and over letters.
- The ground is a periwinkle #C9D2E7 with a soft pink radial glow; the positioning line ('Independent partner for enterprise teams building smarter, AI-ready digital experiences') sits small at bottom-right in NeueRoman 16px.
- A plain uppercase 14px nav (WORK, OUR STORY, LABS, INSIGHTS, CONNECT) and a 'SCROLL' cue; 100px pills; prefers-reduced-motion honoured; 24,165px page.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Hero sections that pair a statement with a 3D or product object; agency and creative-tech positioning.
- Doesn't: Copy that must be read quickly: the irregular spacing slows reading.
