## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Answer the visitor's first question in the hero — 'The museum is open 10:30 a.m.–5:30 p.m. today.' — right next to the welcome, before any programming.

## Why it works
- Live opening-hours sentence sits on the same baseline as the 60px 'Welcome' headline (MoMA Sans 900, -1.2px tracking, line-height 1.0) with a black 'Plan your visit' button directly under it.
- Single proprietary family (MoMA Sans, 88 uses) at 60/40/30/22/18, zero border-radius anywhere — the institution's type does all the branding.
- Utility actions ranked by colour: cyan rgb(0,183,242) 'Tickets', grey 'Donate', text 'Membership'.
- A full-width orange service alert (gallery closures with dates) is dismissible and written in plain sentences, not a marketing banner.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any venue, clinic, shop or service with opening hours or availability — put today's status in the hero.
- Doesn't: purely online products.
