## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let a single studio photograph on black carry a premium product; keep the chrome white, thin and quiet so the object is the only contrast.

## Why it works
- White header with four text links left, the red-dot logo centred and a single outline pill 'Online Store' right — commerce is one clear exit, not a mega-menu.
- 'Leica Camera' set at 54px in Outfit, then a full-bleed black studio shot of the Q3 43 Formula 1 Edition with the product name overlaid in white.
- DNA: one family (Outfit); radii 4px (cards), 8px, 24px; 144 transitions on cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1); max-width 1920px.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Premium physical products whose photography is good enough to stand alone.
- Doesn't: Products that need explanation before desire; a photo-only hero says nothing about what the camera does.
