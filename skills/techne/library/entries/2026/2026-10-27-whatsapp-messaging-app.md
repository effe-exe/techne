## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use the product's own UI atoms (chat bubbles, double ticks, reactions) as the hero illustration, and make sign-up a single field.

## Why it works
- Hero photo in a 28px-rounded frame on a warm cream rgb(252,245,235) page; 'Message privately' in WhatsApp Sans Var 80/400 (line-height 1.0); two real chat bubbles ('Still on for studying?' with blue double ticks 11:53, 'Ready when you are!' with 👍❤️ reactions) float over the photo.
- 'Create an account' card is one field — flag + '+1' phone number — and a 'Get started' button that stays disabled until input; 'Already have an account? Log in' beneath.
- Nav: Features / Privacy / Blog / Apps — Privacy is a top-level item; green 'Download' pill vs outline 'Log In'. A dismissible mint rgb(230,255,218) bar announces browser calling.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: communication and social apps; any product whose UI atoms are iconic enough to illustrate it.
- Doesn't: products with no recognisable micro-UI; phone-number identity is a privacy trade-off not every product should copy.
