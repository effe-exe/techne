## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Shrink the creative canvas to one cosy, bounded box (a single isometric room) so first-time creators finish something in minutes and everything stays remixable.

## Why it works
- Hero shows three devices each with an isometric voxel room (lavender bedroom with yellow rug on an iMac, forest piano diorama on iPad, Explore feed on iPhone) — the 'room' unit is the whole product idea.
- Headline uses weight as hierarchy: 'Create & browse' and 'on iOS and web' in HelveticaNeue Light grey, 'interactive rooms' in regular black, ~58px; single black 'Open on Web' button — try before install.
- A 4px rainbow gradient bar across the top edge is the only colour in the chrome; the page is otherwise grey rgb(240,240,240) with text rgb(51,51,51), letting user content carry colour.
- Consent banner offers three equal-weight buttons — Customize, Reject non-essential, Accept all — honest consent. DNA: 0.3s ease, reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creation tools for novices (website builders, game makers, slide makers): fixed frame + good defaults + remix of others' work as the learning path.
- Doesn't: professional tools where users need an unbounded canvas and precise units.
