## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn a feature list into a sentence: 'All your [notes] [bookmarks] [inspiration] [articles] and [images] in one single, private place' — each noun is a coloured outlined pill.

## Why it works
- Display headline 'Remember everything. Organize nothing.' at ~140px with -8.4px tracking and line-height 0.85 (serif Louize in the font stack), centered and overlapping — huge, confident, almost poster-like.
- Inline pills in five hues (orange, pink, light blue, red, yellow), outlined rather than filled, keep the sentence readable while categorising the content types.
- Nav uses coloured dots before 'What / Why / How' and a green dot before 'Log in' — tiny semantic colour cues instead of icons; single orange-red CTA rgb(255,89,36) 'Sign up'.
- Platform pills (iPhone app, Browser Extension, Android app) as equal-weight secondary actions. Long 0.7s expo-out transitions (cubic-bezier(0.19,1,0.22,1)); no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Consumer and prosumer tools with several content types; any hero that would otherwise be a bullet list.
- Doesn't: Dense B2B products with many features (the sentence breaks past ~5 tokens); the extreme negative tracking needs a well-spaced display face.
