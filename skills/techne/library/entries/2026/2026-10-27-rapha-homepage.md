## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Pair a shouting condensed caps headline with a bookish serif for everything else — the sans carries attitude, the serif carries trust and the small print.

## Why it works
- Hero line "AGAINST BETTER JUDGEMENT" in raphaSansCondensed 64px bold caps (lh 0.97) over a dark close-up rider video, with a square white "SHOP NOW" button; nav in the same condensed caps.
- Everything else — body, buttons, form labels, the cookie and region dialogs — is set in raphaSerifText (16px, lh 1.63), giving the commerce plumbing an editorial, magazine voice.
- Motion controls are respected: the top marquee ticker (NEW IN · JOIN THE RCC · BUNDLE & SAVE UP TO 20%) and the hero video both have visible pause buttons; DNA found prefers-reduced-motion rules.
- Region/currency confirmation uses outlined-floating-label selects and equal-size CANCEL (outlined) / CONFIRM (filled) buttons.
- Caveat: first visit stacks a region modal, a cookie card (no first-layer reject) and a chat bubble over the hero; clarity suffers until dismissed.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: lifestyle/performance brands with a strong editorial voice; any page with autoplaying motion (copy the pause controls).
- Doesn't: the overlay stack on first load is a lesson in what not to do; condensed caps are hard to read beyond a few words.
