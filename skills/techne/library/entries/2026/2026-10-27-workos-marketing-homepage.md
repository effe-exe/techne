## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Show a B2B feature set as a stack of switches with status badges (Enabled / Ready) — the buyer reads 'what I get' as 'what I can turn on'.

## Why it works
- Right side of hero: a vertical stack of cards each with a toggle — Audit Logs (Enabled), Enterprise SSO (Enabled), SCIM (Ready), RBAC, Connectors — fading top and bottom, over a soft violet line pattern.
- H1 'Your app, Enterprise Ready.' in Untitled Sans 80/500 at -5.6px (-7%) tracking; second line is a violet→blue gradient fill.
- Event strip below: keynote video thumbnail 'Here's what we announced at init()' plus three product chips (Passport, Atlas, Airlock) with glossy app-icon tiles.
- Grey rgb(101,107,138) body at 19/1.6; violet pill 'Get started' with soft glow; 'Trusted by' logos in grey tiles; 120px section rhythm. No reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: B2B add-on products, settings/plan pages, any 'feature unlock' story.
- Doesn't: products whose value isn't modular; toggles imply instant enablement — don't use if setup is long.
