## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Define the product as an open, editable list ('Are.na is a. … b. … c. +') — the empty third item invites the reader to finish the sentence, which is exactly what the product does.

## Why it works
- Hero is a lettered list in a single face (areal, 135 uses) at 19.2px/1.45 — no display type, no imagery; item 'c.' is a bare '+' button.
- Live age counter in bold ('For the last 15 years and 79 days') turns longevity into trust copy for an anti-algorithmic platform.
- Two CTAs differ only in fill: navy rgb(0,7,95) 'Sign up' vs grey rgb(247,247,247) 'Download the app', both 3px radius — the whole page uses 3px (72 uses).
- 'How it works' pairs a product screenshot with a vertical stepper (Capture / Arrange / Search) whose inactive steps are greyed — progressive disclosure without a carousel.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: tools for thought, research, note-taking, any product whose value is structure rather than spectacle.
- Doesn't: products that need emotional or visual persuasion on first view.
