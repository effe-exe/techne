## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put the tester controls (your text, size slider, sample presets, alignment, theme) above the catalogue so browsing is always in the user's own words and size.

## Why it works
- The header is a grid of tab cells with counts baked in (Fonts 100 in an inverted black cell, Pairs 59, Licenses) and a 'No styles selected' status cell: the size of each section and the user's selection state are always visible.
- The control panel sits above results: Search, 'Your Text' input, Categories/Properties/Personality dropdowns, a 120px size slider, sample presets (Cities, Excerpts, Names), three alignment toggles, two theme swatches and 'Reset All'.
- Each list row is a 1px-ruled card with the family name in itself at the chosen size ('Satoshi'), plus metadata in light grey: '10 styles · Variable · Closed Source' and 'Designed by Indian Type Foundry'.
- Warm cream ground #FFFFE3 with #10100E text and #C0C0AB secondaries; Passenger Sans at only 12/16px for UI; 9999px radius on 97 elements; transitions 0.15s cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any catalogue of previewable items (fonts, icons, colour palettes, templates, product configurators) where users judge by trying their own content.
- Doesn't: Catalogues whose items cannot be rendered with user input.
