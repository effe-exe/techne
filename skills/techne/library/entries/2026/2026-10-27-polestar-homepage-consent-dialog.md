## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Never pre-enable optional tracking toggles: if 'Allow selected' with untouched defaults equals 'Accept all', the choice is fake.

## Why it works
- On first load the 'Welcome' dialog lists 'Performance' and 'Targeting and Advertising' with orange toggles already switched ON; the buttons are 'Allow selected', 'Do not sell my personal data' and 'Accept all', so the default 'selection' is everything.
- This stacks on top of a black promo bar ('Get up to $25,000 off…') and a lease offer in the hero — three layers before the product is visible.
- The underlying craft is good and worth separating from the lesson: single custom face Polestar Unica (30/400, -1.2px; display at 104–110px), square corners, video hero with a pause ring bottom-right.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Lesson for any consent UI: defaults off, reject as easy as accept (compare Porsche, B&O, Oatly in this library).
- Doesn't: Not a reference for the brand's typography or product photography, which are strong.
