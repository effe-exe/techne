## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give feelings a vocabulary and a shape: colour-coded characters (yellow, green, blue, red) map emotional quadrants so naming a feeling is a tap, not an essay.

## Why it works
- Fold is four soft-edged gradient characters — yellow, green, blue, red — standing in a row above 'A journal for your wellbeing' in Tiempos 60px on black; the four colours are the app's emotion model made visible.
- Single outlined CTA 'Download the app →' (6px radius) and a three-link nav (Practitioners | What people say | Home) — no pricing or upsell at the fold (developer listed by Apple as The How We Feel Project, Inc.).
- Cookie banner gives equal visual weight to 'Use Strictly Necessary Only', 'Manage Preferences' and 'Accept All Cookies'.
- Type pairing: Tiempos serif for warmth and the wordmark, Sohne for UI text; DNA found 0.2s ease-in-out transitions.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: mental-health, journaling, feedback and survey products where users struggle to articulate state; any taxonomy that benefits from a visual map.
- Doesn't: clinical diagnostic tools — playful characters must not trivialise risk; include crisis resources.

## Style DNA (optional, compact)
type: Tiempos 60/40 regular, Sohne 15 UI · color: black bg, white text, four emotion gradients (yellow, green, blue, red) · shape: 6px buttons, 60px rounded art · motion: 0.2s ease-in-out; no reduced-motion CSS
