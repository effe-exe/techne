## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Sell craft by showing the real product at full fidelity: the hero is a live-looking app window, not an illustration, and the marketing chrome borrows the product's own type scale and greys.

## Why it works
- Hero headline Inter Variable 64px / weight 510 / line-height 1.0 / -1.4px tracking (h2 48px/510, -1.06px), left-aligned over near-black rgb(8,9,10); body copy drops to 15px/1.6 with a 67ch measure.
- The fold is dominated by a full product window (issue view, sidebar, agent chat panel) rendered in the same 12-13px UI sizes that dominate the page (12px x121, 13px x55) — the marketing page and the app share one type system.
- Palette is almost entirely greys: text steps rgb(247,248,248) > rgb(208,214,224) > rgb(138,143,152) > rgb(98,102,109); surfaces use translucent white (0.02/0.08 alpha) rather than new hues. Colour is reserved for status icons in the product shot.
- Motion is short and consistent: 0.16s transitions with cubic-bezier(0.25,0.46,0.45,0.94); a prefers-reduced-motion stylesheet is present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: B2B tools whose product UI is itself the selling point; dense dark tools; any team that wants marketing and product to feel like one system.
- Doesn't: Products whose UI is not yet polished (a full-fidelity screenshot exposes it) or consumer audiences who need warmth/illustration; widely imitated, so copying the look reads as derivative.
