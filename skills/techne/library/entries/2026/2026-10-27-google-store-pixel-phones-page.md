## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Tint the whole hero card with the product's own colourway so photo and background read as one object.

## Why it works
- Hero is an inset card (~28px radius) whose warm pink gradient matches the phone's finish; 'Google Pixel' at ~76px in Google Sans Flex sits bottom-left.
- Two pills: white 'Explore the lineup' and outline 'Watch the film' with a play icon; blue 'Sign in' pill in nav.
- Caution: the subline 'Ask more of your phone.' is near-white on light pink — low contrast. DNA: Google Sans Flex/Sans/Text; 100px pills on 38 elements, 28px cards on 26.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Colour-variant products; seasonal campaigns that recolour one layout.
- Doesn't: Low-contrast text over gradients; check the subline.
