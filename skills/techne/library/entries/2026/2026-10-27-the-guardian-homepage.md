## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Colour-code content pillars (news red, culture, opinion…) inside a strictly typographic system so readers parse genre before reading a word.

## Why it works
- Pillar nav (News / Opinion / Sport / Culture / Lifestyle) set large in GH Guardian Headline on navy; the active pillar gets a red underline and story kickers inherit pillar colour (red 'World news' label).
- Headline family dominates (99 uses, 24–28px/700, line-height 1.15); GuardianTextSans for UI (17/700 buttons with 36px radius).
- Reader-funding ask is a permanent, polite header slot ('Support the Guardian — Fund independent journalism with $15 per month') rather than a paywall interrupt.
- Edition switcher plus an inline note ('You are viewing the International homepage — View the US homepage') makes localisation explicit.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: multi-section publications, marketplaces or content apps with distinct genres.
- Doesn't: single-topic products.
