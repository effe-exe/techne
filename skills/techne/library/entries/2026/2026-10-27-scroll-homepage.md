## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Never stack asks: a membership banner, an accept-only cookie bar and an app-install card on one fold bury the journalism the reader came for — one ask per screen, consent first.

## Why it works
- At 1440×900 the fold held three simultaneous asks: a full-width 'We need your help — Subscribe for $120/year' banner, a cookie bar, and a 'Take Scroll With You' app card with store badges covering the Editor's Picks.
- Cookie bar offers only 'Accept' and states that 'closing this message box or continuing to use our site' counts as agreement — implied consent with no reject.
- Lead story image is cut off by the stacked overlays; Noto Serif/Sans 13–28px reading type underneath is perfectly serviceable.
- No reduced-motion CSS found.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Lesson applies: any ad- or membership-funded site. The membership pitch itself (clear price, clear purpose) is fine — the stacking and consent are the problem.
