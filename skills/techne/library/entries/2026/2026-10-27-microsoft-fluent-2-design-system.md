## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Treat AI and content as first-class system sections: Fluent's nav puts 'Working with AI' and 'Content engineering' beside Design language and Web tokens.

## Why it works
- Left nav: Get started, Design language, UX frameworks and guidelines, Working with AI, Content engineering, Web tokens, Web › Components — AI and content sit at the same level as tokens.
- Hero: 'WELCOME TO' eyebrow, 'Fluent 2' in Segoe UI ~150px bold, tagline 'Let the {ideas} flow' with the braces in mono magenta; a refractive glass prism render to the right.
- Two large entry cards split the audience: Design (Figma UI kits) vs Develop (installation and tooling).
- Segoe UI throughout (H1 40/600 at -4% on inner pages), greys rgb(36,36,36)→rgb(97,97,97); radii 2–8px controls, 22px cards; Search '/' shortcut; reduced-motion honored.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: systems that need explicit guidance for AI features and UX writing.
- Doesn't: the glass prism hero is decorative and Microsoft-specific.
