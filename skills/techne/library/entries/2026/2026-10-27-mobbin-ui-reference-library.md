## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Quantify the catalogue and its freshness in one line ('621,500 shipped screens, new every week') — size plus cadence is the value proposition of a reference library.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Where [icon] teams and agents [icon] find designs [icon] that work' at 80px Saans weight 652 (tracking -0.6px), with three real app icons inline (Arc-style, a red 'A', Headspace-style orange face) — the words are punctuated by the content itself.
- Grey 20px subline does the selling with numbers: 'The design reference library for apps and sites. 621,500 shipped screens, new every week.'
- Black 'Join for free' and grey 'See plans →' pills; nav leads with 'MCP' (agent access) before Customers and Pricing. The cookie toast gives 'Accept' and 'Deny' as equal pills.
- Reduced-motion CSS present; easings cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1) and expo-out (0.19,1,0.22,1) at 1s for reveals.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Libraries, marketplaces, datasets, template stores — any product where breadth and freshness are the value.
- Doesn't: Products whose numbers are small or vanity metrics; precise counts invite scrutiny.
