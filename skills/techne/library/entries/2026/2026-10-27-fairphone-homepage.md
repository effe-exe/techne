## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Mark the product name with a highlighter block in the brand accent — the one coloured word tells you what's new without a badge.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Introducing the Fairbuds 2' in Bricolage Grotesque (40–64/600) on deep green rgb(0,67,61), with only 'Fairbuds 2' on a lime rgb(216,255,79) highlighter block.
- Consent: 'Customize Settings', 'Decline Optional', 'Accept All' are identical outline buttons; copy ends 'it's your data, your choice.' A region popover explains US shipping limits ('every US state except Alaska and Hawaii').
- A floating accessibility-tools button sits bottom-right next to chat; radii 24/40px; transitions 0.5–0.8s cubic-bezier(0.22,1,…).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Mission-led brands; launch announcements inside a stable layout.
- Doesn't: Highlight blocks lose meaning if more than one phrase per screen gets them.
