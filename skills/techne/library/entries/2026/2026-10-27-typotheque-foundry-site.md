## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Annotate the hero specimen with its live variable-axis values (Weight 894, Width 100, Rounding 0, Slant 0) so the product's flexibility is explained by a diagram, not a paragraph.

## Why it works
- The hero sets 'Zed' at ~190px in white over a macro photo of a red cactus, with four thin vertical slider lines labelled Weight 894, Width 100, Rounding 0, Slant 0: the variable axes become an info-graphic on top of the mood image.
- Below, a four-up row of 24px-radius category cards in flat brand colours (orange All Fonts, black Global Fonts with a colour wheel, pink Variable Fonts with outline 'A' overlays, cyan Smart Fonts): each concept gets a one-glyph diagram.
- UI in the foundry's own 'November' at 13/16/20/40px; round 9999px pill buttons on #F8F8F8 for Log In and search; orange #FF6600-range logo block top left.
- 'Global Fonts' as a top-level category reflects the foundry's multi-script catalogue — a non-Latin-first framing most foundry sites lack.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any configurable product (variable fonts, parametric hardware, pricing sliders) where showing current parameter values teaches the model.
- Doesn't: Products with no meaningful continuous parameters; fake sliders would be decoration.
