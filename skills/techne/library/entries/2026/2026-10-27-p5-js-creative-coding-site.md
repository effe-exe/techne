## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put Language and Accessibility controls in the top bar, the same size and colour as Search, and write the intro as an invitation that names who is welcome.

## Why it works
- The top bar has three equal pink #F1678E pill controls: 'English' (language), 'Accessibility' (settings dropdown) and 'Search' — accessibility is a peer of search, not a footer link.
- Intro copy at 30px National Park: 'p5.js is a friendly tool for learning to code and make art. It is a free and open-source JavaScript library built by an inclusive, nurturing community. p5.js welcomes artists, designers, beginners, educators, and anyone else!'
- Left sidebar in 20px (Reference, Tutorials, Examples, Contribute, Community, About) with outlined 'Start Coding' and 'Donate' pills; a big pink asterisk logo mark; an interactive video of diverse community members saying 'Hello, p5.js!'.
- Banners are dismissible (× on 'Processing is turning 25!' in blue #1E32AA and 'Looking for p5.js v1 reference? Find it here!'); reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Education, open-source and community products; any site serving beginners or a global, diverse audience.
- Doesn't: Expert tools where a welcoming tone would feel patronising.
