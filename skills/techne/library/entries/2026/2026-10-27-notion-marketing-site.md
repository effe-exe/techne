## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Pair a very tight, heavy headline with hand-drawn character illustration: the type sells seriousness, the doodles sell approachability.

## Why it works
- Centered h1 in NotionInter at 96px / 600 / line-height 1.04 with -4.6px tracking — extremely tight — with an animated pill (peach background, orange dot, word 'Create') embedded inline in the headline.
- Exactly one accent colour for action: primary CTA rgb(0,117,222) blue with white text, 8px radius; the secondary 'Request a demo' is the same blue on a pale-blue tint, so hierarchy is by fill not hue.
- Hero screenshot is a real Notion workspace (sidebar, board view with To-do/In progress/In review/Complete columns) flanked by black line-drawn characters; a customer logo strip pins the bottom of the fold.
- Warm off-white surfaces rgb(249,249,248)/rgb(246,245,244) instead of grey; 0.2s transitions dominate; reduced-motion CSS present; media covers 73% of page area.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Horizontal tools with broad, non-technical audiences; any product that needs to feel both capable and friendly.
- Doesn't: Highly regulated or enterprise-only tools where whimsy undercuts trust; the -4.6px tracking only works at very large sizes.
