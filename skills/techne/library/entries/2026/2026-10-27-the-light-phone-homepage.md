## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Sell less-ness with less: one slow ambient video, two words of nav ('menu', 'shop') and a single customer quote.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed, slow cloud/sky video; nav is a lowercase futura-pt wordmark, 'menu' and a white box 'shop' — nothing else on the first screen but a small centred testimonial with a dot pager.
- Cookie bar reads 'The internet can be sketchy. view our cookie policy' with one 'acknowledged' button (no reject — caution).
- DNA: futura-pt + AkkuratLL; 0.5s transitions with cubic-bezier(0.25,0.2,0,1) on 76 elements; essentially no radii.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Products whose value proposition is reduction (digital wellbeing, focus tools).
- Doesn't: Anything needing explanation above the fold; the first screen says almost nothing about the product.
