## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Pair the purchase CTA with explicit risk reversal ('30-Day Money-Back Guarantee') and a named, photographed testimonial beside the demo instead of anonymous stars.

## Why it works
- Two-line contrast headline 'There are average capture apps. / And there's CleanShot.' in Google Sans Flex 60px/600 at -2% tracking, with 'apps.' and the second line in deep blue.
- Primary 'Get CleanShot for Mac' (blue) + secondary 'How it works' (outlined, play icon) + a '30-Day Money-Back Guarantee >' link — purchase, learn and reassurance in one cluster.
- Demo video card sits next to a quote card attributed to a named person with photo and carousel arrows.
- Soft blue radial glow on white; 14px button radius, 20px cards; 0.1-0.2s transitions; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Paid-upfront software and any purchase decision where refund policy is a real differentiator.
- Doesn't: Free/freemium products (no risk to reverse); the 'average X vs us' headline is a common formula.
