## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Infrastructure doesn't have to look like infrastructure: hand-drawn flying servers with bird beaks and a single-word serif headline ('Launch') make a cloud platform feel joyful.

## Why it works
- Hero is one word, 'Launch', in Mackinac serif 64/500 at -4.5% tracking, underlined by a drawn violet stroke ending in a dot; sub-line 'The platform for devs and agents who just want to ship.'
- Right half: textured, hand-drawn violet server racks with wings and beaks flying among birds over a pastel sky with brush-stroke ribbons.
- Pill nav in a translucent capsule (Products, Docs, Customers, Pricing, Articles, Community, Status); violet rgb(124,58,237) 'Deploy your app' pill plus a ghost 'Onboard your agent' with copy icon.
- Fricolage Grotesque UI 14.5–19; text is tinted violet rgba(40,25,80,0.75) rather than grey; 128px section padding (6×); reduced-motion honored.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: technical products wanting warmth; brands with an in-house illustrator.
- Doesn't: enterprise sales pages where whimsy may undercut perceived reliability.
