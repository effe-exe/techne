## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Colour-code data columns by type (text vs number) with small 'ABC'/'123' badges, and reuse those colours in the headline highlights so the brand teaches the product's model.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Where data meets storytelling' at 66.5px Canva Sans (tracking -1.33px) with 'data' on a pale-blue highlighter block in blue #135AE1 and 'storytelling' on a lilac block in purple #9852D9: two words, two colours, two halves of the product.
- The hero visual is the real editor: a 'My visualisation · by Jane Mapley' header with Share / Create a story / Export & publish, Preview vs Data tabs, and a spreadsheet whose columns are tinted pink (text, 'ABC' badge) or purple (numeric, '123' badge).
- Clear CTA pair: filled blue 'Get started now' and outlined 'Talk to us', both 19px/700 with 3px radius; plain value line 'No coding required.'
- Respects prefers-reduced-motion (CSS detected); transitions 0.2–0.3s ease.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Data tools, spreadsheets, form builders: any product whose core model (types, roles, states) can be colour-coded and echoed in marketing.
- Doesn't: Products without a stable visual grammar to echo.
