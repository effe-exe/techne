## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the people a first-class navigation item ('Designers' next to 'Typefaces') when a marketplace sells many makers' work under one brand.

## Why it works
- Top nav in a mono-style face at 14.6px on #EAEAEC: Typefaces, Designers, Articles, EULA, Customs, Fonts In Use — designers, licence and real-world uses all sit at the same level as the catalogue.
- The hero is a black 24px-radius panel with 'Castillon' in white at display size and 1–5 hollow/filled dot pagination; below, a two-up grid of black specimen cards with a yellow 'Updated' tag.
- Palette is almost purely achromatic (black panels, #EAEAEC chrome, greys #CCC/#808080 for inactive text) so every specimen reads in the same light; one easing, cubic-bezier(0.52,0.07,0.2,0.99), for slides.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Marketplaces and collectives (type, music, illustration, templates) where makers' reputations sell the work.
- Doesn't: Single-author brands; a 'Designers' index of one adds nothing.
