## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the primary action the home screen itself — an amount keypad with Request / Pay — instead of a dashboard that links to it.

## Why it works
- The app opens on a large amount keypad (documented, widely studied): type a number, then choose Request or Pay — no navigation to reach the core job; balance, card and investing live in a bottom tab bar.
- Apple's editorial praises that it 'freed us from personal checks and IOUs' and singles out Boosts — instant discounts tied to the Cash Card — as a retention hook layered on payments.
- Scale/quality signals: 8.8M ratings at 4.8, Editors' Choice, #4 Finance [moderate — store signals].

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product with one overwhelmingly frequent action (send, log, record, scan).
- Doesn't: multi-purpose tools where no single action dominates; P2P money apps also need strong anti-scam friction that a 'type and send' flow can under-serve.
