## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let a proprietary typeface and a black/white UI carry the brand so the colourful work customers make can be the only colour on the page.

## Why it works
- Custom figmaSans throughout (130 of ~140 text nodes) plus figmaMono; h1 56px weight 400 at -1.25px tracking, sizes jump 12/14/16/18/30/44/56/88 — light weights at large sizes rather than bold display.
- The chrome is strictly black on white (CTA 'Get started for free' is a solid black rectangle, secondary is outlined); the hero is a collage of customer-style app screens supplying all of the colour.
- Hero is a three-column composition: headline left, stacked artefact collage centre, an oversized black 'Get started' button right — unusual but the primary action is impossible to miss.
- Interaction timing is uniform: 0.18s ease-out on ~60 elements; reduced-motion CSS present. A dismissible Config announcement bar sits at the bottom instead of pushing the hero down.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Platforms whose users' output is the proof (creative tools, publishing, templates); brands that want a confident neutral frame.
- Doesn't: Products without strong user-generated visuals — a monochrome frame with nothing colourful to hold looks empty; the weight-400 display type depends on a well-drawn custom face.
