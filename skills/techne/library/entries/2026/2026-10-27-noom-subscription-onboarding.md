## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A long personalised quiz can build commitment, but if the trial auto-renews and cancelling requires a chat with a 'coach', the funnel's success becomes legal liability — make cancel as easy as sign-up.

## Why it works
- Plaintiffs alleged Noom moved trial users into expensive automatic renewals and made cancelling hard; reported cancellation path ran through messaging an assigned 'coach' that was a bot pointing users to App Store/Play steps [moderate — allegations; Noom denied wrongdoing].
- Settlement (reported Feb 2022): $56M cash + $6M subscription credits, plus product changes — clear auto-renewal disclosures, email reminders before trials end, and an easily accessible 'cancel' button in the account page [strong as a record of required remedies].
- The required remedies are a ready-made checklist for any trial-based paywall.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Lesson applies: every subscription app with a trial (health, fitness, learning, media).
- Doesn't: the quiz-funnel onboarding itself is not the problem — the renewal and exit mechanics are.
