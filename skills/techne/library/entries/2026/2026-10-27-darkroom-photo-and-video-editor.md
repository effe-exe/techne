## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Remove the import step: operate directly on the user's existing library (Apple Photos) so the first edit happens in seconds, and lead your pitch with the step you removed.

## Why it works
- Headline is the workflow promise, not an adjective: 'Everything to edit. No import needed.' — system 700 at 50px, white on a dark product collage (iPad grid of library thumbnails with red selection rings, MacBook, iPhone).
- Subhead names the tools and the trial in one breath: 'Curves, masks, and color grading for the photos and videos already in Apple Photos. Try every tool free.'
- Single saturated CTA 'Try it Free' rgb(199,4,46) 24px-radius pill beside a ghost 'What's in Darkroom+' — paid tier is explained, not hidden.
- Proof row in four equal columns: Apple Design Award, '4.8 Average Rating 119k+ Ratings, 5k+ Reviews', 'Editors' Choice '18 / App of the Day '15', '20M+ Downloads Since 2015'. DNA: 0.2s ease-in-out transitions, prefers-reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any tool that edits data the user already has elsewhere (photos, docs, code, spreadsheets): integrate with the source of truth instead of making a silo.
- Doesn't: when the external library cannot hold your edit model (non-destructive history, proprietary layers) — then a clear import with explained benefits is more honest.
