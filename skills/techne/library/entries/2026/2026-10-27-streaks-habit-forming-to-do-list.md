## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Cap the list: a fixed grid of large circular tasks (up to 24) makes daily progress visible at a glance and stops the habit list from becoming another backlog.

## Why it works
- Positioning in one line: 'The to-do list that helps you form good habits.' with the mechanism spelled out — 'Track up to 24 tasks you want to complete each day. Every day you complete a task, your streak grows.'
- In-app screen: a 2-column grid of big circular progress rings with pictograms and counts (RUN 2.3 KM · 2, DON'T SMOKE · 11, EAT A HEALTHY MEAL · 6, BRUSH YOUR TEETH 3:00 with play button) on a blue→violet gradient — negative habits ('Don't smoke') are first-class.
- Display type is Roboto Condensed 700 caps at 150px (line-height 0.9) — 'STREAKS' as a poster — over coral rgb(255,112,76); eyebrow 'APPLE DESIGN AWARD WINNER' in darker letterspaced caps.
- Price stated beside the download: 'A one-time purchase, with no subscription.' DNA: reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: habit, chores, medication and routine apps; any daily checklist where completeness matters more than capture.
- Doesn't: users who are harmed by streak pressure (see the Gentler Streak entry for the counterpoint); open-ended task managers.
