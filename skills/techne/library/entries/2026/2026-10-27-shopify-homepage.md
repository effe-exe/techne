## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use a very light (300) display weight at huge size over motion footage — the type stays elegant while the video carries the energy — and continue with two-tone headlines that put the claim in white and the qualifier in grey.

## Why it works
- Hero "Be the next" in Shopify-Inter at 96px weight 300, -1.92px (-2%) tracking, lh 1.08, white over a blurred, kinetic full-bleed video of a merchant; subline "Dream big and build fast on Shopify. The world's best commerce platform."
- Two pills: filled white "Start for free" and outlined "▶ Why we build Shopify" — action vs story.
- The next section rises over the video as a near-black card with large top radius and a two-tone headline: "Sell everywhere people shop." white + "Online and in person." grey rgb(161,161,170).
- Accent mint rgb(54,244,164) used sparingly in text; motion mostly 0.3s cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1); prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: platform marketing that needs both aspiration and clarity; dark-mode landing pages.
- Doesn't: weight-300 white text over video needs a scrim; it relies on blur/darkening to stay legible.
