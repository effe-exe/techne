## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- In emergency products, trust is the interface: say who you are (a nonprofit), what you alert on, and show the exact alert copy a person will see on their lock screen.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Real-Time Disaster Intelligence You Can Trust' in Inter 700, 72px, -0.72px; subhead states identity and scope in one line: 'Watch Duty, a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, alerts you of nearby wildfires and flooding in real-time.'
- The hero device is a lock screen with one notification: 'Vegetation Fire — Fast moving vegetation fire, light flashy fuels per arriving units' — the product's actual voice, sourced from responders.
- Three equal outlined download chips (APP STORE / GOOGLE PLAY / ONLINE) in Roboto Mono 12px caps with 1.2px tracking; the only filled control is the amber rgb(242,169,0) 'GET THE APP' button, the same amber as the flame icon.
- Evidence: ADA Social Impact jury plus widely reported use during the 2025 Los Angeles fires [moderate; adoption reported in press, not measured here]. DNA: cubic-bezier(0.215,0.61,0.355,1) 0.3s.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: alerts, safety, health and civic products; any notification-led app — design the notification copy first.
- Doesn't: marketing-led consumer products where institutional framing would feel stiff; don't fake 'nonprofit-grade' trust signals.
