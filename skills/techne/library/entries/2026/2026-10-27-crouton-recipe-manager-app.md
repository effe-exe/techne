## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Parse messy content into structured, actionable parts — highlight the data the user acts on (quantities, units) in an accent colour and make it the control (scale, convert).

## Why it works
- App screenshots on the site show ingredient lines where quantity+unit ('1 lb', '½ cups', '1 Tbsp') are tinted system blue and the ingredient name stays black — scannable at arm's length while cooking.
- Recipe header offers one primary 'Start' (green play pill) for step-by-step cooking, beside a Scale chip and servings/prep/cook stat chips (6 · Prep 15 min · Cook 20 min).
- Native iOS idioms throughout (large-title 'All Recipes', search field, 5-tab bar Meal Plan / Recipes / Groceries / Discover / Settings) — it wins on interaction, not visual novelty.
- Site is deliberately plain: Nunito 40/500 headline 'Recipe Organizer & Meal Planner', one App Store badge, 'Available on iOS, iPadOS, macOS, visionOS & watchOS'; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any import-and-use content (recipes, workouts, itineraries, invoices): structure the blob, colour the actionable tokens, offer a focused 'do it now' mode.
- Doesn't: free-form creative content where imposing structure loses meaning.
