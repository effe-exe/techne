## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Nav as a sentence: comma-separated links at body size ('Work, Agency, Careers, Store'). One type size shared by UI and copy keeps an expressive site calm.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed video hero (the Locomotive mark projected onto a person's hoodie, cyan cast) under a bottom-left lockup in the custom LocomotiveNew condensed serif, 70px, copper-brown: 'Locomotive® Digital-first Design Agency', next to a boxed OPS/DES/DEV mark.
- The nav is a comma-separated sentence ('Work, Agency, Careers, Store') plus 'Let's talk' at the same 26px HelveticaNowDisplay used for body text. Size distribution: 26px ×56, 70 ×15, 110 ×9, so the system has essentially three sizes.
- No border radius anywhere (0 radii), black/white surfaces only, so colour comes from the footage.
- Motion is easeOutCubic, cubic-bezier(0.215,0.61,0.355,1), at 0.2–0.3s. WebGL is present and prefers-reduced-motion CSS is honoured.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Agencies, editorial brands and portfolios that want an expressive first screen without UI clutter; the three-size type system transfers anywhere.
- Doesn't: Dense product navigation (a sentence nav breaks past about 5 items), and brands without strong video assets.
