## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Design for gloves and glare: put the few numbers that matter mid-activity (vertical, distance, speed, heart rate, time) on the wrist in big colour-coded type, and save analysis for later on the phone.

## Why it works
- Device trio shows the split of jobs: Apple Watch with five large colour-coded stats (13,926 FT, 11.4 MI, 55.8 MPH, 145 BPM, 2:45:09); iPhone logbook with season stats and upcoming trips; Android phone with 3D trail map and speed-coloured run trace.
- Headline 'Take Your Snow Days to the Next Level' in Fellix 700 at 60px, -1.5px, navy rgb(11,32,57) on an icy sky-blue gradient; body copy hyperlinks each feature noun in blue (detailed stats, Locate your friends, condition reports, trail maps) so the paragraph doubles as navigation.
- Trust line up front: 'Slopes is built by a small team with a big passion for skiing and snowboarding.'
- DNA: 9999px pills, 12px cards, 0.15s cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1); no reduced-motion CSS.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any activity tracker (running, cycling, diving, field work) — split glanceable in-the-moment UI from reflective post-activity analysis.
- Doesn't: desk-bound analytics products where there is no 'in the moment' context.
