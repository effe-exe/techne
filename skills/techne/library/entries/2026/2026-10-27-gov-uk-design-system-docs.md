## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Accessibility and honesty as visual style: 19px body, visible focus, square high-contrast buttons, and a cookie banner where Accept and Reject look identical.

## Why it works
- Cookie banner offers 'Accept analytics cookies' and 'Reject analytics cookies' as identical green rgb(15,122,82) buttons, plus 'View cookies' link — the ethical baseline, shown on first load.
- GDS Transport throughout; body at 19px (the system default), H1 48/700 line-height 1.04; links rgb(26,101,166) underlined.
- Zero border-radius anywhere (radii list empty), no transitions detected — nothing decorative competes with content. Buttons carry a dark bottom 'shadow' edge.
- Nav: Get started / Styles / Components / Patterns / Community / Accessibility; sub-copy argues reuse of research from other teams. Patterns are backed by published user research.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: public services, healthcare, finance, any high-stakes form-heavy product.
- Doesn't: brand-led consumer marketing; the system is intentionally plain.
