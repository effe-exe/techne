## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Constrain the creative act (one random daily moment, two minutes, both cameras, no filters) so the constraint itself becomes the product's identity.

## Why it works
- Core loop (documented app behaviour): one notification per day at an unpredictable time, a two-minute window, front+back camera captured together, and friends' posts unlock only after you post — a reciprocity gate that kills lurking and performative editing.
- bereal.com mirrors the 'unpolished' promise: a single dark, grainy, flash-lit video of friends behind 'Your daily dose of real life.' in white Inter ~48px bold, one 'Get the app ↗' text link; nav is just Ads / News / Jobs + 'Get Verified' and a white 'Contact us' pill.
- Black rgb(0,0,0) with grey-blue text rgb(209,214,222); Inter + Figtree; 64px pill radii, 16px cards.
- Recognition: Apple iPhone App of the Year 2022 [moderate as craft signal]; the product's later usage decline is a reminder that a novel constraint wins attention faster than it sustains habit.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: social and creative products seeking authenticity; prompts and challenges in habit/learning apps.
- Doesn't: utility apps; random-time pressure prompts are inappropriate where anxiety or safety are factors.
