## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- When retiring a product, lead with the date and one action: say plainly when it ends, then offer a single 'Read more' into the details — no marketing, no pressure.

## Why it works
- The whole fold is a single message: logo, serif headline 'A farewell to Arc' (Exposure VAR 76px/600, -2.4px tracking) and two lines stating 'Arc is sunsetting on December 22, 2026. Scroll to read what's happening and what's next.'
- One dark pill CTA ('Read more') centered on rgb(11,11,11); no sign-up, no upsell above the fold.
- Body type switches to ABC Oracle at 21px/1.42 with a 45ch measure further down — set for reading, which fits a page whose job is explanation.
- Long-form, low-media page (media coverage 0.04) — the brand voice and type do the work; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Product sunsets, major migrations, pricing changes, breaking API deprecations — any announcement users must not miss.
- Doesn't: Not an exemplar of the Arc product itself any more (it is ending); only the hero was observed, so the export/migration details below the fold are unverified.
