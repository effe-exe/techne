## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make a hard medium (3D) feel like a familiar one (sketching): keep chrome to thin edge rails and let the Pencil stroke be the primary input.

## Why it works
- Headline 'At last. Draw in 3D.' set in the handwritten 'marydale' face at 80px (-1.6px), with 'Draw in 3D.' on a skewed mint-green highlighter swash — the type itself sketches.
- Hero is the real editor on a black iPad canvas: grey 3D skeleton strokes, a large circular brush cursor, a slim left rail (brush size/opacity presets, undo/redo) and a tiny bottom Transform / Liquify toggle — nearly all screen is canvas.
- Pretendard UI type at 16–18px, ink rgb(28,29,36) and grey rgb(104,107,120); 'Buy now' is a dark pill — direct purchase.
- DNA: springy cubic-bezier(0.175,0.885,0.32,1.275) on some transitions; no reduced-motion CSS found.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creative and spatial tools where users bring a mental model from a simpler medium (paper, whiteboard).
- Doesn't: precision 3D (CAD, BIM) where numeric input matters more than gesture.
