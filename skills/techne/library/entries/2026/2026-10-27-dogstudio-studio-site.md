## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Pair one giant editorial serif statement with a single art-directed 3D mascot: one voice and one creature carry the brand.

## Why it works
- The hero stacks 'We Make Good Shit' in GT Sectra Display serif, centred over an iridescent 3D dog among drifting leaves on a deep blue-violet nebula, crossed by thin red diagonal lines.
- A single nav affordance ('› Our Showreel' with a red chevron) and a hamburger keep the chrome minimal. Positioning copy at about 23px reads: 'Dogstudio is a multidisciplinary creative studio at the intersection of art, design and technology.'
- The cookie notice gives 'Accept' and 'Deny' identical styling (red text links, same size), with plain wording about functional and analytical cookies: honest consent inside an expressive site.
- Heebo and Gilroy for UI; quart-out and cubic-out easings at 0.3–1.2s; WebGL; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Studios and entertainment brands with a mascot; the equal-weight consent pattern applies everywhere.
- Doesn't: Brands that cannot carry the profanity or the dark, heavy 3D scene.
