## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Show generative output as a finished, coherent campaign in context (one fictional brand across packaging, billboard, social, palette swatches) and frame the prompt around the user's deadline — 'What's due this week?' — instead of the model's power.

## Why it works
- Hero background is a top-down studio table covered in a single fictional brand's assets (cans, posters, a street billboard, colour swatches, logo explorations, hands and sticky notes) — consistency across formats is the proof point for an AI creative agent.
- Centred 52px Graphik 500 headline (-2.08px) 'Luma is your creative partner', 22px subhead 'You have the idea. Luma helps make it real.', single white pill 'Try for free'.
- A frosted card rises from the fold: 'What's due this week?' with a placeholder 'Create a product launch video for our new summer collection' — the prompt is phrased as a work brief.
- Black/white chrome, uppercase 14px nav, 24px panels; caveat: no prefers-reduced-motion CSS detected and the busy collage needs the dark scrim for headline contrast.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: AI tools for marketers, agencies and creators; any product where outputs must be consistent across formats.
- Doesn't: audiences who want to see the raw tool UI first; collage backgrounds can bury text on small screens.
