## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Treat consent as part of the brand: Attio's cookie prompt gives 'Reject' the same size and shape as 'Continue' — honest defaults cost nothing visually.

## Why it works
- interDisplay 64px/600 at line-height 0.95 and -1.3px tracking for 'Welcome to agentic revenue.'; body 18px/1.3 in muted grey; colours defined in lab() for very fine near-white steps (bg lab 99.99, panels lab 96-98).
- CTA pair is quiet: outlined 'Talk to sales' and near-black 'Start for free' at 10px radius, repeated in the nav — no colour competes with the product shot.
- Hero is a live-feeling app shell (workspace switcher, Quick Actions ⌘K, 'Good morning, Alex' with an 'Ask anything…' box and suggestion chips) on a faint lavender vertical-line texture.
- Cookie banner bottom-left offers 'Continue' and 'Reject' as equal-sized buttons; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: B2B SaaS that wants to feel premium and calm; any site's consent UI.
- Doesn't: Brands that need a strong colour identity — this restraint relies on product UI quality; very long page (17.6k px) may bury pricing.
