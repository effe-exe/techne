## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the bio the headline: one sentence set huge ('Rauno Freiberg is an Estonian interaction designer working with Vercel and Devouring Details') replaces the tagline, About page and logo wall.

## Why it works
- The whole first card is a single sentence in the 'X' grotesk at 85px with staggered indents per line, overlapped by a flat yellow (#FFFF02) circle with a crosshair cursor at its centre. Bold weight marks only the proper nouns (Vercel, Devouring Details).
- Content sits in horizontally scrolling white cards on #EDEDED (the next card, 'Devouring Details', peeks in at the right edge), with a tick-mark ruler and an outlined frame at the top as a position indicator.
- Small, considered interactions in the DOM: 'Email' becomes 'Copied' (copy to clipboard instead of a mailto), year-tabbed Projects (2023 / 2022), and a closing mantra ('Make it fast. Make it beautiful. Make it consistent… Make it.').
- Only 2 sizes in use (85 and 720px display), 12px card radii, 0.15–0.2s ease-in-out, a display-P3 orange accent; no WebGL.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Personal sites, founder pages, about pages; copy-to-clipboard email anywhere.
- Doesn't: Horizontal card scrolling fights the default scroll on mouse wheels; test it with trackpad, wheel and keyboard. No reduced-motion CSS detected.
