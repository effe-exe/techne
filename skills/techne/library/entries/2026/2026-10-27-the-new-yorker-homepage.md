## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Treat the homepage lead like a magazine cover: one commissioned illustration, a short display title and a centred two-line dek with the writer's name in bold.

## Why it works
- Lead is a split: centred Irvin-style caps title ('OUR MAN IN PARIS', IrvinHeadingPro) over a 21px Caslon dek (TNYAdobeCaslonPro, line-height 1.33, ~48ch) — the byline 'Lauren Collins' is the only bold.
- Watercolour illustration on white with no frame or crop box; whitespace does the separation.
- Four families with strict roles: Caslon for reading (89), Neutraface for display labels (45), Graphik 12–13px for UI (34), Irvin for heads.
- Header is minimal: centred wordmark, 'Newsletter / Sign In' and search at 12px Graphik.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: publications, publishers, portfolios with commissioned art.
- Doesn't: commodity news — and a ~120px empty ad slot sat above the masthead at capture.
