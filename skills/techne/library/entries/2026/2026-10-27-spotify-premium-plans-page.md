## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- State the trial and the post-trial price directly under the trial CTA, at the moment of decision, not in a footer.

## Why it works
- Hero is three lines of SpotifyMixUITitleVariable at 128px/900 ('The ultimate home for music', 'music' italicised) over a looping vinyl video cropped into a circle; a visible pause button sits bottom-right of the video.
- Two CTAs only: filled Spotify green 'Try 2 months for $0' (9999px pill) and an outline 'View all plans'; directly beneath, 12px grey copy states 'Free for 2 months, then $12.99 per month after… Offer ends November 1, 2026' — the renewal price sits next to the button, not behind a link.
- Black/rgb(18,18,18) surfaces with white type, grey rgb(145,148,150) secondary; transitions are 0.15s — the motion lives in the video, not in UI.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: subscription paywalls and trial offers; brands that can carry a single oversized display line.
- Doesn't: products needing comparison up front — plan detail is pushed below the fold; 12px disclosure is the legal minimum of prominence, not a model for contrast.
