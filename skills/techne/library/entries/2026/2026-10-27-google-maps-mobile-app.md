## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Keep the map as the primary surface and layer search, place cards and directions as sheets over it, so context is never lost while drilling in.

## Why it works
- About page: centred multicolour pin, 'Your guide to anywhere' in Google Sans 60/400 at -0.5px, one sentence, one blue rgb(26,115,232) 48px-radius 'Try now'. Hero collage shows a natural-language prompt card ('Plan out my February road trip… Grand Canyon, Horseshoe Bend… any recommended stops along the way?') over map tiles — AI trip planning framed as a query, not a chatbot.
- Restrained Google palette: rgb(32,33,36) text, rgb(95,99,104) secondary, rgb(248,249,250) surfaces; Material elevation shadows rgba(60,64,67,0.3/0.15).
- Store signals: #1 Navigation, 7.4M ratings at 4.7 [moderate].

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any location, logistics or spatial product; drill-down UIs where the parent context matters (bottom sheets over a persistent canvas).
- Doesn't: products where the map is incidental — a map-first layout wastes space.
