## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Rebuild an expert tool around direct manipulation (Pencil + touch) and promise a concrete learning time ('Learn in 15 minutes') instead of a feature list.

## Why it works
- Headline 'GO FROM IDEA TO 3D MODEL IN MINUTES' in PP Fraktion Sans 900 uppercase, 72px, line-height 0.9, -2.4px tracking, white on rgb(13,13,13); subhead positions both audiences: 'simple enough for beginners and powerful enough for engineers'.
- Tabbed demo strip under the hero — LEARN IN 15 MINUTES / FAST MODELING, ANYWHERE / SEND TO 3D PRINT — with a blue progress underline over a video of Pencil modelling on iPad.
- Electric blue rgb(29,78,255) is the only accent (SIGN UP, primary CTA); square 1px-radius buttons with inset highlight shadows give an engineering-tool feel; easings cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1).
- Friction noted: the primary CTA is 'SIGN UP TO DOWNLOAD' — account required before trying.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: pro tools (CAD, DAWs, analytics) that want novices: time-to-first-success as the headline, direct manipulation as the method.
- Doesn't: copy the account-gated download — let people try first where possible.
