## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn an abstract data product into a physical metaphor: tactile, hand-made-looking 3D objects (funnels, pipes, magnifier, mailbox) explain 'data in, revenue out' without a diagram.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed hero is a grainy clay-textured landscape with props that map to the product's pipeline (funnel of shapes, pipe, magnifying glass, mailbox, bar-chart blocks) — the illustration is the explainer.
- Headline 'Build systems to grow revenue' in Roobert variable 88px/575 at -3.5px tracking, white on deep green, with a lime CTA ('Get a demo') as the only saturated UI colour against black 'Start free trial' in the nav.
- Nav exposes a ⌘K search affordance — a signal to a power-user audience before they open the product.
- Cookie banner keeps 'More choices' and policy link visible next to 'Accept all' (accept is visually emphasised); no reduced-motion CSS was detected — check autoplaying elements before borrowing.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Abstract B2B products (data, infra, automation) that need a memorable brand world; teams with budget for bespoke 3D art.
- Doesn't: Small teams without an illustration pipeline (a cheap imitation looks worse than none); brands needing a serious, institutional tone.
