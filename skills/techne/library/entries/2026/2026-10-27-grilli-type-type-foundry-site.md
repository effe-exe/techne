## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Set every product's name in the product itself on a strict column grid, so the catalogue index doubles as the specimen.

## Why it works
- The retail index sets each family name in its own face at 47.8px (GT Mechanik, GT Canon, GT Era, GT Standard, GT Flaire, GT Pantheon) in a two-column list with 1px rules: you compare voices at a glance without opening a page.
- Strict Swiss grid: a narrow left label column ('GT Mechanik Minisite', 'Retail typefaces') and a wide content column; all UI is GT-Corporate-Web at 16px, grey #8C8C8C for nav and black for active text, on white.
- Free trials are a first-class nav item and a boxed sidebar ('Try out our fonts – for free... Download trial fonts') sits beside the list, an honest low-commitment path.
- The featured release (GT Mechanik minisite) is a test-card illustration in olive and pink line-work; personality lives in the feature slot, the system stays neutral. Hover transitions 0.1s ease-out across 76 elements.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any catalogue whose items differ by look or feel (fonts, themes, colour palettes, icon sets): render the name in the item itself.
- Doesn't: Catalogues of items that cannot be previewed inline (services, SaaS plans).
