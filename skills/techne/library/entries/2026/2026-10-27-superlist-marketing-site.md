## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Promise relief, not features: 'A little less on your mind.' — one emotional benefit, with a single hand-drawn underline on the word that matters ('less').

## Why it works
- Headline in Haffer XH Bold 68px (88px variant also present) at -3px tracking and line-height 1.02 — very tight, chunky grotesk; violet hand-drawn underline under 'less'.
- Kicker in tracked uppercase 'FOR WORK, FOR HOME, AND FOR EVERYTHING IN BETWEEN.' sets scope before the headline.
- Red-orange gradient pill CTA 'Start for free' + text link 'See how it works' on warm white rgb(255,253,249); product shot shows Today view with meeting card ('Take notes'), shortcut hint '⌘N', and a task detail with real-feeling estimates.
- 0.2s transitions only; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Personal/team task tools, wellbeing-adjacent productivity; any product whose benefit is reduced stress.
- Doesn't: Tools sold on power and configurability, where 'less' undersells.
