## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- 'Built like a game, runs like an app': spend game-feel (3D, physics, haptics, sound) on the one interaction people repeat daily, and strip everything else ('No menus… Just simple swipes and taps').

## Why it works
- Brand page is a type poster: a giant periwinkle '!BORING' wordmark, then 'Playful software for life's boring routines.' in Founders Grotesk 700 at 76px line-height 1.0 on light grey rgb(224,224,224).
- Positioning sentence is the principle: 'Most apps help you save time. We help you savor it.' Each app is labelled like a product SKU (!Habits APPx004, !Weather APPx002) and skins like fashion drops (SKNx001…), with Jet Brains mono for codes.
- Explicit design philosophy published: 'Beautifully designed like a game using 3D, animation, physics, haptics, and sounds… Apps that do one thing exceptionally well… No menus.' The studio's essays include 'The World's Most Satisfying Checkbox' and 'The Sound of Software'.
- Monetisation via seasonal skins/artist collaborations rather than feature gating; black 4px-radius 'Download' button, no dark patterns observed.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: daily-repeat micro-interactions in any product (checking a habit, completing a task, sending a payment) where satisfying feedback increases return without adding features.
- Doesn't: dense professional tools and accessibility-critical flows — heavy 3D/haptics must respect Reduce Motion and never replace legible state.
