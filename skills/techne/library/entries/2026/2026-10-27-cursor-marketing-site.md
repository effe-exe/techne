## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Show the agent's actual workflow — task queue, 'ready for review' states, file diffs, live preview — inside a full-width product frame; the screenshot is the argument, the headline just names the category in two lines.

## Why it works
- Headline is deliberately small: CursorGothic 26px/400, -0.325px tracking, two lines — the 1300px-wide product window below carries the page (media coverage 0.34).
- The hero window is a believable scenario: left rail of agent tasks ('IN PROGRESS 1', 'READY FOR REVIEW 4' with timestamps and +135 -21 diff counts), middle transcript ('Read about-acme.md', 'Thought 6s', files +52 -0), right live localhost preview — human-in-the-loop review is the visual story.
- Warm, non-tech palette: rgb(247,247,244) paper ground, rgb(38,37,30) olive-black ink, oklab alpha greys; product framed on a muted oil-painting landscape instead of a neon gradient. Platform-detected CTA ('Download for Linux ↓') in a pill.
- Cookie banner gives 'Reject All' the same dark-pill weight as 'Accept All Cookies'; reduced-motion CSS present; transitions 0.12-0.2s standard ease.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: devtools and agentic products where trust comes from seeing the review loop; any product whose UI is its best marketing.
- Doesn't: products with no visual surface (APIs, infra) or where a fake demo would overpromise; the painterly backdrop is brand-specific — don't copy it.
