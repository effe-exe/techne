## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Present multi-script products as equals: the Arabic specimen is the hero, the Latin 'echoes' it, and variants are a segmented switch on the specimen itself.

## Why it works
- A full-width blue #2B73DB band states the positioning plainly in 29LT Bukra: 'Specialists in multi-script typography, from Arabic and Latin to other global scripts.'
- The family block '29LT Idris — A Naskh type system ... echoed by a versatile Latin' has a three-way segmented control (Sharp + Flat / Pure / Round) directly above a large Arabic specimen (معاون); display sizes run 144–216px.
- Cookie dialog offers Customise, Reject All and Accept All as three equal-size buttons and states 'Our website does not display ads' — a fair consent pattern.
- Black nav bar with a theme toggle; transitions 0.3s on 123 nodes; 2px radius buttons in #1863DC.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Bilingual / multi-script products, localisation-heavy brands, any catalogue where variants should be compared in place.
- Doesn't: Single-script, single-variant products; the switch adds nothing.
