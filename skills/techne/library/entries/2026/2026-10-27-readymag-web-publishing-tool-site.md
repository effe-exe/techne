## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Float one white value-prop card with a single big CTA over a live collage of customer-made pages, so promise and proof share one screen.

## Why it works
- The background is a moving collage of real Readymag-built pages (a condensed 'JAVI NORELLI' poster site, an eye close-up, a long-form 'Non-Objective Times' essay with footnote numerals, a giant 'publish' word): editorial output as wallpaper.
- Centered on top, a 20px-radius white card: 'Make the web more like you.' in black, then 'A human-powered design tool for creating distinctive websites' in 40% grey, and a full-width orange #FF5900 'Try Readymag' pill (~84px tall).
- Nav is a row of separate light-grey pills (Solutions, Pricing, Examples, Templates, Learn) with an orange 'Sign up' pill; cookie bar offers 'Allow all', 'Manage' and 'Reject all' as equal pills.
- 'Human-powered' positions the tool against AI generators in two words. WebGL used; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Creation tools (site builders, publishing, templates) whose users' output is visually varied and impressive.
- Doesn't: Tools whose output is private or uniform (internal dashboards, forms).
