## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give a utilitarian tool a warm, editorial voice: a display serif and one saturated sunlight colour make 'browser' feel like a calm workday rather than a tech spec.

## Why it works
- Display serif Exposure VAR (h2 48px/500, -2.4px tracking) for the 'More than a browser. A better workday.' line and a giant swash 'DIA' wordmark that the product window overlaps — type and product share one layered composition.
- Background is a blurred, dappled-light photograph in saturated yellow (surface rgb(255,203,48)) fading into warm paper rgb(251,250,246); the browser mock is tinted to match rather than shown in neutral chrome.
- Single high-contrast CTA: a wide black pill 'Join the Waitlist' with a quiet 'Available for macOS and Windows' caption — platform availability answered next to the action.
- Floating pill nav (ABC Oracle, 20px radius, translucent rgba(235,235,235,0.7)); 0.2s transitions on ~120 elements; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Consumer-leaning productivity tools that compete on feel; AI products that want to look calm instead of futuristic.
- Doesn't: Enterprise buyers who expect feature density; the photo-blur background costs contrast for any text placed over it.
