## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Treat personality as a user-adjustable layer on top of serious data: the joke is optional, the forecast is not.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed saturated blue rgb(10,125,255) with a white wordmark whose 'O' is a robot eye, and a yellow highlight on 'crazy-powerful'; the phone shows 72° / Feels 69° with the line 'Make the most of this nice weather that I generated for you. Or else.' over an illustrated hillscape with delivery drones.
- Feature copy states 'You can even customize CARROT's personality to your liking' — the voice is a setting, so users who want plain data can have it.
- Honest tiering: every premium feature in the list carries an asterisk and '*Requires Premium Club membership' is printed on the page.
- Data credibility kept alongside jokes: switchable sources (AccuWeather, Apple Weather, Foreca), custom alerts, achievements for weather events and travel.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: utilities in crowded categories (weather, finance, fitness, to-dos) where voice differentiates, provided the user can dial it down.
- Doesn't: high-stakes or distress contexts (health alerts, severe-weather emergencies, money loss) — humour must switch off there.
