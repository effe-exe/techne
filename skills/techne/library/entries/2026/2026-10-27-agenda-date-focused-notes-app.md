## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Organise notes on a timeline (past / today / future) by linking them to dates and calendar events, so context comes from when, not from folders.

## Why it works
- Positioning in three words under the wordmark: 'Organize your days' and 'Notes meets Calendar' (Avenir Next light 24px) over a flat orange/amber illustrated landscape — the mountains read as an 'A'.
- Pricing is stated up front in plain language: 'Agenda is FREE, with no time limits. You can use it forever, at no cost.' followed by 'Supercharge your note taking through Premium features.'
- Platform requirements stated in the footer line ('Requires macOS 12 / iOS 15 / iPadOS 15 or higher') — no surprises after download.
- Palette is two oranges rgb(255,142,0)/rgb(255,154,1) plus navy rgb(43,78,108) section bands; no motion on site.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: meeting notes, journals, project logs, CRM notes — anything where 'when' is the natural index.
- Doesn't: reference knowledge (recipes, docs) that is timeless; date-first organisation adds noise there.
