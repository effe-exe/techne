## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the regulatory disclaimer a designed component (a calm floating bar) instead of fine print — trust copy treated as UI earns more trust than hiding it.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed, atmospheric landscape photo (a desk with laptop on a misty hillside) under a centred 49px arcadiaDisplay headline at weight 480 — calm, unhurried, the opposite of the hustle aesthetic typical of startup banking.
- Hero CTA is a combined email field + indigo "Open account" pill (40px radius) plus a ghost "Launch demo" pill: first step and low-commitment alternative side by side.
- The "not an FDIC-insured bank" disclosure sits in a dark rounded bar floating at the bottom of the first viewport, legible at ~13px white on near-black — compliance made visible, not buried.
- Custom Arcadia family (text + display cuts) used exclusively; dark surfaces rgb(23,23,33)/rgb(30,30,42) with lavender-white text rgb(237,237,243). Motion 0.3s/0.15s Material-style cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1); DNA found prefers-reduced-motion rules.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: premium or regulated services that want to feel calm and confident; any product where an honest disclosure must be seen.
- Doesn't: mass-market consumer products where an aspirational photo can read as vague; 84% media coverage means heavy pages.
