## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Animate the transformation itself: a rambling spoken sentence drifts in as a pale looping ribbon, passes through a waveform pill and exits as a clean, dark ribbon tagged 'Grammar corrected' — the value proposition as motion, no screenshot needed.

## Why it works
- Headline in EB Garamond 96px (-2.88px, line-height 0.95): 'Don't type,' roman then 'just speak.' in italic — the italic carries the spoken, human half of the promise; a small uppercase eyebrow 'WISPR FLOW DICTATION' names the product.
- The speech ribbon: grey 'raw' text curls in a loop on the left, enters a black-outlined waveform capsule, and leaves as white-on-black polished text, with a dark-green check chip 'Grammar corrected' marking the change.
- Navigation embeds a segmented switch (Dictation | Notetaker) for the two products; one lilac rgb(240,215,255) CTA with a 2px ink border, 'Available on Mac, Windows, iPhone, and Android' under it.
- Cream rgb(255,255,235) ground, ink rgb(26,26,26), deep green rgb(3,79,70) banner, coral/amber accents; springy cubic-bezier(0.34,1.56,0.64,1) on hovers; WebGL with prefers-reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any AI that transforms input (cleanup, translation, summarisation, formatting) — show before → process → after in one continuous motion.
- Doesn't: products whose transformation is invisible or slow; keep the animation pausable and respect reduced-motion.
