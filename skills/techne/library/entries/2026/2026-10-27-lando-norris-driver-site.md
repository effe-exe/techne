## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put live, glanceable context in a persistent corner widget (next race, circuit outline), so an expressive fan site still answers 'what's next?' on the first screen.

## Why it works
- A ticket-shaped card bottom-left reads 'NEXT RACE: MEXICO GP' with a circuit outline and a laurel-framed helmet ('MCLAREN F1 SINCE 2019'): live season context as a UI component.
- The logotype pairs 'LANDO' in Brier serif with 'NORRIS' in Mona Sans Variable bold. A neon-lime 'STORE' button (#D2FF00, 7.2px radius) and an outlined menu square are the only actions top-right.
- An off-white ground with faint topographic contour lines and soft blobs (a WebGL field) gives texture without competing. Later sections switch to dark olive rgb(40,44,32) with lime rgb(178,199,58).
- Headings in Mona Sans 32–72px uppercase; custom easing cubic-bezier(0.65,0.05,0,1) at 0.75s; no reduced-motion CSS detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Athletes, artists, events and teams: any personal brand with a schedule. The corner widget fits product launches too ('next drop').
- Doesn't: Neon lime on off-white needs a contrast check for text use; keep it for fills with dark text, as done here.
