## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Offer self-hosting as a co-equal secondary CTA ('Sign up, it's free' / 'Self-host install') when control over data is part of the value.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Think and build digital products. Together.' in Work Sans at 72px with weight contrast (400 for the claim, bold for 'Together.') in deep indigo #151035 on pale #F5F8FB.
- CTA pair 'Sign up, it's free' (teal fill) and 'Self-host install' (outline), plus a pill announcement 'Any agent can design in Penpot. Try our open MCP Server' above the headline.
- The hero visual is the real editor in dark UI (Design system 'Music App', Layers/Assets, Design/Prototype/Inspect tabs, avatars); cookie bar offers Accept all, Reject all and Customize as equal buttons.
- prefers-reduced-motion CSS present; 8px radius on buttons; one soft shadow rgba(99,101,105,.25) 0 10px 30px.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Open-source or privacy-sensitive products where self-hosting is a real choice; tools adding agent/API access.
- Doesn't: Pure SaaS with no self-host option; the slot would mislead.
