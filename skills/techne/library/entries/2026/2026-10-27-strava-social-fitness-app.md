## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn private activity data into lightweight social currency (kudos, segments, routes) so the community — not the app — supplies the motivation.

## Why it works
- Homepage is the sign-up: centred 'Community-Powered Motivation' (Boathouse 32/600), one line 'Join over 100 million active people on Strava for free', 'Already a Member? Log In' above three stacked full-width buttons (Google, Apple outline; 'Sign Up With Email' in Strava orange rgb(252,82,0)).
- Triptych layout: cycling photo left, form centre, runners right with a phone map + watch recording screen (00:34:53, 11:14/mi pace) and a hand-drawn orange route squiggle — the product's data shown as the hero ornament.
- Single family Boathouse at 15/16 body; 4px radii; restrained (one large photo shadow).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product where peers' activity motivates (learning, habits, creative communities).
- Doesn't: sensitive health contexts; public leaderboards and default-public maps can create comparison pressure and privacy risk — keep defaults private.
