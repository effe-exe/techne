## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give 'Reject all' the same visual weight as 'Accept all' and write the consent copy in the brand voice — honest defaults are a brand moment, not a compliance chore.

## Why it works
- Cookie dialog: "Accept all" and "Reject all" are identical filled navy rgb(9,23,35) pills, "Customise" is outlined — no asymmetric nudging. Copy stays in voice ("We're not talking about the crunchy, tasty kind").
- Headline "Spend your money on life, not your life on money" in custom MonzoSansDisplay at 48.8px weight 800, over a full-bleed video inside a 32px-radius rounded card (inset 24px from the viewport). Subline names concrete mechanics ("Your salary sorted into Pots. Your spare change turned into savings.").
- Trust cues are third-party and specific: a "Which? Recommended Provider" roundel and an FSCS-protected badge placed in the hero, not the footer.
- Pill system: 128px/500px radius buttons, 16px cards; motion 0.2–0.3s with cubic-bezier(0.22,0,0.12,1); DNA found prefers-reduced-motion rules. Coral brand red rgb(255,79,64) used sparingly on surfaces.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any regulated consumer product needing warmth plus trust; every site with a consent banner.
- Doesn't: the playful voice needs restraint in high-stress moments (fraud, debt) — Monzo's app tones down there; copy the calibration, not the jokes.
