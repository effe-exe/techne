## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Reveal copy line by line through masks over full-bleed footage: the type 'arrives' with the scene instead of sitting on top of it.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed dusk footage of a Venetian dome; the intro paragraph ('Global digital design studio partnering with brands and businesses that create exceptional experiences…') was captured mid-reveal, each line sliding up inside its own mask.
- A giant 'Digital' rises from the bottom edge in translucent white Lausanne 300 at 250px, line-height 0.9, -11.5px tracking (-4.6%). Body copy is 24/1.33 at a 44ch measure.
- Quiet chrome: 'Scroll to explore' bottom-right, and contact details (email, phone) in the DOM menu. The palette is footage plus warm off-white rgba(224,205,189,.8) and ink #0D0E13.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Hospitality, real estate, culture and studio sites with strong film; masked line reveals transfer to any long-form hero.
- Doesn't: No reduced-motion CSS was detected, and text over video needs a contrast check frame by frame.
