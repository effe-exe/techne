## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- If the hero is an immersive animated piece, ship its off-switches with it: a mute toggle, a hide-animation (eye) toggle and a plain 'Read More' exit.

## Why it works
- The 'One Point Zero' fold is a full-screen animated tunnel of black organic glyph shapes on red #ED1C24, receding to a vanishing point where tiny type flickers: the site opens as an artwork, not a shop.
- All chrome is cyan pill/tab chips in Unica78 at 19px (tracking 0.38px): 'LLineto', 'One Point Zero', search; bottom-left 'Read More', a chevron and a speaker-mute toggle; bottom-right an eye icon that appears to toggle the visual layer.
- Only 12 links on the fold and no visible headline or catalogue entry: clarity is sacrificed for impact (hence role: technique). No reduced-motion CSS was detected; the explicit controls partly compensate.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Launch microsites, campaign moments, art-led brands where a first-visit spectacle is the point and returning users can skip it.
- Doesn't: Any task-led surface; a full-screen animation without a headline blocks first-time understanding.

## Style DNA (optional, compact)
type: Unica78 19px chips only · color: red #ED1C24 + black artwork, cyan chips with red text · shape: 2px tabs, 19px pills · motion: full-screen animation, mute + eye toggles, no reduced-motion CSS
