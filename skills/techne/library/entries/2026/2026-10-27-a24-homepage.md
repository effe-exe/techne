## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Set the title so large it crops off the frame (225px, -5% tracking) over a film still — confidence through scale, with a single outlined 'Buy tickets' as the only action.

## Why it works
- Hero title in Neue Montreal at 225px / weight 485, line-height 0.8, letter-spacing -11.18px (≈ -5%), breaking across two lines and running off the right edge.
- Nav is 12px caps Neue Montreal (FILM, TV, MUSIC, LIVE, NOTES, SHOP) with a tiny centred A24 mark — chrome shrinks so the image dominates (media coverage 1.0).
- Near-black ground rgb(14,14,13) with a 6px inset frame; one 1px-radius outlined 'BUY TICKETS →' button.
- Cookie panel gives ACCEPT ALL and CHOOSE as equal full-width buttons; easing cubic-bezier(0.215,0.61,0.355,1), reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: film, music, fashion, launches — single-hero promotional pages.
- Doesn't: anything where the headline must be fully legible; the cropped word was partly hidden behind the cookie panel at capture.
