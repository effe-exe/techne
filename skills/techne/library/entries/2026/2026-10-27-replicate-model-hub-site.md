## Why it works
- 'Run AI with an API.' in rb-freigeist-neue at ~72px, tight two lines, white on a saturated magenta→orange→lime halftone gradient; subhead states the whole promise: 'Run and fine-tune models. Deploy custom models. All with one line of code.'
- Hero card left: JetBrains Mono snippet with tabs (Node, Python, HTTP) and a loader ring; right: the output photo (a banana-shaped armchair) with the prompt 'A soft armchair shaped like a peeled banana.' and the model id overlaid in small type.

- Square black CTAs (0px radius) in basier-square; caveat: WebGL halftone, no prefers-reduced-motion CSS detected, promo bar on top.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: APIs, SDKs, CLIs, any developer product where input→output is the proof.
- Doesn't: non-technical audiences; the high-saturation gradient needs a strong dark text shadow or scrim for contrast.
