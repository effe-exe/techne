## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Prove 'collaborative' with named live cursors on the marketing page itself (even in the announcement bar), not with a stock photo of people in a meeting.

## Why it works
- Named cursor flags ('Andrey', 'Rad', 'Brie') with coloured arrow pointers sit on and around the hero artefacts, including the black top announcement bar.
- Serif display Apris 56px/400 ('Human collaboration at the speed of AI') above Roobert UI text; dotted-grid page background references the board.
- Hero is a mini board: a Customer Feedback table with status chips, a Flow Process doc, a BPMN swimlane diagram and a prompt card — artefact types shown instead of listed.
- Single email field + 'Get Started Free' black button; cookie banner offers 'Reject All' (text-weight) alongside 'Cookies Settings' and 'Accept All Cookies' (outlined) — reject is available but lighter. Reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any multiplayer product; collaboration features that are hard to show in a static screenshot.
- Doesn't: Single-player tools; cursors everywhere can read as clutter on mobile.
