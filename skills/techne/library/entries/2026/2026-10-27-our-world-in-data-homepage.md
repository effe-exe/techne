## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make search the hero and prove depth with countable inventory chips (13,836 charts · 126 topic pages · 26 explorers · 506 articles) plus one honesty line ('All free: open access and openly licensed').

## Why it works
- Hero is a single search field with example queries in the placeholder ('Try "Life expectancy", "Poverty Nigeria Vietnam"…'), framed by a mission line in Playfair Display gold on navy rgb(0,33,71).
- A 'POPULAR PAGES' pill row (CO₂ Emissions, Population Growth…) sits above the hero — the most-wanted answers are one click away before search.
- Two-family system: Playfair Display for headlines (32/600), Lato for everything else (279 uses) — editorial authority on top of a neutral UI face.
- Cookie banner gives 'Reject optional cookies' and 'Accept optional cookies' equal-size buttons and states 'We never use ads on our website or sell user data.'

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any large reference/knowledge product (docs, datasets, catalogues).
- Doesn't: small sites where inventory numbers would be unimpressive.
