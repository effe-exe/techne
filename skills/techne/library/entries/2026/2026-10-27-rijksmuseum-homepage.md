## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the collection's most famous object be the page, and lay the wordmark over it at full width in a translucent tone so brand and artwork share the same frame.

## Why it works
- The Night Watch fills the viewport on slate rgb(64,71,79); 'RIJKS MUSEUM' spans ~1290px across the top in a semi-transparent grey so the painting reads through it.
- One typeface (RijksText, 43 uses) at only three sizes (21.8/17.4/12px) — a very tight scale lets the image carry hierarchy.
- A visitor photographing the painting in the foreground frames the site as an invitation to visit, not a catalogue.
- Cookie panel offers 'Accept' and a plain 'No, rather not' text link — decline is visible and human, if lower in weight.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: brands with one iconic asset (a product, place or artwork).
- Doesn't: when the asset is unknown — the hero has no headline or CTA beyond the ticket button.
