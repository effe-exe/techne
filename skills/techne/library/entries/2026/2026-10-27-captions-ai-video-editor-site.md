## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- When the input is the user's own material, make the hero a drop zone, not a prompt: 'Add footage to start', a disabled 'Create my video →' that wakes once footage exists, and a rail of style templates to pick from.

## Why it works
- Serif display (Exposure 48px, -0.48px) 'AI that edits like a professional editor would.' over a pastel peach-to-sky grainy gradient; nav in a glassy rounded bar.
- The main panel is a dashed drop area with a stacked-thumbnail '+' card and 'Add footage to start'; beneath, attach icon, a 'Prism Pro' model chip and a greyed 'Create my video →' — the disabled state communicates the required first step.
- An 'Edit ⌃⌄' mode selector sits above the panel; template thumbnails (Real estate, education, 'LEARN') preview caption styles as the second decision.
- Cookie banner offers Preferences / Reject / Accept with Reject as a visible filled button; white surfaces, ink rgb(29,31,32), grey rgb(126,132,134); 12-40px radii; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: editors, transcription, photo/video enhancement — any AI that works on user-supplied files.
- Doesn't: generators starting from nothing; on mobile the drop zone must become a clear picker.
