## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let visitors pick their device first: a Mac / iPhone / iPad switcher with a hand-drawn underline swaps the screenshot and the store badge, so every visitor sees their own context.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Markdown notes you'll love' in custom bearsansheadline 51px/400, with 'love' underlined in a light-blue hand-drawn stroke; subhead 'on Mac, iPhone & iPad'.
- Device tabs (laptop / phone / tablet icons + labels) with a sparkle-and-underline active state; a single 'Download on the Mac App Store' badge changes with the tab.
- Hero screenshot shows a real note ('Polar Bears' with photo) in the three-column app (dark sidebar, note list with red selection bar, editor) — Bear's red rgb(221,76,79) is the only accent.
- Generous whitespace, body 22.4px/1.4 with a 52ch measure; very little motion (no transitions detected).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Multi-platform apps whose experience differs per device; writing and note tools.
- Doesn't: Web-only products (nothing to switch); platform-specific store badges exclude other OSs.
