## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Borrow the vocabulary of the tools your users work in: dashed selection boxes around image fragments, pixel bitmaps and pill 'brush strokes' turn an infra landing page into a canvas that feels like a creative editor.

## Why it works
- Focal Web 96/500 headline at -3.84px tracking (line-height 0.9) in cyan rgb(153,237,255) on a dark olive rgb(64,55,0) field — an unusual, ownable pairing; three outline icons (video, audio, image) above name the modalities before the text does.
- Image fragments sit inside dashed marquee boxes like selected layers; pixel-bitmap shapes in blue/lime, pink and red pill shapes and scattered squares read as brush and selection tools.
- Lime 'Get Started' and white 'Contact Sales' square buttons (0-2px radius); body copy is a small 12px Focal Text serif-ish block — hierarchy by scale extremes; Hal Timezone Mono for labels.
- Consent panel offers Accept All / Reject All / Customize as equal full-width buttons; reduced-motion CSS present. Weakness: 12px body on olive is hard to read; busy fold.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creative-tool and media-infra brands that need to stand out among identical dark-gradient AI sites.
- Doesn't: enterprise or regulated buyers who need calm; never copy the palette — copy the idea of quoting your users' tools.
