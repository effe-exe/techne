## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the constraint the feature: a fixed number of slots (seven coloured dots) removes filing decisions and turns 'where did I put it' into muscle memory.

## Why it works
- App icon/wordmark tile shows the whole product model: a heavy rounded 'Tot' over a capsule of exactly seven coloured dots (yellow, orange, red, magenta, blue, cyan, green) — each dot is one note.
- One-sentence positioning in myriad-pro 200 at ~35px on black: 'Tot is a simple app. It lets you collect & edit text on your Mac, iPhone, iPad, and Apple Watch', with 'collect & edit text' in lilac rgb(221,187,255).
- Per-platform sections are colour-themed by the dot palette (dark magenta, navy, forest surfaces rgb(32,3,21)/rgb(3,16,56)/rgb(4,17,10)); download buttons are outlined 12px-radius pills each in a different dot colour (magenta, cyan, lime rgb(193,254,94)).
- Phone screenshot shows the in-app status line '15 lines · 49 words · 310 characters' — quiet, useful metadata instead of chrome.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: capture tools, clipboards, quick-switchers, favourites, launchers: bounded sets beat infinite lists when speed of recall matters.
- Doesn't: archival or knowledge-base tools where volume grows without bound; also, colour-only identity must be paired with position/labels for colour-blind users.
