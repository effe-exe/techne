## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A light-weight serif at 72px over editorial photography reads 'luxury' faster than any adjective — pair it with a one-line price ('Buy from $70,900').

## Why it works
- Headline in Lucid Serif VF 72/400 (-0.5px) over a coastal lifestyle photograph; the subline states the price ('Experience a superior long range electric sedan. Buy from $70,900¹').
- Tracked caps on rectangular CTAs ('VIEW INVENTORY' white, 'DEMO DRIVE' tan in nav) and a gold text colour rgb(215,190,150) give a jewellery-like register.
