## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give long projects structure inside the editor itself — a library of sheets/chapters with word counts and a deadline — so planning and writing happen in one place.

## Why it works
- iPad screenshot shows a manuscript 'Novel Without a Cause' with '95,000 words' and a deadline 'Jan 31, 2027', a Chapters list (Front Matter, One…Five, Back Matter, Notes, Characters) beside sheets with status tags.
- iPhone shows the editor's contextual menu (Highlight, Delete, Annotation, Comment) — markup-based annotation rather than a formatting toolbar.
- Interstate 700 at 48px headline 'The Ultimate Writing App for Mac, iPad and iPhone'; 18/1.6 body at ~63ch measure; yellow rgb(255,204,0) butterfly and 2px yellow underline on the active nav item are the only colour.
- Proof and offer side by side: Mac App Store badge, 'Free trial on all devices.', 'Apple Design Award Winner' laurels. Reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: tools for long projects (books, theses, documentation, scripts): goals, structure and status inside the workspace.
- Doesn't: quick notes and capture — the structure is overhead there (see Tot, Bear).
