## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Wrap a rigorous grotesk UI around one absurd, high-production hero image per release, and show live presence ('19 online') to make a shop feel like a place.

## Why it works
- Everything is set in Monument Grotesk (117 text nodes) with Monument Grotesk Mono for small print: a big left-aligned 8-item stacked nav (~24px) and a centred row of #F0F4F6 pill filters (New Releases, Font Customizer, Early Access, Variable Fonts, Editions, Licensing).
- The release slider is a 27px-radius card with a toy-like 3D render (a DHL-style logistics scene for 'ABC Schengen') plus a white 'ABC Schengen · Explore' bar and numbered 1–5 pagination dots: each release gets a joke-grade art direction.
- Small social touches: a green '19 online' presence pill next to Trial fonts/Login/Bag, and a cookie notice in yellow #FFEA00 with mono text and a fortune-cookie illustration; the notice says it uses authentication cookies only.
- Motion is tuned, not default: 0.25s transitions on 69 nodes with cubic-bezier(0.55,0.08,0,1); section padding ~110px. No reduced-motion CSS was detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Brands whose audience is other designers, where wit and production value are the differentiator; retail shops wanting a sense of 'others are here'.
- Doesn't: Sober B2B or regulated products; the joke-render style dates quickly and costs a lot per release.
