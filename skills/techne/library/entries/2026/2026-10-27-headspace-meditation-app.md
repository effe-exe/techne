## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use warm, rounded character illustration to lower the emotional barrier of a difficult behaviour, and give stressed users a one-tap 'panic' entry point.

## Why it works
- Apple's editorial highlights 'rich, accessible lessons' and that the Apple Watch app 'lets you hit a literal panic button when extra-stressful situations arise' — a crisis shortcut alongside the long-form course model.
- Brand system (seen on headspace.com, see related entry): orange sun mark, soft rounded characters, flat warm palette — reduces the clinical feel of mental health.
- Pricing in the listing is plain: $12.99 monthly / $69.99 annual, with a 'Headspace Plus' tier ($94.99) [store data].

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: health and wellbeing, onboarding to intimidating skills, products for anxious users.
- Doesn't: professional/clinical tools where whimsy undermines credibility; illustration-heavy UI needs strong alt text and contrast discipline.
