## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let one symbol and one typeface carry the brand: monochrome palette, a single logomark rendered as the hero object, and tight display tracking.

## Why it works
- Hero is a split layout: 'Agentic Infrastructure' in GeistSans 64/400 at -3.84px tracking (-6%) left, the black triangle logomark with a soft glow (WebGL detected) center, three short value lines right.
- Palette is near-monochrome: bg rgb(250,250,250), text rgb(23,23,23) and rgb(77,77,77); the only fill CTA is black pill 'Deploy now' next to an outlined 'Talk to sales'.
- Nav has three tiers of buttons (Get a Demo / Log In outlined, Sign Up filled) — hierarchy through fill, not color. Customer logo row sits at the fold edge.
- Motion respects prefers-reduced-motion (CSS media query present); transitions 0.1–0.3s cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: technical B2B brands that want precision and confidence; any product with a strong geometric mark.
- Doesn't: brands that need warmth or illustration; monochrome makes state and category color harder to introduce later.
