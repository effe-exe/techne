## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let one pro instrument scale from automatic to manual: an Automix mode for beginners and the full deck for experts, in the same interface.

## Why it works
- Hero shows the same deck UI across MacBook, iPad and iPhone: colour-coded stacked waveforms, BPM readouts (125.0), jog wheels and a cover-art library — dense but consistent across form factors.
- Body copy states the range: 'Mix your favorite music or use Automix for seamless automatic DJ mixes. From beginner to professional…' with Spotify and Apple Music integration named.
- Proof row of three: Apple Design Award Winner 2024 / All Platforms 'Mobile. Desktop. Spatial.' / Microsoft Store Award Winner 2024.
- Cookie dialog gives equal-weight ACCEPT ALL and DECLINE ALL buttons. Weakness: generic superlative headline 'The #1 DJ App' (Avenir 48/500).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: pro creative tools (audio, video, photo) that want a beginner on-ramp without a separate 'lite' app.
- Doesn't: tools where automation would undermine the user's craft identity without an obvious off switch.
