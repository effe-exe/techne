## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Launch a sub-brand with its own voice but a clear endorsement line — 'A new browser from Perplexity' above the headline — and show the assistant as a panel docked beside a real-looking web page rather than a chat window.

## Why it works
- Condensed display serif (GT Canon, up to 90px) 'The browser that works for you' centred on a warm paper rgb(251,250,244) ground, with grainy teal and rust spheres cropped at the edges as the only ornament.
- Endorsement eyebrow 'A new browser from Perplexity' (underlined link) and a platform line 'Available for Mac, Windows, iOS, and Android' precede one dark pill 'Download Comet' with a circular icon well and a halo ring.
- Below the fold-line, a desktop and phone mock of a magazine page ('Haute Journal — The Spring 2026 Trend Report') with a frosted 'Assistant' panel rising over it — the browser's job is shown in context.
- Palette: ink rgb(9,23,23) at 75/64% alpha, teal download pill top-right; 16-54px radii. Caveat: no transitions or prefers-reduced-motion CSS detected at load.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: product-line extensions and sub-brands; AI features that live alongside existing content (browsers, readers, email).
- Doesn't: brand-new companies without a parent to endorse them; condensed serifs lose legibility at small sizes.
