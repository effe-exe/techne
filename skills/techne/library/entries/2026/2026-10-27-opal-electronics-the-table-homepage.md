## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Tell the company story as one photographed object: a top-down shot of a table holding every prototype frames a plain-text manifesto column.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed top-down photo of a glass table covered in Opal cameras, PCBs, colour-tube samples and a hand holding chrome parts; a centred text column floats over its quiet middle.
- The copy is an essay, not a pitch: 'the table' (Die Grotesk C bold, ~49px), dated 'June 1, 2026', then short paragraphs at 21px/1.1 in white ('In our office, there is a table…').
- Minimal chrome: only a stacked 'opal electronics' wordmark top-left and 7 links on the whole page (DNA). The photo is the navigation metaphor.
- Black ground, white and grey (#818181) text, 1.5s cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1) transitions; WebGL present, no reduced-motion CSS.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Hardware and craft brands, founder letters, company relaunches: any moment when the story matters more than the SKU.
- Doesn't: Shopping and comparison pages; the earlier award-winning Opal Tadpole product page is gone, so it cannot be studied as a product page any more.
