## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Write the cookie banner in your own voice and give 'Deny' the same box as 'Accept all' — honesty becomes part of the brand.

## Why it works
- Banner copy: 'Cookie banners are ugly, annoying, and make the internet worse. Unfortunately, we have to have one because of EU law…' with Accept all / Manage / Deny as three equal 150px bordered boxes.
- Monospace UI (GT America Mono Light/Bold, 285 combined uses) for nav and credits ('Words by Aria Schrecker') against Editor serif for titles — a print-like contrast.
- Warm paper ground rgb(255,247,244) with a mustard rgb(244,208,111) title plate overlapping blue-ink engraving art.
- Square corners throughout (one 8px radius on the page); 1px black rules separate nav rows.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any EU-facing product that wants consent to feel respectful; publications with a strong editorial voice.
- Doesn't: very long pages — this homepage is ~45,000px with 1,138 links.
