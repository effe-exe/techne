## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn a user's own usage data into a once-a-year, story-format, share-ready artifact — personal data as social currency drives organic reach.

## Why it works
- Reported results for Wrapped 2025: 200M engaged users within about 24 hours (vs ~62 hours in 2024), and 500M+ shares on day one (+41%) — engagement counted as viewing at least one story [moderate — company-reported, consistently cited].
- Format: full-screen tap-through stories with bold type and per-user stats (top artists, minutes, genres), each slide sized as a ready-to-post card; 2025 added social layers (Wrapped Party, Clubs, friend comparisons) per Spotify's newsroom.
- Live holding page (Oct 2026): giant ghosted '26' numerals behind '2026 Wrapped' in SpotifyMixUITitle at ~144px, copy 'Keep playing what you love, and we'll get in touch when it's ready', a green 'Listen now' pill and a QR panel to get the app — anticipation managed honestly with no fake date.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product with meaningful personal history (fitness, finance, learning, reading, dev tools 'year in code').
- Doesn't: sensitive data (health conditions, spending on certain categories) — default to private, let users choose what's shareable.
