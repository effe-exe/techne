## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Demonstrate the feature as the hero: render the editor with Focus Mode on (past sentences greyed, current paragraph black, blinking cyan caret) so the page itself is the proof.

## Why it works
- Under the tagline 'Keep your hands on the keys and your mind in the text.' the page shows a large editor card in iA's monospaced type: the previous paragraph in light grey, the active paragraph in black, a thick cyan rgb(0,178,255) caret at the end.
- Custom iASansDay at 57.8px/700 (-1.16px) for the title, 28.5px body at 1.45 line-height with ~40ch measure — a page built for reading rather than scanning.
- Chrome is almost nothing: 'iA / Writer' breadcrumb, How To, Support and a cyan 'GET' pill; background rgb(247,247,247) with a soft four-step 0.05 shadow lifting the editor card.
- DNA: 0.2s ease-in-out transitions; prefers-reduced-motion CSS present; separate iASansNight face exists for dark mode.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: writing, coding and note tools; any app with a 'focus' or 'zen' mode — show it, don't list it.
- Doesn't: collaborative docs where awareness of others' edits and comments is the core job.
