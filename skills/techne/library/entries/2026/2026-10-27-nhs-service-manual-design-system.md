## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Write consent like a person: 'I'm OK with analytics cookies' / 'Do not use analytics cookies' as equal buttons, with the vendor (Adobe Analytics) named in the explanation.

## Why it works
- Cookie banner explains in plain sentences what cookies do, names the analytics service, and offers two identical green rgb(0,127,59) buttons with a dark bottom edge.
- Top nav places 'Content guide' and 'Accessibility' beside 'Design system' and 'NHS service standard' — writing and inclusion are part of the system, not appendices.
- Frutiger 48/600 H1, 19/1.47 body as default; NHS blue rgb(0,94,184) header and links; pale grey-blue rgb(240,244,245) page; 4px radii only.
- No transitions detected; left nav groups Principles / Setup / Design with plain labels (Prototyping, Production code, Styles, Components, Patterns).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't

- Doesn't: brand-expressive consumer products.
