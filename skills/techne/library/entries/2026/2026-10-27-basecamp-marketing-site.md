## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Turn the homepage into an index of honest one-line answers: underlined link + em dash + plain claim ('Pricing & sign up — A free plan + paid upgrades').

## Why it works
- Right column is seven underlined blue links each followed by a short explanation (Pricing & sign up, Basecamp 5 is here, Features, Paths, API/CLI/Skills, Reliable to the core) — navigation and pitch in one list.
- A live-feeling count '102,641 people are working in Basecamp right now' replaces logo walls as social proof.
- Left column shows a full example project (Message Board, Logo Inspiration, Project Tasks, Assets & Proofs, Deadlines, Workflow) in a browser frame, with 'Check out more example projects' below.
- Headline in Graphik 42px/800 placed below the index; header is two sentences as buttons: 'Have an account? Sign in' and 'Want an account? Sign up free'.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any product whose visitors arrive with specific questions (pricing, migration, reliability); B2B with plain-spoken brand.
- Doesn't: Visual/emotional brands; the dense link list can overwhelm on mobile.
