## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Expose settings and sound controls from the first frame, even during the preloader, so visitors can tune quality and audio before the immersive experience starts.

## Why it works
- The preloader is culturally specific: a gold (rgb 246,206,160) scalloped rosette ring drawn around a progress counter ('34') in an ornamental display face (Maghfirea) on charcoal #252525.
- Two circular outline buttons (gear for settings, speaker for sound) sit bottom-right from the first frame, before any content loads.
- Meta copy states the promise plainly: 'Explore the capital of ancient Persia at its height during the reign of King Xerxes.' The museum (Getty wordmark top-left) lends credibility.
- Graphik for UI; easings 0.3–0.6s in-out sine/quad variants; WebGL. No reduced-motion CSS detected at load.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Museums, education and documentary experiences; any heavy WebGL or audio experience (settings-first is the transferable part).
- Doesn't: Quick-reference content; the full 3D walk-through was not observed in this capture (only the loader), so judge the main experience separately.
