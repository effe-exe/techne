## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the product greet the visitor: the device screen literally says 'Hello.' and the headline finishes the introduction in plain words.

## Why it works
- A large 3D yellow device render centred on cool grey; its screen shows a speech bubble 'Hello.'; a tilted sticker 'Season 3 GET IT NOW' adds the only promotional note.
- Headline in Roobert (~57–68px) 'It's a new, tiny handheld game system' with words alternating white and light grey for rhythm; nav items each carry a small glyph (Games, Dev, Education, Help) and a purple Display-P3 'Shop' pill.
- DNA: colours authored in color(display-p3 …); WebGL in use; prefers-reduced-motion CSS present; 800px text column on 178 elements.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Novel or playful hardware/software where personality is the selling point.
- Doesn't: Serious or regulated categories; whimsy can undercut trust.
