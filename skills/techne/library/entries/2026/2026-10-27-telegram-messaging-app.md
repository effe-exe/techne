## Why it works
- Homepage is almost a document: blue paper-plane mark, 'Telegram' and 'a new era of messaging' in grey, then two device mockups with 'Telegram for Android' / 'Telegram for iPhone / iPad' links; system font stack (-apple-system) at 20px body.
- Right rail 'Recent News' lists dated releases (Aug 25 'Welcome Messages, Buttons in Messages and Signed Gifts', Jul 14, Jun 11) — the changelog does the persuasion.
- App-side (documented): rich sticker and emoji animation and frequent feature drops; the site's mockups show the custom themes/backgrounds.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: products with a fast release cadence and an engaged user base; developer-adjacent consumer tools.
- Doesn't: brand-led launches needing emotional storytelling — the site is functional to the point of dated (4px radii, single blue rgb(0,136,204)).
