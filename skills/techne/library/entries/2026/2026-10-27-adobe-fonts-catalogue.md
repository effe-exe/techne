## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let users search by description ('Describe the font you're looking for') and credit the makers with a rotating 'Foundry Spotlight' row in an aggregator.

## Why it works
- The hero is a single wide pill search field with placeholder 'Describe the font you're looking for' and a blue 'Search' button under 'Find the perfect font' — the entry point is intent in the user's words rather than a taxonomy.
- First content row is 'Foundry Spotlight: Resistenza Type' with specimen cards (LeKick, Norman Variable, Superpop Variable, Gotti Variable), each captioned with family + foundry and 'View family' — the aggregator foregrounds independent makers.
- UI in adobe-clean 14–18px; 16px-radius outlined pills ('View more', 'Sign in'); short 0.125s ease-out transitions.
- Weakness: the red-to-blue gradient hero is generic Creative Cloud branding and adds nothing to choosing type.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Large catalogues and marketplaces where users know the feeling they want but not the name; aggregators that depend on third-party creators.
- Doesn't: Small catalogues where browsing everything is faster than describing.
