## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let variable-height images pack into a masonry grid so content density stays high without cropping, and give the search bar the widest slot in the header.

## Why it works
- Logged-out home: 'Create the life you love on Pinterest' in Pin Sans ~70px bold, tight tracking, beside a loose cluster of 16px-rounded pins of different aspect ratios; red rgb(230,0,35) 'Join Pinterest for free' paired with a warm-grey 'I already have an account' — new vs returning split in one row.
- Header gives search the widest element ('Search for easy dinners, fashion, etc.' in a warm-grey rgb(229,229,224) pill) — the product is discovery-by-query as much as by feed.
- Below: a timely 'Spooky season starts here' module with a rounded strip of pins — seasonal intent surfaced on the landing.
- DNA: 16px radius dominates (57 counts); text black and warm grey rgb(98,98,91).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: visual discovery (recipes, fashion, interiors, inspiration libraries, stock media).
- Doesn't: text-heavy or comparative content; masonry breaks predictable reading order for screen readers and keyboard users unless DOM order is managed.

type: Pin Sans 70/28 bold (-0.5px), 12–16 UI · color: white, warm greys rgb(229,229,224)/rgb(98,98,91), Pinterest red · shape: 16px pins/buttons, 32px modules · motion: minimal
