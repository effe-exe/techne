## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- End a campaign hero with two equal, audience-split exits ('Shop women's' / 'Shop men's') instead of one vague 'Shop now'.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed photo of a skier's spray; centred campaign line in Elan ITC caps 32 'SPEAK IN TURNS' and a single sentence 'The iconic Sidewinder jacket is back.'
- Two identical outline rectangles 'Shop women's' / 'Shop men's' split the next step by audience.
- A floating white nav card sits inset 40px over the photo; a black utility bar surfaces circular-economy paths ('OUTLET', 'RESALE', 'TRADE-IN'). Cookie bar offers only 'Continue' (caution).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Apparel and any catalogue with a primary audience split.
- Doesn't: Gendered splits don't fit unisex products; use the axis your catalogue actually has.
