## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the product's own colour-coded content supply the palette: a black-and-white page with pastel calendar blocks and floating category icons reads as 'your life, organised'.

## Why it works
- Short, confident headline 'It's time.' in NotionInter 64px/700 at -2.1px tracking above a three-line subhead; app icon and product name sit above it as a label.
- CTA pair is black solid ('Get Notion Calendar free', rgb(25,25,24), 8px radius) plus outlined 'Download for macOS' — platform-specific secondary action rather than 'Learn more'.
- Floating illustrated tiles (laptop, basketball, cat, checklist, bike, mug) each with a coloured left edge echo calendar event chips; pastel surfaces rgb(221,252,250), rgb(255,235,227), rgb(247,229,255) recur in the hero screenshot.
- Hero is a MacBook + iPhone mock of a real week view with play button for the film; 0.15-0.25s transitions; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Calendar, scheduling and planning tools; any product where user content is naturally colour-coded.
- Doesn't: Data-heavy B2B products with no friendly content to show; floating-icon collages easily become clip-art.
