## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- State the price under every destination card ('Free admission' / 'Ticket or Membership required') so the visitor's cost question is answered in the listing, not the detail page.

## Why it works
- Split hero: artwork crop left, solid cobalt rgb(8,65,183) panel right with a tracked caps exhibition title (Tate regular 58px, 6.38px letter-spacing ≈ 11%) and two white arrow buttons (Book now / Free for Members).
- 'OUR GALLERIES' row: four location cards each with a one-line access condition in grey under the name.
- Single custom family (Tate regular, 101 uses) with heavy tracking on caps headings and 1.6 line-height body at 16px/62ch.
- The halftone-blur TATE logo is the only decorative element in the header.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: multi-location venues, marketplaces, any listing where access/price varies per item.
- Doesn't: single-product pages.
