## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Number and date every story like a magazine issue (#226 · OCT 2026) and let each piece own one saturated colour field, so an archive reads as a collection, not a feed.

## Why it works
- Each card is a pill tag (#226) + mono date over a full-bleed colour field (magenta rgb(223,32,223), violet rgb(154,76,173), yellow rgb(251,205,81)) framing a dark screenshot of the actual interactive: the thumbnail shows the mechanism, not a stock image.
- Three-voice type system: Atlas Typewriter for UI/meta (65 uses, 12.8px uppercase buttons), Atlas Grotesk for deks (14–16px, grey rgb(109,109,109)), and a heavy lowercase Gooper SemiCondensed for titles — playful headline, sober reading text.
- Navigation is by intent rather than section: OUR FAVES / POPULAR / UPDATING / YOUR INPUT / VIDEO / AUDIO, each with a hand-drawn icon; 'Your input' flags participatory pieces up front.
- Restrained motion (0.25s ease transitions) with prefers-reduced-motion CSS present; 28px radii on pill tags are the only rounding.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any archive of long-form or interactive work (case studies, research, changelogs) where each item deserves an identity.
- Doesn't: high-frequency news where per-item art direction can't be produced.
