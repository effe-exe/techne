## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the programme itself the navigation: full-width ruled rows of 104px caps headlines with a thumbnail and an arrow, readable as a poster and clickable as a list.

## Why it works
- Wordmark spans the full 1440px width; below it each row (WHAT'S ON / YAYOI KUSAMA → EXHIBITION / ADAM PENDLETON…) is Union Regular 104px uppercase at line-height 0.95 between 6px black rules.
- Single family, single weight (Union Regular, 51 uses), near-black rgb(10,10,10) on white; the only colours are a neon-green 'Book ticket' button and a rotated yellow 'Unbubble' sticker campaign.
- Utility icons for search and audio/accessibility sit in the first nav row beside Visit / What's on.
- Cookie panel offers 'Accepteren' and 'Noodzakelijke cookies' (necessary only) as equal outlined buttons.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: cultural programmes, event listings, conference schedules, portfolios with few items.
- Doesn't: long catalogues — 104px rows show only ~4 items per viewport.
