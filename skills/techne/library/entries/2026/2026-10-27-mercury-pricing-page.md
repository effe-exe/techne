## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Lead the tier grid with the free plan as the filled primary button and make paid tiers additive ('Everything in Plus, and more') — the free tier is the product, upgrades are increments.

## Why it works
- Headline "Pricing that fits your business" (57px arcadiaDisplay) is followed by the anchor fact "Banking services and essential tools are always $0/mo" — the price floor is stated before any tier.
- Three columns ($0, $29.90, $299/mo); only the $0 plan has a filled indigo CTA, paid plans get pale lavender buttons. Visual emphasis goes to the honest default, not the upsell.
- Annual toggle states its exact effect inline ("Annual Pricing (15% off)"); paid columns list deltas only, and the free column offers "Jump to section" anchors (Banking, Sending and receiving money, …) into a long comparison below.
- The FDIC disclosure bar persists here too, with underlined links to partner banks.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: freemium financial or SaaS products whose free tier is genuinely complete.
- Doesn't: products where the free tier is a teaser — emphasising it would mislead.
