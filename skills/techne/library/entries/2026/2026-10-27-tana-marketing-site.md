## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Show what the AI did, not what it is: the hero is a stack of finished outputs (an issue sent to Linear, a Gmail draft, a yes/no question) each with its own state chip.

## Why it works
- Serif headline 'Meetings that ship' in Source Serif 4 48px/500 (-2.2px tracking) with a three-line plain-English explanation of exactly what the product does during a call.
- Right-aligned primary CTA detects the visitor's OS ('Download for Linux') with 'No credit card required' directly under it — reassurance placed at the moment of commitment.
- Hero mock pairs a two-person video call with a 'Listening' side panel of action cards: 'Linear · Issue … Sent', 'Gmail · Draft … Send', 'Tana … Yes / ×' — state chips (black Sent, blue Send, black Yes) differentiate done vs pending.
- Soft lavender-to-peach gradient band behind the demo; Inter for UI at 12-15px; short 0.12-0.16s transitions; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Agentic/AI features where trust depends on seeing concrete outputs and approval states; any automation product.
- Doesn't: Products whose output is not easily shown as discrete artefacts; stock-photo call tiles are generic.
