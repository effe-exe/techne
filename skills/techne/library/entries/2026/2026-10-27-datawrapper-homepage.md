## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Remove the signup wall and say so under the CTA ('You can try it, no account needed'), then preview the 4-step workflow so users know the whole job before starting.

## Why it works
- Centered 56px/700 Roboto headline 'Create better charts, maps, and tables with ease', 20px grey subline, a single teal #007DA0-range 'Start creating →' button and the microcopy 'You can try it, no account needed.'
- Real published charts (line charts with annotations, a choropleth of Europe, donut small-multiples, a beeswarm map, a bar chart) float around the headline as a collage, in the muted editorial palettes newsrooms actually use.
- Below the fold the app frame shows a numbered stepper: 1 Upload Data → 2 Check & Describe → 3 Visualize → 4 Publish, with 'How do you want to upload your data?' — the workflow is previewed before commitment.
- Cookie dialog offers 'Reject non-essential' and 'Accept all' as equal-width buttons, plus 'Manage cookie preferences'. Shadows are a faint rgba(0,0,0,.04) 0 1px 2px; radii 4px.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any tool with a learnable multi-step flow (import → configure → publish) and a free tier that can skip signup.
- Doesn't: Products that genuinely need an account before value (banking, health records).
