## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A motion product that honours prefers-reduced-motion and explains itself in four noun-plus-benefit chips (Tiny, Motion Tokens, Interactive, Dev ready) earns trust from both designers and engineers.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Great designs come alive with motion!' at 96px DM Sans 500 with -4.8px tracking (-5%), next to a cast of animated character shapes (robot, star, blobs) in saturated toy colours: the illustration demonstrates the format.
- A row of four 16px-radius chips with coloured icon tiles states the format's value in two lines each: 'Tiny — file format but mighty', 'Motion Tokens — bind properties to real data', 'Interactive — for delightful experiences', 'Dev ready — and easy to implement'.
- The palette is defined in OKLCH (near-black oklch(0.141 …), greys oklch(0.552/0.705 …)) with a single teal CTA; transitions use cubic-bezier(0.4,0,0.2,1) at 0.15–0.3s across 188 nodes, and reduced-motion CSS is present.
- Conventional SaaS layout otherwise (Products/Integrations/Tools/Customers/Learn/Pricing, ⌘K search, language switch).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Motion/animation products, creative tools and any brand whose product is movement: show it moving, but ship the reduced-motion path.
- Doesn't: Serious B2B where cartoon mascots would undercut credibility.
