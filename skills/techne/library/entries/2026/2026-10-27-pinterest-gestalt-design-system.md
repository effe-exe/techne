## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the design system's landing page show the product's own content shape — rounded pin cards, toggles, buttons — floating around the title, so the system's vocabulary is visible before you read a word.

## Why it works
- Centred 'Pinterest's Design System' in pinSans 70/900 at -0.7px, surrounded by a collage of rounded (28px) pin-ratio photos plus live-looking primitives — a blue toggle, a 'Gestalt' button, a pin icon — the system's components used as illustration.
- Header: moon icon (dark-mode toggle), grey 'Gestalt 1.0 ↗' pill and a Pinterest red rgb(230,0,35) 'Login' with 16px radius; warm grey rgb(246,246,243)/rgb(229,229,224) section cards ('Get Started', 'Foundations', 'Components').
- Lock icons on the section cards at capture suggest parts of the current docs now sit behind login, with 1.0 still public.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: design-system and developer-docs landing pages; brands whose content has a distinctive shape (pins, cards, tiles).
- Doesn't: if the docs are gated, outside teams can't learn from them — prefer the open-source repo and 1.0 docs as reference.
