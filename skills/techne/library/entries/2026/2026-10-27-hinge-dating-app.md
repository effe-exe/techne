## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Define success as the user leaving with what they came for ('designed to be deleted'), and design profiles around prompts that start conversations rather than swipes.

## Why it works
- Full-bleed documentary-style photo of a couple at a fountain; 'The dating app designed to be deleted™' in Tiempos Headline 58/600, white, bottom-left — an editorial serif in a category dominated by rounded sans.
- Navigation is Mission / Impact / Labs / Newsroom / Careers — the site leads with purpose and research (Hinge Labs) rather than features or download buttons.
- Off-white rgb(255,254,253) base, aubergine rgb(103,41,95) accent, Modern Era for UI; 9999px black pills.
- Product (documented): profiles built from photos + answered prompts, likes attached to a specific prompt or photo — a conversation-starter model versus pure swipe.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: products where the healthy outcome is less usage (job search, dating, debt payoff, therapy) — align metrics and messaging to the outcome.
- Doesn't: verify the business model honours the slogan — subscription tiers and 'Standouts'-style paid likes can pull against it.
