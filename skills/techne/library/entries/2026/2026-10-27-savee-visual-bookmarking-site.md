## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Embed small image tiles inside the headline between words so the copy itself previews the content the product holds.

## Why it works
- Headline 'The best place [tile] to save [tile] and share inspiration [tile]' at 95.8px/500 in a custom grotesk (saveeFont) with -3.35px tracking (-3.5%); three ~85px rounded image tiles (a device, a figure in red, a blue chair) sit inline like words.
- One-sentence explanation in 21px grey: 'References curated by designers, saved in one click and sorted into boards.' then a black 'Join for free' pill and a grey #E4E4E4 'See plans' pill.
- Near-grayscale chrome (#FDFDFD / #F3F3F3 ground, #050505 to #A3A3A3 text) so saved imagery supplies all colour; a 'Get the app — Scan with your phone' card bottom-right hands off to mobile.
- Easing mix of cubic-bezier(0,0,0.2,1) on 123 nodes and expo-out cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1) on 72 nodes.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Content-collection products (bookmarks, moodboards, media libraries, marketplaces) where the user's content is the value.
- Doesn't: Abstract or B2B products with no representative imagery to inline.
