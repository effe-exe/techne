## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the app's own balance screen be the hero, centred like a poster, with the headline and the explanation pushed to opposite edges — the product is the brand.

## Why it works
- One-viewport page (1,440px tall, 66 links): a saturated green field rgb(0,224,19), a centred phone showing real UI ("Cash balance $789.13", Add money / Withdraw, Savings "$980.00 — $20 to goal", Borrow "$150.00 available"), headline left, explainer + CTA right. Three columns, no scroll needed to understand it.
- Cash Sans at 56px weight 400, line-height 0.95, -1.68px (-3%) tracking for the headline "The way money should work" — light weight on loud colour keeps it confident, not shouty.
- Controls are white/black pills (999px radius, 12px text) top-right; motion 0.3–0.6s ease-in-out, prefers-reduced-motion rules present.
- Caveat: the cookie notice offers only a close X and a "Cookie Preferences" link — no equal-weight reject (contrast with Monzo).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: consumer apps with a single signature screen; brands with an ownable colour.
- Doesn't: multi-product platforms that need navigation depth; pure-green fields need careful text contrast (black text works here, white would not).
