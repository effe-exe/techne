## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Let the content float: dozens of tilted, softly-shadowed image tiles drift around a single search field, so the hero is both the brand and a preview of what you will find.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Find your next art obsession' in custom cosmosOracle 72px/700 at -2.9px tracking with an inline image pair after 'obsession'.
- The primary interaction is a pill search field ('Search for art') with a colour-dot button beside it, then a black pill 'Join Cosmos for free' — search before sign-up.
- ~40 artwork tiles rendered as tilted 3D cards with thickness and soft shadows on warm grey lab(95) — depth without a busy background; colours are defined in lab()/oklab().
- Bottom-right 'Get the app' card carries a QR code for desktop visitors; WebGL present with reduced-motion CSS; 0.4s / 0.15s transitions.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Visual discovery, media libraries, template galleries — products whose inventory is the hook.
- Doesn't: Text- or data-centric products; floating tiles can become noise without strong curation.
