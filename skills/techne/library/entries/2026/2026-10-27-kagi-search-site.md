## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- State the business model in the headline — 'Better search results. No ads.' — and the trial limit right under the CTA ('The first 50 searches are on us'): honesty about who pays is the trust signal.

## Why it works
- Centred Lufga 60/600 headline of six words, two-line 20px explanation of how it works ('We pull from the leading search engines, then re-rank everything and filter out spam'), one dark pill 'Try it now'.
- Quiet grey line beneath the CTA discloses the free-trial cap before sign-up — no surprise paywall later.
- A line-drawn dog mascot with an orange ball gives warmth without imagery overhead; white ground, ink rgb(24,24,26), grey rgb(69,69,73); 48px and 999px radii.
- Fold is uncluttered (no cookie wall, no promo bar); prefers-reduced-motion CSS present; transitions 0.15-0.4s standard ease.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: subscription products competing with free, ad-funded incumbents; any product whose model is itself a differentiator.
- Doesn't: products with a free core where 'no ads' isn't distinctive; mascots can undercut enterprise credibility.
