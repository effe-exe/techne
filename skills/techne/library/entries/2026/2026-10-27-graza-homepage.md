## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Pair a condensed serif headline with typewriter-mono UI labels to make a grocery item feel crafted and a little funny.

## Why it works
- Pour-shot video full-bleed; headline 'Extra Virgin Olive Oil makes everything better.' in ITC Garamond Condensed 72 (-2.16px).
- Nav and buttons in GT Alpina Typewriter ('Shop', 'Get Refills', 'Glog', 'Cart [0]'); the CTA is a chartreuse rgb(209,224,48) pill with a hard offset shadow.
- Peach rgb(246,230,217) page colour and Apercu body; 'Get Refills' surfaces the subscription/refill model in top nav.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Consumer packaged goods, food and hospitality brands.
- Doesn't: Typewriter mono for long body text; keep it to labels.
