## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- State the hard requirement right under the download CTA ('Requires macOS Sequoia (15.0.0) or newer') so nobody downloads something that will not run.

## Why it works
- 'Designers, welcome home.' in Reckless serif at 76px/500, tracking -1.5px, on a soft lilac-pink gradient; the positioning line opposite: 'a toolkit made by designers, for designers'.
- The single dark #151515 'Get started for free ↓' pill has a pink-to-violet glow ring and the grey line 'Requires macOS Sequoia (15.0.0) or newer' beneath it: honest gating at the moment of decision.
- A white 20px-radius announcement card ('Now in beta: Component variants and sections ... Try it today') sits between hero and the real app screenshot (toolbar, Documents/Pages sidebar, inspector with X/Y fields, collaborator avatars '+3').
- InterVariable for 101 UI nodes; easing cubic-bezier(0.455,0.03,0.515,0.955) (ease-in-out-quad) at 0.125s on 59 nodes.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Native or platform-limited software (desktop apps, hardware-dependent tools) and anything with prerequisites users must meet.
- Doesn't: Web apps with no prerequisites; the line would be noise.
