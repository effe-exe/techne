## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Stagger a three-word headline across the grid and tuck small underlined taglines into the gaps: one composition carries both the brand statement and its two proof points.

## Why it works
- 'global / creative / studio.' in Beatrice Display Light at 105px, lowercase, stepped diagonally across a near-black ground. Two underlined 18px taglines sit in the negative space ('we turn aesthetics into experiences', 'tech that's light as air').
- Lowercase nav in Biotif at 17px (home, case studies, what we do, who we are, news, contact), with the current page dimmed to grey rather than underlined.
- The cookie banner offers 'ALLOW ALL', 'DENY' and 'SHOW DETAILS' as three identical outlined pills: honest consent in an award-grade site.
- Pill-heavy UI (9999px radius ×41); expo-like cubic-bezier(0.26,1,0.48,1) at 0.8s; WebGL present and prefers-reduced-motion honoured.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Studio and brand homepages; any hero that must hold a statement plus two supporting claims without a paragraph.
- Doesn't: Long headlines (more than about 4 words breaks the staircase).
