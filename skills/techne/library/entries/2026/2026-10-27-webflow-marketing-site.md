## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Personalise inside a sentence: 'What [marketing teams ▾] love about Webflow' — an inline dropdown swaps the benefit cards for each audience.

## Why it works
- Headline 'Build for what's next' in WF Visual Sans Variable 80px/600 left-aligned; blue rgb(19,102,226)-family 'Get started' + grey 'Talk to Sales' rectangles.
- Right-aligned inline select in the sentence 'What marketing teams love about Webflow' drives a row of benefit cards (scheduled publishing, '40% scroll this far' analytics, avatars for collaboration, visibility-score table).
- Cookie panel gives 'Reject all' and 'Accept all' identical size (outlined vs filled) plus 'Manage Preferences'.
- A persistent 'Made in Webflow' badge bottom-right — the site is its own case study. 0.3s transitions; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Multi-audience B2B products; any landing that otherwise lists 'For X / For Y / For Z' tabs.
- Doesn't: Single-audience products; hidden personalisation can confuse if the default choice is wrong.
