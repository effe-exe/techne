## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make the spatial information accessible, not just the buttons: speak pitch, chord guidance and exact finger-on-fret placement through VoiceOver so a blind player can learn independently.

## Why it works
- Apple's citation: 'Robust VoiceOver integration provides spoken information and instructions on everything from pitch to chord guidance to where players should place their fingers on the frets.'
- Also supports Dynamic Type, Increased Contrast and Differentiate Without Color — 'to enable people of all abilities to play with autonomy'; built with SwiftUI by solo developer Bijoy Thangaraj (India).
- App Store accessibility labels (via search summary): VoiceOver, Larger Text, Dark Interface, Differentiate Without Colour Alone, Sufficient Contrast, Reduced Motion; developer reports no data collected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any product whose core content is visual-spatial (diagrams, maps, charts, instrument layouts): describe the spatial relationship in words and audio.
- Doesn't: —; the principle is broadly applicable, though audio guidance needs careful verbosity settings.
