## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A single-hue system (one red on black, mono everything else) can carry a whole expressive identity: the constraint reads as confidence and keeps the site light.

## Why it works
- Everything is one red (lab 49.9 73.4 57, about #E61E1E) on pure black: the 200px 'therma' display headline 'WHERE THINGS GET DEVELOPED' (line-height 0.8, -10px tracking, uppercase), nav tabs on dark-red fills, and the 'MANIFESTO →' button.
- Body and UI are mono at 14px uppercase (185 of about 200 text nodes), with a dictionary-entry block as positioning copy: '[ DARKROOM ], NOUN — 1. A lightproof room for developing photographs. 2. A studio engineering creativity into reality.'
- Case studies interrupt the red system with full-colour imagery (an 'OREO & BTS' neon street scene) under 120px red titles, so the work is the only other colour on the page.
- Zero border radius, prefers-reduced-motion honoured, no WebGL on the homepage, and a persistent 'BECOME AN OPEN SOURCE SPONSOR' bar that matches their Lenis maintenance.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Studios, dev tools and any brand that wants a strong identity at low production cost: one hue plus one mono face.
- Doesn't: Products with semantic colour needs (errors, status), since red-on-black leaves no room for a warning colour; long-form reading in all-caps mono.
