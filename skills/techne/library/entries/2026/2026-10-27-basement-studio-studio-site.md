## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Superscript counts in the nav ('Showcase (26)', 'Blog (29)') tell visitors how much sits behind each door before they click.

## Why it works
- The hero is a navigable real-time 3D model of the studio's own space: stairs, an orange-lit arcade cabinet, a 'BSMNT SHOT!' basketball hoop, a neon 'basement.' sign, a CRT wall and a dog. Culture shown, not claimed.
- Plain top nav in Geist 12/600 with superscript counts: 'Showcase (26)', 'Blog (29)'. At the bottom centre is a 'HUMAN | MACHINE' segmented toggle with an orange active state (its function was not tested).
- Geist 87/600 h1 at -3.48px tracking (-4%) and line-height 0.9; black ground, greys #E6E6E6 / #C4C4C4 / #757575. Prefers-reduced-motion is honoured even with WebGL on.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Studios and teams selling culture; the nav counts transfer to any docs, blog or catalogue navigation.
- Doesn't: Low-power mobile devices, or teams without 3D capacity; the scene is the expensive part.
