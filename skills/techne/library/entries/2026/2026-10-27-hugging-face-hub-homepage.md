## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use the real faceted browser as the hero: tasks, a parameter-size slider, libraries, apps and inference providers beside a live list of models with update times, downloads and likes — the catalogue's breadth is the pitch.

## Why it works
- Left: hugging-face emoji mascot, Source Sans Pro 48/700 'The AI community building the future.', 18px subhead, outline 'Explore AI Apps' pill with an 'or Browse 2M+ models' text link — two clear exits.
- Right: a tilted dark panel replicating the Models page — filter tabs (Main, Tasks, Libraries, Languages, Licenses), task chips, a 1B→500B parameter slider, framework chips (PyTorch, JAX, GGUF, MLX), app and provider chips, and 'Models 2,555,000' with real entries (e.g. moonshotai/Kimi-K2.5 — 'Updated 2 days ago', downloads, likes).
- Nav pairs a prominent search ('Search models, datasets, users…') with Models / Datasets / Spaces / Buckets 'NEW' / Docs; IBM Plex Mono for repo ids; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: marketplaces, registries and catalogues (packages, templates, datasets, plugins) — show filter power and freshness.
- Doesn't: products with small catalogues, where an empty-ish browser undermines credibility.
