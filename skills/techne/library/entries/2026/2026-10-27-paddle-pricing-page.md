## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Set the price itself as display type ('5% + 50¢' in a big serif) inside a card that lists everything the fee includes — an all-inclusive number reads as confidence.

## Why it works
- "Our pricing" in serrifCondensed at 64px (lh 1.0, -1% tracking), subline "…at an all-inclusive price. No added costs, no hidden fees." Two beige rgb(235,231,223) cards with 32px radius: "Pay-as-you-go" and "Custom pricing".
- The pay-as-you-go card lists four inclusions with line icons (payments/checkout/billing, global tax compliance, fraud protection & chargebacks, no monthly or migration fees) and then shows the rate "5% + 50¢" as display-size serif — inclusions first, number second.
- Cookie settings panel: four category toggles all default OFF, and "Reject all" and "Accept all" are identical black pills.
- Warm off-white rgb(249,248,247), ink rgb(28,26,21), Inter for UI; motion 0.16–0.3s ease-out; prefers-reduced-motion rules present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: bundled services (merchant of record, payroll, managed hosting) where the customer must understand what the fee replaces.
- Doesn't: if your base rate is high, the bundle explanation must come before the number (as here) or it reads as expensive.
