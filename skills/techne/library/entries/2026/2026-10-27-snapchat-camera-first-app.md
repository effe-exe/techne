## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Open on the creative tool (the camera), not the feed, so every session starts with making rather than consuming.

## Why it works
- Documented app model: launches straight into the camera; Stories (ephemeral, tap-through) and Lenses originated here and were copied across the industry.
- Web: icon tab bar mirrors the app (Stories / Spotlight / Chat 'NEW' / Lenses / Snapchat+), a plain 'Log in to Snapchat' form with blue rgb(0,150,229) 100px pill, and tall 20px-radius cards of Lens selfies with dark translucent 'Find Your Friends' / 'Try Now' chips.
- A persistent bottom bar addresses parents ('Learn what we're doing to help keep Snapchatters safe') — safety messaging given a permanent slot.
- Avenir Next throughout (48/600, 28/700, 14 body).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: creation tools (photo, video, notes, design) — default to the canvas.
- Doesn't copy: Snapstreaks — daily-exchange counters that teens report feeling pressured to maintain; streak mechanics for social obligations convert habit into anxiety.
