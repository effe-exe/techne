## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Use one bespoke typeface at a few large sizes with only black, white and a single blue for primary action — let the art supply all other colour.

## Why it works
- ABC Walker is used for everything (251 uses) at 72/60/48/32/24/18 — a large, even scale with line-height 1.0 for heads and 1.5 for 24px intro text.
- Header: left utilities (search, What's On, Visit, Art & Artists), centred WALKER wordmark, right Join & Give / Shop / outlined 'Tickets' pill (50px radius).
- Hero image is inset 44px from the edges with date range bottom-left and category bottom-right in white — a gallery-label convention.
- Transitions 0.15–0.4s with expo-out cubic-bezier(0.16,1,0.3,1); reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: cultural and luxury brands where content imagery varies wildly.
- Doesn't: white title text over a light sculpture photo was hard to read at capture — needs a scrim for light images.
