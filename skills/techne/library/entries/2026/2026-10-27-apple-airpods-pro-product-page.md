## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Every autoplaying hero gets a visible pause control, and price plus Buy stay one glance away while the story scrolls.

## Why it works
- The hero video (an AirPod drifting through iridescent concentric rings) has a circular pause button pinned top-right, so the motion is user-controllable from the first frame.
- A frosted pill bottom-right shows '$249' and a blue 'Buy' button and stays with the reader while the page tells the story (28,918px tall).
- Hierarchy in two lines: 'AirPods Pro 3' at 28/600, then the claim 'The world's best in-ear Active Noise Cancellation.' at 64/600 (-0.58px tracking), then a footnoted qualifier 'Up to 2x more than AirPods Pro 2.¹' (claims are footnoted).
- SF Pro Text/Display only; 28px card radius (69 elements); prefers-reduced-motion honoured; 0.1–0.32s easings.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Any product page with video or animation; commerce pages where the story is long but the purchase must stay reachable.
- Doesn't: Low-consideration products, where a 29k-pixel story is overkill.
