## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give the graphics desk its own dark showcase: one 'Latest project' split card, then a rail of painted key art — treat visual journalism as a portfolio, not a section of the news river.

## Why it works
- Charcoal ground rgb(39,41,45) with LATEST PROJECT and FEATURED PROJECTS as tracked caps section rules.
- Latest card: BUSINESS kicker, Merriweather 30/900 title ('Going Global 100'), 16px Roboto dek, byline/date in small grey caps, and an outlined 'Read more' button beside a data-map key image.
- Featured rail uses commissioned painted illustrations (horse, typhoon street, subdivided flat) rather than chart thumbnails.
- Two families only: Merriweather for titles, Roboto for UI.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: any org with a flagship content type (research, reports, case studies) that deserves its own hub.
- Doesn't: no reduced-motion CSS found; carousel arrow is the only overflow cue.
