## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Give developers their own install path beside the main CTA: 'Download for Mac' plus 'Install via Homebrew | Download V1' and the exact requirement ('macOS Tahoe and Apple Silicon required') in small monospace.

## Why it works
- Centered headline 'Your shortcut to everything.' in white over a single bold brand image — glowing red diagonal light streaks on near-black — no product screenshot needed above the fold.
- One light-grey pill CTA 'Download for Mac' (Apple glyph) at the bottom of the fold; beneath it, monospace small print states requirements and alternatives — the technical audience gets precise facts, not marketing.
- Floating rounded nav bar lists platforms (iOS, Windows) and Store/Pro/AI/Teams as peers — the extension store is a first-class destination.
- Subhead sells qualities in three words: 'Fast, ergonomic and reliable.'

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Developer and power-user tools; any download whose requirements commonly cause support tickets.
- Doesn't: Consumer audiences who don't know what Homebrew is (keep alternatives secondary, as Raycast does).
