## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Publish your operating philosophy as a product-grade page and switch voice through type: a 128px serif marks 'this is a manifesto' while the nav stays in the product's UI sans.

## Why it works
- 'Practices for building' in Tiempos Headline 128px/400, line-height 1.0, centered under a small caps-style 'THE LINEAR METHOD' label — the only serif on linear.app, so the register shift is unmistakable.
- Three-line intro at 17px/1.6 with a 48ch measure in grey rgb(138,143,152); otherwise the page reuses the marketing site's exact chrome (same nav, pill buttons, rgb(8,9,10) background).
- Illustration is restrained line art (two overlapping dashed circles with a hatched intersection) — conceptual, not decorative.
- 0.1s transitions with the same easing as the homepage; reduced-motion CSS present.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Company principles, design-system philosophy pages, onboarding essays, changelog intros.
- Doesn't: Brands without a genuine point of view — a manifesto without substance reads as pretension.
