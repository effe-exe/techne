## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- A serif display face plus one saturated highlight (yellow underline on 'work for you') can make B2B software feel human — but don't stack a quiz modal and a cookie bar on first load.

## Why it works
- First load opens a personalisation dialog 'Make Mailchimp work for you' in Means Web serif (64/400, -1.2px) with a yellow underline highlight; it asks for industry and up to 4 goals, with an outline 'No thank you' pill beside a yellow 'Customize my experience'.
- Simultaneously a dark cookie bar sits at the bottom and an Intuit family bar sits at the top — three interruptions before the homepage (caution).
- DNA: Means Web + Tally; ink rgb(35,30,21); radii 4/16/26px; spring easing cubic-bezier(0.5,2.5,0.7,…) for overshoot on hover.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Brands that want warmth via type and one signature colour; self-serve products that can personalise from 2 questions.
- Doesn't: Asking questions before showing value; first-visit modals hurt people who came for one thing.
