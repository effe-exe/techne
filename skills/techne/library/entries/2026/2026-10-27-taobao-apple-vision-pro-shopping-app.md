## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Make comparison a first-class gesture: let shoppers place two products side by side at true scale instead of flipping between product pages.

## Why it works
- Apple's jury citation (ADA 2025 Interaction): 3D models "comparable to their real-life counterparts", designed with "placement, position, controls, size, and function" in mind, and the ability to "compare items side by side from an extensive selection of products". [award — weak as performance evidence]
- The lesson is structural rather than visual: the purchase decision's hardest step (comparing size/fit/finish) is turned into a spatial interaction instead of a spec table.
- Deliberate diversity pick: a Chinese mass-market commerce platform winning an interaction award shows "great" is not one Western-minimalist aesthetic.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: furniture, electronics, fashion accessories — anything where scale and finish drive returns; AR "view in your room" features on phones.
- Doesn't: commodity or low-consideration purchases; teams without 3D asset pipelines.
