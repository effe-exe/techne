## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Promote the guarantee to the top bar ('FREE SHIPPING OVER $149 • LIFETIME WARRANTY • 30-DAY RETURNS') so risk reversal is the first thing read.

## Why it works
- Black utility bar states shipping threshold, lifetime warranty and 30-day returns; the hero poster repeats it as 'GUARANTEED FOR LIFE.'
- Split hero: left an orange illustrated poster with cases labelled 'NEW'; right a black panel with tracked-caps kicker, 64px caps headline and a plain sub ('Full grain leather. New Colors. With or without SlimLink. Even works with iPhone 17 Pro/Max.').
- Search field with an example placeholder ('Search for packing cubes'). Cookie bar copy is self-aware but offers only 'Cookies Settings' and a highlighted 'ACCEPT ALL' (caution).

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: Durable goods with real guarantees; any store where returns anxiety blocks purchase.
- Doesn't: Only state guarantees you honour; vague 'satisfaction guaranteed' does the opposite.
