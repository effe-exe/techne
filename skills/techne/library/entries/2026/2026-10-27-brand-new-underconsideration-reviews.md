## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Show change as a fixed BEFORE / AFTER pair inside every card, with a one-word status tag (Reviewed / Noted / Linked) telling readers how deep the coverage goes.

## Why it works
- Each card: context line ('New Logo and Identity for White & Case by Uncommon Creative Studio'), red status word right-aligned, a pun headline in bold, then two equal white panels captioned BEFORE and AFTER.
- Day headers in red ('Monday October 26, 2026') group posts like a diary.
- Like count and comment count in small tracked caps under each card; ABCSocial (181 uses) for UI, Alaska for headings.
- Left rail sells subscription with a concrete artefact ('A 2-page PDF with some objective reasons to the value of a Brand New subscription').

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: changelogs, redesign case studies, product comparisons, A/B results.
- Doesn't: a large third-party display ad sat above the content at capture.
