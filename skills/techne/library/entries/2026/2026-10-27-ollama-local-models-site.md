## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Say the privacy promise in the second sentence and keep the whole homepage on one screen: claim, 'Your prompts are never stored or trained on', one button, three numbers, a logo grid — restraint as credibility.

## Why it works
- The page height is 900px — the entire homepage fits one 1440×900 viewport: 22px/500 system-font claim, 20px body, a black pill 'Get started', a stat row (9M+ installs a month · 1B+ model downloads · 200T+ tokens served) and a 12-logo grid in grey.
- Copy leads with privacy and choice: 'Access the latest open models with complete privacy locally or in the cloud. Your prompts are never stored or trained on.'
- Nav shows the GitHub star count (184.9K) as social proof next to Download / Models / Docs / Pricing; line-drawn llama mark, pure white, oklch greys, zero transitions detected.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: developer tools, utilities, privacy-first products with strong word of mouth.
- Doesn't: products that need education or visual demonstration; the 22px headline would be too quiet for an unknown brand.
