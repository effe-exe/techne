## What to steal (principle, 1–3 bullets)
- Put the product's first action in the landing hero — pickup, dropoff, 'See prices' — so the marketing page is already the app and value (price) comes before sign-up.

## Why it works
- Black hero: geolocated 'Wichita, US · Change city', 'Go anywhere' in UberMove 52/700, a 'Pickup now' time pill, then a stacked Pickup / Dropoff input pair joined by a thin connector line (the route metaphor drawn in the form), and a white 'See prices' button — prices before any account.
- Secondary path is a text link ('Log in to check your activity'), not a competing button; right side is a 2×3 photo grid with an overlay 'Ready to travel? Schedule ahead' pill.
- Black/white palette with rgb(17,17,17) 999px pill buttons and 8px cards; UberMoveText 14–18 for UI. Cookie notice offers 'Opt out' beside 'Got it' as a peer button.

## Where it applies / where it doesn't
- Applies: transactional consumer products with one dominant intent (ride, delivery, booking, transfer quote).
- Doesn't: products whose value needs explanation before input; multi-intent platforms (Uber pushes Eats/Business into the top nav for that reason).
