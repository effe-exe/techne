# Seed Gallery: Exemplary Products to Learn From

> **Use this when:** you need concrete, real-world exemplars for a category (fintech, productivity, health, commerce, media, dev tools, AI, onboarding, paywalls, portfolios, design systems, data viz).
> **Use this when:** you want "what to steal" as a principle, not a screenshot to copy.
> **Do not use as evidence of performance:** these are craft exemplars; validate adopted patterns with data (see data-driven references).

---

## How to read this gallery

- Each entry: **Name** · URL · **Steal:** the transferable principle · `tags`.
- Entries were chosen for durable, widely recognised craft. Products change constantly: **re-verify the live product before citing a specific screen**, and note the date you saw it.
- "Steal" means abstract the principle (see `inspiration-method.md` §8). Never copy art, copy, or brand assets.
- ⚠ marks entries that are *also* useful as cautionary examples.
- Gallery last curated: 2026-10 (seed). Grow it with `library-update.md`.

Tag vocabulary (keep consistent): `platform:{ios,android,web,mac,cross}` · `surface:{landing,onboarding,paywall,pricing,dashboard,settings,checkout,editor,feed,docs,empty-state,search,data-viz,portfolio,brand}` · `quality:{typography,motion,density,clarity,trust,delight,accessibility,performance,keyboard,copy}` · `domain:{fintech,productivity,health,commerce,media,devtools,ai,education,travel,social,government}`

## 1. Fintech

1. **Stripe** · stripe.com · **Steal:** explain a complex product with layered progressive disclosure — headline → animated diagram → code sample → docs; gradient-as-brand without sacrificing readability. `platform:web surface:landing quality:typography,motion domain:fintech`
2. **Stripe Docs** · docs.stripe.com · **Steal:** three-pane docs with live, personalised code samples (your test keys inline) and language switcher that persists. Docs as product. `surface:docs quality:clarity domain:devtools,fintech`
3. **Wise** · wise.com · **Steal:** radical fee transparency — show the exchange rate, fee and arrival time *before* commitment; the comparison with banks is the pitch. Trust through numbers. `surface:pricing,checkout quality:trust,clarity domain:fintech`
4. **Monzo** · monzo.com · **Steal:** plain, warm, human copy in a regulated domain; instant transaction notifications with merchant logos turn raw data into recognisable events. `platform:ios,android quality:copy,trust domain:fintech`
5. **Revolut** · revolut.com · **Steal:** dense multi-product app kept navigable via strong hierarchy and a consistent card system; ⚠ also study feature sprawl and upsell density as a caution. `platform:ios,android surface:dashboard quality:density domain:fintech`
6. **Mercury** · mercury.com · **Steal:** premium, calm B2B banking aesthetic — restrained palette, editorial type, product screenshots as the hero. `platform:web surface:landing,dashboard quality:typography,trust domain:fintech`
7. **Ramp** · ramp.com · **Steal:** value-quantified marketing ("save X%") paired with fast, keyboard-friendly finance ops UI. `platform:web surface:landing,dashboard domain:fintech`
8. **Copilot Money** · copilot.money · **Steal:** native-feeling iOS finance with delightful categorisation review and charts that stay legible on a phone. `platform:ios,mac surface:dashboard,data-viz quality:delight domain:fintech`
9. **Cash App** · cash.app · **Steal:** single-purpose home screen (a keypad) — the primary action *is* the interface; bold brand within extreme simplicity. `platform:ios,android quality:clarity domain:fintech`
10. ⚠ **Robinhood** · robinhood.com · **Steal:** simplification of a complex domain; **caution:** gamified trading cues (it removed its confetti animation in 2021 amid regulatory scrutiny of "gamification") — study as the ethical line between delight and nudging risky behaviour. `quality:delight domain:fintech`

70. **Things 3** · see #13 · **Steal:** one-time purchase as a positioning statement; no subscription pressure. `surface:pricing`
71. **Apple (iCloud+, Apple One)** · apple.com/apple-one · **Steal:** bundle comparison with plain monthly prices and family sharing clarity. `surface:pricing`

Paywall ethics checklist (apply to any reference): price + period + renewal visible near CTA; trial end date stated; no pre-selected most-expensive-per-month plan disguised as "best"; easy close (X visible, ≥ 44×44 pt); restore purchases; cancel path one tap from settings. Cross-check with platform guidelines (App Store Review Guidelines 3.1.2 on auto-renewing subscriptions).
